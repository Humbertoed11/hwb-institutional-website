“Give it to me. I can clean this up in less time than it takes you to smoke a cigarette.”

That’s what I told a business owner late the other night.

Here’s what happened:

I stopped by one of our commercial accounts around 10 PM to check on my cleaning crew.
The lights were still on in the front office.
The owner was slumped over his desk, staring at an Excel spreadsheet.

He looked exhausted.

He looked up and said:
“I have to invoice tomorrow morning, and my data is a complete mess.”

He had been wrestling with rows and columns for hours.
He had access to AI tools, but that wasn’t solving the issue.

Because having AI isn’t the hard part.

The hard part is knowing how to:
↳ Take a messy business problem
↳ Understand the operations behind it
↳ Turn that insight into a clean system that actually fixes it

I looked at his spreadsheet and told him to give me 10 minutes.
Before he even finished his break, the numbers were reconciled, cleaned, and ready to bill.

As I drove home, it hit me:

• No customer data ➔ No invoice
• No invoice ➔ No payment
• No payment ➔ No revenue

The data sitting inside your business isn’t just numbers.
It is the lifeblood of your operation.

Think about what actually runs a company day-to-day:
— CRM customer records
— Contracts & work orders
— Time tracking & payroll
— Invoices & purchase orders

When that data gets messy, the entire engine stalls.

I’m in the commercial cleaning business.
Every single day, my job is walking into a messy physical facility, diagnosing what needs attention, restoring order, and making the environment productive again.

Data is no different.

I never expected a late-night check-in on a cleaning account to get me thinking about operational data cleaning as a service.

Yet, here we are.
