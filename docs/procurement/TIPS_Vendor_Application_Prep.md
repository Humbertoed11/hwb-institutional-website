### 2.0 Scope of Work & Capabilities Statement
HWB Cleaning Services LLC provides hospital-grade, clinical-clean commercial custodial and building maintenance operations tailored to Texas educational facilities:
1. **Daily Operational Janitorial & Day Porter Services:** Continuous sanitization of classrooms, circulation corridors, administrative suites, cafeterias, and multi-stall restroom complexes.
2. **Specialized Periodic Maintenance:** High-speed burnishing, scrubbing, stripping, and waxing of vinyl composite tile (VCT); hot-water extraction carpet care; gym floor athletic hardwood maintenance.
3. **Turnkey Post-Construction Clean-Up:** Three-phase rough, final, and touch-up cleans for new campus buildouts, gymnasium additions, and cafeteria renovations.
4. **Bio-Hazard & Infectious Disease Remediation:** EPA List N hospital-grade disinfection compliant with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 (Bloodborne Pathogens).
