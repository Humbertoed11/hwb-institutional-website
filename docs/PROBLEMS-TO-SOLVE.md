## BUG-185: Unchecked `Send to Campaign` & `Remove from Campaign` UI and API Access on View-Only / Sales Accounts
**Detected:** 11/13/2026 (Reported by CEO Humberto Dominguez during live training with Sales representative Mylin Kennedy `mkennedy`)
**Status:** **RESOLVED** (`11/13/2026` — Certified Locally by `test_57_campaigns_module_permissions_and_add_user_preset_bug_185` in `scripts/test_web_performance_rack_14.py`)
**Symptoms:**
1. **Leads Toolbar & Lead Detail Modal Rendered Campaign & Bulk Mutation Controls Without Permission Checks (`HIGH`):**
   - `Send to Campaign`, `Remove from Campaign`, `In Email Outreach...`, `Start Calling Session`, `Status`, `Who Cleans`, and `Mark as DNC` in `templates/backoffice_operations.html` (and the inline `Remove from Campaign` button in `static/js/backoffice_operations_core.js`) rendered unconditionally for any user with `leads.view`, allowing View-Only trainees and Sales personnel without campaign authorization to open the campaign enrollment modal.

   - All 16 `/api/v1/marketing/...` routes in `blueprints/crm_api.py` checked `@login_required` without `current_user.has_permission(...)` or `Sales` `owner_id` filtering on `preflight-check` / `enroll-leads`.

3. **`#modal-add-user` Omitted Module Access Matrix & Role Preset Initialization (`MEDIUM`):**
   - Creating a new user in `/admin/executive` (`HWB-WEB Sigma Executive.html` / `blueprints/operations.py`) did not display the Module Access Rights & Permissions table or fall back to `ROLE_MODULE_PRESETS[role]` when checkbox inputs were absent.

1. **Dedicated `campaigns` (`Email Campaigns (Send & Remove)`) System Module (`core/models/user.py`, `blueprints/operations.py`, `templates/HWB-WEB Sigma Executive.html`):**

   - Added the full **Module Access Rights & Permissions** table (including `Email Campaigns (Send & Remove)`) to `#modal-add-user` (`openAddUserModal()`, `updateAddModalPermsFromRole()`, `resetAddPermsToRoleDefaults()`) and `#modal-edit-user`, plus backend `ROLE_MODULE_PRESETS` fallback in `add_user` and `invalidate_operations_caches()` after user permission updates.
2. **UI Gating & CEO Outbox Separation (`templates/backoffice_operations.html`, `static/js/backoffice_operations_core.js`):**
   - Gated `Send to Campaign`, `In Email Outreach...`, and `#modal-enroll-leads-campaign` on `can_send_campaigns` (`campaigns.edit`); gated bulk and modal `Remove from Campaign` buttons on `can_remove_campaigns` (`campaigns.delete`); gated bulk calling/status/DNC buttons on `can_edit_leads` (`leads.edit`); and gated `#btn-enroll-hub-release-now` inside the enrollment confirmation banner on `can_approve_outbox` (`outbox.edit`).
3. **Backend API Enforcement & `Sales` `owner_id` Isolation (`blueprints/crm_api.py`):**
   - Enforced `campaigns` and `outbox` permissions across all 16 `/api/v1/marketing/...` routes (`log_and_abort_abac`), unshadowed `elif action == 'remove_from_campaign':` in `api_batch_lead_action()`, and enforced strict `owner_id = current_user.id` filtering for `Sales` users across `preflight-check`, `enroll-leads`, and `remove_from_campaign` (both `lead_ids` and `recipient_ids`).

   - Enforced `HTTP 409 CALENDAR_SLOT_CONFLICT` double-booking & 1-hour travel buffer checks, in-place Outlook `PATCH` rescheduling, and `POST` creation across **all** booking endpoints (`POST /api/v1/leads/<id>/cadence-save`, `PUT /api/v1/leads/<id>` Edit Lead Profile, and `PUT /api/v1/accounts/<id>` Client Account Schedule Next Step), plus `POST /api/v1/leads/<id>/walkthrough-cancel` and automatic Outlook `DELETE` cleanup whenever a lead or account transitions to `Do Not Call (DNC)`, `Not Interested`, `Unsubscribed`, or `Canceled`.
3. **Unified `980px` Walkthrough Calendar Modal & `Choice 1A` Pending/Saved State Sync (`templates/backoffice_operations.html`, `static/js/backoffice_operations_core.js`, `templates/sales_desk.html`):**
