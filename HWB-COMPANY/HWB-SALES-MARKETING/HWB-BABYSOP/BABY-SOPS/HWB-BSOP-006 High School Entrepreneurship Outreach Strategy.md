# HWB-BABYSOP: Junior Founder Outreach Strategy

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Provided by HWB-BABYSOP: The Standard for Startup Infrastructure.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the marketing and educational roadmap for engaging high school entrepreneurs. The objective is to seed the SigmaFidelity™ methodology in student projects, creating a long-term pipeline of system-driven founders.

## 2.0 Core Offerings for Students
1.  **The Future Project Registry:** A database where students register their business ideas and receive a formal **Project Birth Certificate.**
2.  **Side-Hustle SOP Kits:** Simplified, high-fidelity templates for lawn care, car detailing, babysitting, and digital freelancing.
3.  **Sigma-Junior Certification:** A digital credentials program verifying the student's understanding of "Information Fidelity" and "Process Control."

---

## 3.0 Strategic Channels
*   **Club Partnerships:** Collaborate with DECA, FBLA, and Junior Achievement chapters in the DFW metroplex (Plano ISD, Dallas ISD).
*   **Social Proof (TikTok/Reels):** George will produce "Founder Logic" shorts—showing how a simple SOP can double a student's side-hustle profit.
*   **Physical Assets:** Distribution of "Fidelity Notebooks" and "Founder ID" cards at school career fairs.

---

## 4.0 Expert SEO Target: "Student Entrepreneurship"
George will optimize the `/babysop/junior` sub-directory for the following keywords:
*   "How to start a business as a teenager."
*   "Business plan for school projects."
*   "Teen entrepreneur resources Texas."
*   "High school business SOPs."
