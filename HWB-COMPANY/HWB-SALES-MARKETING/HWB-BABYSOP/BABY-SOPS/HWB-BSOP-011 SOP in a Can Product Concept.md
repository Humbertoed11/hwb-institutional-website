# Product Concept: Sigma-Spray™ (SOP in a Can)

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Clear the Chaos | Standardize the Gemba | SigmaFidelity™ Tactical Gear.*

## 1.0 Product Vision
The **Sigma-Spray™** is a metaphorical and physical "SOP in a Can." Designed as a premium canister, it serves as the ultimate "Chaos Buster" for new founders. It bridges the gap between the physical janitorial heritage of HWB and the digital infrastructure of BABYSOP.

## 2.0 Product Contents (The "Skeletal Kit")
The canister is not just a spray; it is a high-fidelity delivery system containing:
1.  **The Master Key:** A rugged, metallic USB-C drive pre-loaded with the entire **HWB-BABYSOP Library.**
2.  **The Blueprint:** A waterproof, "Gemba-ready" printed copy of the **Business Birth Certificate (HWB-BSOP-001).**
3.  **Fidelity Fuel:** A sample of high-caffeine "System-Driven" coffee beans to power the founder through their first 5S cycle.
