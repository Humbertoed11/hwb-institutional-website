# HWB-BABYSOP: Strategic Vendor Selection Map

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Provided by HWB-BABYSOP: The Standard for Startup Infrastructure.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the essential third-party service providers required to build a scalable small business. This map serves as a referral guide for HWB-BABYSOP users and a partnership roadmap for the HWB Sales Team.

## 2.0 The "First Five" Essential Partners
Every "Baby Business" should establish contracts with the following partners within the first 90 days:

1.  **The Legal Guardian:** (Business Lawyer) - Protects intellectual property and handles entity formation.
2.  **The Risk Shield:** (Insurance Agent) - Mitigates the "Zero-Cost Illusion" through GL and Workers' Comp.
3.  **The Data Auditor:** (CPA/Accountant) - Ensures financial fidelity and tax optimization.
4.  **The Digital Architect:** (IT/Web Service) - Builds and secures the digital boundary.
5.  **The Gemba Steward:** (HWB Cleaning Services) - Maintains the physical facility to ISO 9001 standards.

---

## 3.0 ISO 9001 Compliance (Clause 8.4)
In alignment with the SigmaFidelity™ standard, all external vendors must be evaluated based on:
*   **Traceability:** Can they provide evidence of their work?
*   **Responsiveness:** Do they meet service-level agreements (SLAs)?
*   **Fidelity:** Does their output match the requested standard?
