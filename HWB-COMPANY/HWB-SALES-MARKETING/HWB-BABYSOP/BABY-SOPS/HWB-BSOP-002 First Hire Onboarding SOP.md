# SOP: First Hire Onboarding (The System-Driven Start)

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Provided by HWB-BABYSOP: The Standard for Startup Infrastructure.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the standardized procedure for onboarding the first employee of a startup or small business. This SOP eliminates the "Onboarding Deficit" by ensuring all legal, cultural, and operational requirements are met before the hire performs their first minute of labor.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to the first five (5) hires of any business utilizing the HWB-BABYSOP infrastructure kit.

## 3.0 Prerequisites
*   **HWB-BSOP-001 (Birth Certificate)** must be completed and an EIN obtained.
*   **Workers' Compensation Insurance:** Policy must be active in the state of operation.
*   **Job Fidelity Description:** A clear list of "Value-Add" tasks for the role.

---

## 4.0 Procedure

### 4.1 Legal & Compliance Readiness (The "Paper Trail")
1.  **Form I-9:** Verify employment eligibility. Keep a digital and physical copy.
2.  **Form W-4:** Ensure correct federal income tax withholding.
3.  **State-Specific Forms:** File "New Hire Reporting" with the state agency within 20 days.
4.  **Employment Agreement:** Signed contract detailing compensation, roles, and the **SigmaFidelity™ Zero Synthetic Data Policy.**

### 4.2 Cultural Integration (The "Soul Transfer")
1.  **Founder Vision Walk:** A 30-minute 1-on-1 session where the Founder explains the **Business Birth Certificate** and the "Why" behind the brand.
2.  **The Standard Orientation:** Explain the importance of documentation. "In this company, if it isn't documented, it didn't happen."

### 4.3 Operational "Gemba" Orientation
1.  **Tool/Software Access:** Provision all logins (email, CRM, project management) 24 hours *before* the start date.
2.  **The "First Win" Task:** Assign one simple, highly-documented task that can be completed and verified within the first 4 hours of employment.
3.  **Audit Loop:** The Founder must audit the new hire's first output against the relevant SOP to confirm "Information Fidelity."

---

## 5.0 Verification
*   Successful filing of New Hire Report with the State.
*   Completed "Employee File" (I-9, W-4, Signed Contract).
*   Active and verified logins for all required systems.

## 6.0 Notes and Cautions
*   **Caution:** Do not allow a new hire to start without a signed Workers' Comp policy. This is a critical "Zero-Cost Illusion" risk.
*   **Fidelity Tip:** Use the first hire to *audit* your existing SOPs. If they can't follow the instructions, the instruction is the defect, not the person.
