## 3.0 Documentation Standards (Built-for-Audit)
Every "Baby SOP" and project asset must adhere to the following:
1.  **Version Control:** Explicit versioning and revision history.
2.  **Document Control Block:** Clear identification of Author, Approver, and ID.
3.  **Visual Logic:** Integration of Mermaid.js flowcharts for process clarity.
4.  **Empirical Basis:** Zero tolerance for synthetic data. All reports must cite a primary source.

## 4.0 RFP & Certification Readiness
HWB-BABYSOP is designed to generate the "Evidence Bundle" required for high-level contracts:
*   **Capability Statement:** Formally documented via HWB-MKT-001.
*   **Quality Manual:** The cumulative library of HWB-BSOP assets.
*   **Financial Integrity:** Documented through the S4/IEE reporting layer.
