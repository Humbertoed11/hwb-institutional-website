# HWB-Merch: Selection and Quality Standards

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Provided by HWB-BABYSOP: The Standard for Startup Infrastructure.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the selection criteria and quality control standards for HWB-branded merchandise. The goal is to provide "Baby Businesses" with professional apparel and tools that project institutional authority and a "System-Driven" mindset.

## 2.0 Strategic Objective: "The Uniform of Excellence"
Merchandise is not merely promotional; it is a visual signal of organizational fidelity. Every item must:
1.  **Reduce Visual Noise:** Minimalist, high-end aesthetics.
2.  **Signal Professionalism:** High-quality fabrics and materials.
3.  **Reinforce the Brand:** Consistent use of the HWB New Logo and SigmaFidelity™ terminology.

---

## 3.0 Core Product Categories

### 3.1 Foundational Apparel (The "System-Driven" Look)
*   **Item:** Premium Cotton T-Shirts & Polos.
*   **Design:** HWB New Logo (Chest) | "SigmaFidelity™ Standard" (Sleeve).
*   **Color Palette:** Institutional Slate, Corporate Blue, and Optical White.

### 3.2 Founder Tools (The "Fidelity Kit")
*   **Item:** Field Notebooks & Precision Pens.
*   **Design:** Embossed Sigma Logo.
*   **Utility:** Designed for "Gemba" walks and manual SOP drafting.

---

## 4.0 Quality Standards & Inspection (Poka-Yoke)
| **Feature** | **Quality Standard** | **Verification Method** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logo Fidelity** | Must match HWB-WEB HWB New Logo exactly. | Visual comparison against master .png. |
| **Material Integrity** | Minimum 180 GSM for apparel. | Feel and durability test. |
| **Print Durability** | No cracking after 10 wash cycles. | Batch testing protocol. |

---

## 5.0 Vendor Selection Criteria
George will only source merchandise from vendors who meet the following:
*   **Reliability:** 99% on-time delivery rate.
*   **Transparency:** Open disclosure of manufacturing origins.
*   **Fidelity:** Ability to reproduce high-resolution SVG/PNG assets without loss of detail.
