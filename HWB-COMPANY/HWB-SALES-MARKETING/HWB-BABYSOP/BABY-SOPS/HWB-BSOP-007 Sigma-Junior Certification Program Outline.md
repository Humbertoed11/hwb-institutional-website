# Sigma-Junior™: The System-Driven Founder Certification

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*A Digital Product of HWB-BABYSOP: The Standard for Startup Infrastructure.*

## 1.0 Program Overview
The **Sigma-Junior™ Certification** is a 4-module masterclass designed to install high-fidelity organizational logic into student-led projects. It transforms "teen hustles" into "scalable systems" using the same ISO 9001 and Lean Six Sigma principles used by HWB Cleaning Services LLC.

---

## 2.0 Curriculum Structure

### Module 1: The Identity Kernel (Project Birth)
*   **Lesson 1.1:** Why your business needs a "Birth Certificate" (HWB-BSOP-001).
*   **Lesson 1.2:** Establishing your Legal & Philosophical Boundary.
*   **Outcome:** Completed Business Birth Certificate and unique **Project ID**.

### Module 2: The Fidelity Shield (Data Integrity)
*   **Lesson 2.1:** The "Zero Synthetic Data" Mandate: Why make-belief data kills growth.
*   **Lesson 2.2:** Evidence-Based Management: Tracking your first real-world metrics.
*   **Outcome:** Setup of a "Fidelity Log" for customer interactions and sales.

### Module 3: The Gemba Standard (Process Mapping)
*   **Lesson 3.1:** Identifying "The Real Place": Where your value is actually created.
*   **Lesson 3.2:** Writing your first SOP: The "Poka-Yoke" method for zero defects.
*   **Outcome:** A functional, audited SOP for the student's primary service (e.g., Lawn Mowing, Design).

### Module 4: The 8th Waste (The Value of Time)
*   **Lesson 4.1:** The "Zero-Cost Illusion": Why your own labor isn't "free."
*   **Lesson 4.2:** Mopping with Payroll: How to identify and eliminate non-value-add tasks.
*   **Outcome:** A 12-month "Growth Roadmap" based on the S4/IEE framework.
