# HWB-BABYSOP: Silver Fox Mentorship Program

*Bridging the Wisdom Gap | SigmaFidelity™ Legacy Integration.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To create a verified network of "Silver Foxes"—retired or active executives, teachers, and business owners—who provide strategic oversight and mentorship to "Baby Business" founders. This program captures "Institutional Wisdom" before it is lost to the 8th Waste (Unused Talent).

## 2.0 Registration Fidelity
Prospective mentors must register via the BABYSOP portal and provide:
1.  **Professional Pedigree:** Verification of 20+ years in a leadership or educational role.
2.  **Area of Fidelity:** Specific expertise (e.g., Accounting, Legal, Operations, Sales).
3.  **The "Silver" Commitment:** A pledge to uphold the Zero Synthetic Data Policy in all advisory sessions.
