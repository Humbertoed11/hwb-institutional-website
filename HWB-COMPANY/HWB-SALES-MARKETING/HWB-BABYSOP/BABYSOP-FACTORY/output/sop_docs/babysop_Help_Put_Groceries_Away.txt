# Santo Bazooka's Help Put Groceries Away Mission

### 🌟 MY BIG GOAL
"Helping our family unpack the groceries keeps our food fresh, our kitchen happy, and makes us all part of the winning home team!"

### 📝 MY STEPS TO SUCCESS
1. 🛒 **The Bag Scout**: Carefully look at the bags on the floor or counter to see what's inside!
2. 🥦 **The Cold Sorter**: Find all the "brrr" items like milk, yogurt, and cheese and put them in one spot.
3. 🧊 **Fridge Hand-off**: Gently hand the cold things to the Chief Auditor to put in the refrigerator.
4. 🍎 **Pantry Parade**: Carry the apples, crackers, and snacks to their special homes in the pantry.
5. ♻️ **Recycle Round-up**: Fold up the empty bags and put them in the recycling bin so we can use them again!
