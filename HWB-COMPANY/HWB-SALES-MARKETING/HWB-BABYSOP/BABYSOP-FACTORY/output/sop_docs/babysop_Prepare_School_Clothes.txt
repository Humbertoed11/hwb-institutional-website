# "Santo Bazooka's Prepare School Clothes Mission"

### 🌟 THE BIG GOAL
The mission is for Santo Bazooka to become a Morning Hero. By preparing school clothes the night before, Santo Bazooka ensures a peaceful morning with plenty of time for breakfast and extra sleep.
