## 2.0 Strategic Purpose: The "Zero-Growth Illusion"
This project targets the primary cause of small business failure: **Structural Chaos**. By providing "Baby SOPs," we eliminate the waste of "trial-and-error" administration, allowing entrepreneurs to focus on value-add activities from Day 1.

### 2.1 Expert SEO Targets (Local & Global)
George has identified the following high-authority keywords for the domain launch:
*   **Primary:** "Baby Business SOPs," "Small Business Policy Templates," "Startup Infrastructure Kit."
*   **Secondary:** "How to start a cleaning business," "Scalable Business Systems," "Founder Merchandise."
