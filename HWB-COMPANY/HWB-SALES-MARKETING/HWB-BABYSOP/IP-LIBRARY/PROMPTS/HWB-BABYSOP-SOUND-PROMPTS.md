## 2.0 Soundscape Prompts

### 2.1 The "Daycare Daily Cycle (7AM-8PM)" (Optimized for MusicDonna.com)
**Mandate:** Use the "Instrumental" or "No Lyrics" toggle during generation.

**Prompt:**
> "Immersive 360-degree ASMR soundscape: 'The Daycare Daily Cycle (7AM-8PM)'. Style: Realistic field recording, atmospheric, high-fidelity textures. Mood: Warm, safe, bustling, and professional. 
> 
> Layer 1: Morning Dawn (7AM). Soft ambient room tone, distant door opening and closing, echoey footsteps on polished floors, gentle arrival of first children with quiet greetings. 
> 
> Layer 2: Peak Activity (10AM-3PM). Vibrant wall-of-sound featuring happy children's laughter, playful shouting, and soft chatter. Prominent 'babies cooing' and gentle infant gurgles in the foreground. Texture of wooden building blocks clinking and plastic toys rattling. 
> 
> Layer 3: Quiet Transitions. 15-second 'Nap Time' interlude with rhythmic deep breathing, distant white noise, and a very faint, instrumental-only lullaby (piano/glockenspiel). 
> 
> Layer 4: Evening Wind-down (5PM-8PM). Gradually diminishing noise, fewer voices, the sound of a distant vacuum, soft clicking of light switches, and a calm, silent finale. No electronic beats, no synthesized voices, pure organic daycare ambience."

---

## 3.0 Deployment Standards
1.  **Bitrate:** Minimum 320kbps for all generated audio.
2.  **Normalization:** Peak amplitude must not exceed -3dB to ensure clarity when layered with voiceovers.
3.  **Third Person:** All audio metadata and documentation must adhere to the 3rd person perspective.
