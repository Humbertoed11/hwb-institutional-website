# Standard Operating Procedure: **Corporate Vendor Outreach SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP defines the standard procedure for account executives to identify, approach, and register with large corporations as a preferred vendor for commercial cleaning services.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all sales personnel responsible for high-ticket B2B lead generation and procurement registration.

## 3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Guidance First:** If a vendor portal's requirements are non-standard, ASK the CEO before submitting sensitive documents.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Log all portal registration attempts in the `SigmaInteractionLog`.
3. **Physical Truth:** Reference absolute paths for corporate W-9s and Insurance Certificates.

## 4.0 Procedure

### 4.1 Lead Identification
1.  **Identify Potential Clients:** Research large corporations and property management companies in the DFW metroplex.
2.  **Find Vendor Portals:** Search for "supplier," "procurement," or "contact" pages on target websites.
3.  **National Partners:** Explore partnership opportunities with national facility management providers.

### 4.2 Registration
1.  **Review Requirements:** Ensure HWB meets all licensing and safety criteria.
2.  **Complete Applications:** Accurately fill out registration forms and provide required documentation.
3.  **CRM Update:** Add all new leads and their status to the **Lead Pipeline**.

## 5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
*   Successful registration confirmation received from client.
*   Lead status updated in CRM to "Registered."

## 6.0 Notes and Cautions
> **NOTE:** Maintain a professional and helpful tone during all communications.
> **CAUTION:** Never submit synthetic insurance data. All certificates must be verified.
