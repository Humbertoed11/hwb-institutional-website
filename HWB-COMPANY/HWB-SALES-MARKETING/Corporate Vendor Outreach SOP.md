# Standard Operating Procedure: **Corporate Vendor Outreach SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for account executives to identify, approach, and register with large corporations as a preferred vendor for commercial cleaning services.

## 2.0 Scope

*   This procedure applies to all account executives and sales personnel responsible for lead generation and business development.

## 3.0 Prerequisites

*   Access to the internet and a web browser.
*   Access to the company's CRM and the `home_builders_leads.csv` file.

## 4.0 Procedure

### 4.1 Lead Identification

1.  **Identify Potential Clients:**
    *   Research large corporations, property management companies, and facility management companies.
    *   Utilize online resources such as business directories, professional networking sites, and industry publications.
2.  **Find Vendor Portals:**
    *   Visit the websites of potential clients and look for "vendor," "supplier," "procurement," or "contact" pages.
    *   Search for vendor registration forms or contact information for procurement departments.
3.  **Partner with National Providers:**
    *   Identify and research national facility management companies and commercial cleaning service providers.
    *   Explore partnership opportunities with these companies to gain access to their client base.

### 4.2 Lead Qualification and Registration

1.  **Review Vendor Requirements:**
    *   Carefully review the vendor registration requirements for each potential client.
    *   Ensure that the company meets all necessary criteria, such as insurance, licensing, and safety standards.
2.  **Complete Vendor Applications:**
    *   Accurately and completely fill out all vendor registration forms.
    *   Provide all required documentation, such as W-9 forms, certificates of insurance, and state licenses.
3.  **Update Leads Spreadsheet:**
    *   Add all new leads and their status to the `home_builders_leads.csv` spreadsheet.
    *   Keep the spreadsheet updated with any changes in contact information or registration status.

### 4.3 Outreach and Follow-up

1.  **Initial Contact:**
    *   Once registered, or if direct contact is possible, reach out to the procurement department or relevant contact person.
    *   Introduce the company and its services, highlighting key differentiators and value propositions.
2.  **Follow-up:**
    *   Follow up with potential clients on a regular basis to stay top-of-mind.
    *   Provide additional information as requested and maintain a professional and persistent approach.

## 5.0 Verification

*   The number of new leads added to the `home_builders_leads.csv` spreadsheet.
*   The number of successful vendor registrations.
*   The number of new contracts or bidding opportunities.

## 6.0 Notes and Cautions

*   Always be professional and courteous in all communications with potential clients.
*   Tailor your approach to each client based on their specific needs and requirements.
*   Be persistent but not aggressive in your follow-up efforts.
