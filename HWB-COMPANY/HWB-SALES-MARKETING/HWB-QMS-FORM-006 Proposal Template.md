# Standard Operating Procedure: **Sales Proposal Template**

## 1.0 Purpose
This template provides a standardized, high-fidelity framework for submitting bids and proposals. It is designed to emphasize HWB’s strategic differentiators: **Information Fidelity, Resource Optimization, and ISO 9001 Alignment.**

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all formal business development bids, RFPs, and service proposals generated by the Sales & Marketing department.

## 3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Guidance First:** If an RFP requirement is ambiguous, ASK the CEO before drafting a response.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Every sent proposal must be logged in the `SigmaInteractionLog`.
3. **Physical Truth:** Use absolute server paths for the "Proof of Performance" data attached to proposals.

## 4.0 Procedure
1.  Open a new document using the **HWB-QMS-FORM-007 Official Letterhead**.
2.  Populate the bracketed information `[ ]` with client-specific data.
3.  Ensure the "Six Critical Areas" are tailored to the specific solicitation (RFP).
4.  Submit as a high-quality PDF via the internal Transmission Hub.
