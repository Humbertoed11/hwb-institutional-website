# Sales Proposal Template

## 1.0 Purpose
This template provides a standardized, high-fidelity framework for submitting bids and proposals. It is designed to emphasize HWB’s strategic differentiators: **Information Fidelity, Resource Optimization, and ISO 9001 Alignment.**

## 2.0 Procedure
1.  Open a new document using the **HWB-QMS-FORM-007 Official Letterhead**.
2.  Populate the bracketed information `[ ]` with client-specific data.
3.  Ensure the "Six Critical Areas" are tailored to the specific solicitation (RFP).
4.  Submit as a high-quality PDF.
