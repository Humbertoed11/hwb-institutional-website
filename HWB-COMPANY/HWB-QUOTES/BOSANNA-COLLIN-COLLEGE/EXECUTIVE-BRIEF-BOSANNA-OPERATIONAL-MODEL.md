### 1.0 Executive Summary & Objective

The purpose of this Executive Brief is to establish the calibrated delivery model for **Bosanna LLC** (as Prime Contractor) and **HWB Cleaning Services LLC** (as Turnkey Facility Operations Partner) in servicing the Collin College Frisco Campus under the District's **$1,500,000.00 annual spending cap ($125,000.00/month)**.

To comply with Collin College's strict contract conditions while protecting both companies from operational and financial exposure, this brief establishes the **Turnkey Hybrid Model**:
1. **Bosanna LLC** controls the statutory **W-2 labor structure** (via an approved commercial staffing partner), handles **direct wholesale trade billing for restroom paper products and soap**, and remits the 1.0% TIPS Cooperative fee.
2. **HWB Cleaning Services LLC** provides the complete **on-site operational engine**: furnishing an $80,000+ commercial heavy machinery fleet, automated chemical dilution systems, on-site bilingual shift supervision, biometric time-clock tracking, campus police desk reconciliation, and daily APPA Level 2 quality assurance.

Under this calibrated model, Bosanna and HWB split the contract's distributable net operating profit **50/50**, delivering **$186,601.20 per year in pure net profit to Bosanna** and **$186,601.20 per year to HWB**.

### 4.0 The 3 Strategic Levers That Hit Exactly $1.50M

1. **Lever 1: SOW Baseline Staffing (904.0 Hours/Week vs. 1,120.0 Hours/Week):**
   * The original proposal included an expanded 1,120.0-hour schedule (28 FTEs).
   * Re-aligning to the District's official recommended staffing in SOW § 134–136 (12 night custodians × 7 nights = 672 hrs, 1 night supervisor × 7 nights = 56 hrs, 4 M-F day porters = 160 hrs, 2 Saturday day porters = 16 hrs -> Total = 904.0 hrs/wk, 22.6 FTEs) immediately reduces loaded W-2 labor to **$78,124.80/month** ($937,497.60/year).
2. **Lever 2: Unbundling Restorative Floor Care (RFP Line 46 & Line 47):**
   * Tile strip-and-wax (Line 46 at $0.27/SF) and hot-water carpet extraction (Line 47 at $0.30/SF) are unbundled from the flat monthly base and billed under their dedicated RFP line items during semester breaks (Winter, Spring, Summer).
   * Daily floor scrubbing, buffing, and spot maintenance remain in the base.
3. **Lever 3: Calibrated Wage Tiers:**
   * Cleaners & Day Porters: $16.00/hr base wages (~$19.50/hr loaded staffing bill rate).
   * Shift Supervisors: $22.00/hr base wages (~$26.80/hr loaded staffing bill rate).
   * Loaded factor of ~21.8% covers statutory FICA, FUTA, SUTA, and Code 9014 Workers' Compensation.

### 5.0 The 5 Decisive Advantages for Bosanna LLC

1. **Zero Equipment Debt & Free Capital Fleet ($80,000+ Value):** HWB furnishes, delivers, and maintains the entire machinery fleet at zero capital cost to Bosanna, saving Bosanna $60,000.00/year in equipment lease and maintenance costs.
2. **Complete W-2 Statutory Legal Shield:** Commercial staffing partner serves as statutory employer of record for all 22.6 FTEs, eliminating payroll tax audits and workers' comp liabilities.
3. **20-Minute Rapid-Response Penalty Shield:** HWB shift supervisors conduct nightly inspections and correct deficiencies within 20 minutes, stopping District 2-hour invoice clawbacks.
4. **Dual-Audit Verified Proof of Hours:** Digital biometric punches reconciled daily with Collin College Police desk sign-in logs, providing undeniable audit records for monthly payment approval.
5. **Hands-Off Six-Figure Passive Profit:** Bosanna captures **$15,550.10 every month ($186,601.20 per year)** in pure prime margin with zero boots on the ground and zero equipment debt.
