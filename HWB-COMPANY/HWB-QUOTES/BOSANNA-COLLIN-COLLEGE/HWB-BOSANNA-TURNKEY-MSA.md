### RECITALS & OPERATIONAL INTENT

**WHEREAS**, Collin County Community College District (the "District" or "Owner") has solicited comprehensive custodial, janitorial, and restorative facility maintenance services for its **Frisco Campus** (located at 9700 Wade Boulevard, Frisco, Texas 75035), encompassing ten (10) academic, athletic, and administrative facilities totaling approximately **478,418 cleanable square feet**, plus parking garage stairwells, elevators, and campus grounds (the "Facility"); and

**WHEREAS**, Bosanna serves as the awarded Prime Vendor under the TIPS Purchasing Cooperative agreement and possesses the prime commercial contract with the District to deliver custodial services for the Frisco Campus (the "Prime Contract"); and

**WHEREAS**, the Prime Contract specifically contains Section 153 and Section 243 ("No Subcontractors"), which state that all cleaning personnel performing work on District property must be direct statutory W-2 employees of the awarded vendor; and

**WHEREAS**, Bosanna desires to engage HWB as its **Exclusive Turnkey Management Services and Facility Operations Provider** to provide operational management, Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt quality systems, standardized operating procedures (SOPs), heavy industrial equipment leasing, proprietary chemistry proportioning systems, JanSan procurement oversight, biometric attendance reconciliation, and shift supervision; and

**WHEREAS**, Bosanna shall directly employ all custodial cleaning technicians and field shift leads on **Bosanna’s direct W-2 payroll**, thereby fully satisfying District Special Condition Section 153 and Section 243 without violation of public procurement rules; and

**WHEREAS**, HWB possesses the institutional expertise, certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt methodology, ISO 9001:2015 compliant quality management systems, equipment fleet, and administrative infrastructure necessary to ensure 100% compliance with District specifications;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties, and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

### ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

1.1 **"APPA Level 2"** means the Association of Physical Plant Administrators standard of "Ordinary Tidiness," which mandates that all horizontal and vertical surfaces remain clean, that washrooms gleam and are odor-free, and that no dust, dirt, or stains remain unaddressed for more than two (2) working days, as incorporated in District Scope of Work Section 31–32.

1.2 **"Cleanable Square Footage"** means the agreed four hundred seventy-eight thousand four hundred eighteen (478,418) cleanable square feet among the ten (10) specified campus buildings, plus the parking garage elevators and concrete stairwells.

1.3 **"Dedicated W-2 Workforce"** means the staff of approximately twenty-four (24) to twenty-five (25) badged cleaning technicians, day porters, and shift leads recruited and managed by HWB, but hired, employed, and compensated directly on Bosanna’s W-2 payroll.

1.4 **"District Specifications"** means the Collin County Community College District Custodial Terms & Conditions and Frisco Campus Scope of Work, including all appendices, incorporated herein by reference.

1.5 **"HWB Management Fee"** means the monthly turnkey operational management, equipment lease, chemistry, procurement, and EBITDA profit fee invoiced by HWB to Bosanna in the fixed amount of **$28,576.58 per month** ($342,918.91 annually).

1.6 **"Prime Contract Sum"** means the total monthly billing submitted to the District in the amount of **$124,426.19 per month** ($1,493,114.28 annually = $3.12 per square foot).

1.7 **"Prime Margin"** means the pure net margin retained by Bosanna in the fixed amount of **$18,663.93 per month** ($223,967.14 annually), representing fifteen percent (15.0%) of top-line billings after the 1.0% TIPS administrative fee.

1.8 **"Turnkey Management Services"** means all operational, technical, technological, supervisory, and logistical services performed by HWB as detailed in Article II of this Agreement.

### ARTICLE II: ENGAGEMENT & SCOPE OF TURNKEY MANAGEMENT SERVICES

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment:** Bosanna hereby engages HWB, and HWB hereby accepts engagement, as the sole and exclusive provider of Turnkey Management Services for the Frisco Campus during the entire term of this Agreement and all option years. Bosanna shall not self-perform the management services, nor engage any other third party to perform facility management, quality control, or equipment leasing at the Facility.

2.2 **Operational Management & Workforce Scheduling:**
(a) HWB shall engineer, deploy, and administer the mandatory **904.0 weekly man-hour schedule** required by District Scope of Work Section 161–163.  
(b) HWB shall schedule and direct:
- Four (4) Day Porters (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM);
- Two (2) Day Porters (Saturday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM);
- Twelve (12) Night Custodians (Monday–Sunday, 10:00 PM – 6:30 AM); and
- One (1) Full-Time Non-Cleaning Shift Supervisor (Nightly).  
(c) HWB shall maintain an active relief rotation to ensure 100% shift coverage with zero overtime bleed and zero unfulfilled shifts.

2.3 **Quality Assurance & APPA Level 2 Compliance:**
(a) HWB shall enforce its proprietary SigmaFidelity™ Quality Assurance Program, conducting nightly digitized inspections across all ten (10) buildings.  
(b) HWB’s Account Manager shall perform mandatory weekly campus walkthroughs with District Facilities Operations personnel (SOW § 128) and deliver certified inspection reports.  
(c) HWB shall guarantee rapid dispatch protocols ensuring that any cleaning deficiency flagged by the District is acknowledged and corrected within **twenty (20) minutes** to prevent the District’s contractual two-hour clawback penalty (T&C § 159).

2.4 **Capital Equipment Fleet Provision & Maintenance:**
(a) HWB shall furnish, deploy, and maintain in first-class operating condition all commercial equipment specified in District Scope of Work Section 182–205 and Exhibit B of this Agreement, including:
- Commercial riding rotary auto-scrubbers;
- 20-inch walk-behind automatic scrubbers;
- Patented HOST dual-counter-rotating cylindrical brush dry carpet extractors (SOW § 76);
- High-speed 2000 RPM burnishers;
- HEPA commercial backpack vacuums;
- Ground-based carbon-fiber high-reach dusting wands (up to 30 ft);
- Heavy-duty tilt trucks, rolling Brute gondolas, and JanSan mobile carts.  
(b) All machinery shall remain the exclusive property of HWB. HWB shall bear all costs of ongoing preventive maintenance, repairs, pad replacements, and battery maintenance.  
(c) HWB shall ensure all equipment is permanently engraved and tagged with institutional inventory numbers per SOW Section 204.

2.5 **Chemical Systems & Dilution Proportioning:**
(a) HWB shall furnish and install automatic closed-loop chemical dilution proportioning systems in designated janitorial closets across all ten (10) campus buildings (SOW § 142).  
(b) HWB shall provide all primary cleaning chemistry, neutral sanitizers, Taski Profi hard floor restorers, non-acid daily restroom disinfectants, and quarterly acid descalers.  
(c) HWB shall ensure up-to-date Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are mounted in secondary containers and binder racks in every closet.

2.6 **JanSan Consumable Supply Procurement Management:**
HWB shall manage the procurement, warehousing, delivery, and inventory control of all paper towels, two-ply toilet tissue, touchless hand soap cartridges, urinal pods, toilet seat covers, and trash liners (SOW § 138–161), coordinating wholesale manufacturer distribution agreements to keep supply costs within budgeted operating allowances.

2.7 **Biometric Attendance & Security Synchronization:**
(a) HWB shall deploy an on-site cellular biometric fingerprint time-clock terminal in the central custodial staging office (SOW § 202).  
(b) HWB’s Shift Supervisor shall verify each worker's physical sign-in at the Collin College Police desk (Bldg S) against digital biometric punches.  
(c) HWB shall compile and certify the monthly employee hours printout required to accompany each billing invoice.

### ARTICLE III: WORKFORCE STRUCTURE & W-2 EMPLOYMENT PROTOCOL (THE LEGAL SOLUTION)

3.1 **Direct Employment by Bosanna:**
(a) To ensure 100% legal compliance with District Terms & Conditions Section 153 and Section 243, **all cleaning technicians, day porters, and shift supervisors performing physical labor on District property shall be direct W-2 employees of Bosanna**.  
(b) Bosanna shall execute standard employment agreements, W-4 tax forms, and I-9 employment eligibility verifications for each worker.  
(c) Under no circumstances shall physical cleaning workers be engaged as 1099 independent contractors or as employees of an undisclosed subcontractor.

3.2 **Payroll Administration, Taxes & Insurance:**
(a) Bosanna shall bear sole legal and financial responsibility for the timely disbursement of employee wages, overtime (if authorized), and statutory payroll withholdings (FICA, Medicare, Federal Income Tax).  
(b) Bosanna shall fund and maintain statutory Texas Workers' Compensation Insurance covering all on-site employees, with statutory limits and an Employer’s Liability endorsement of not less than $1,000,000.  
(c) Bosanna shall remit all state unemployment insurance (SUTA) and federal unemployment tax (FUTA) obligations.

3.3 **HWB Recruitment, Onboarding & Supervisory Authority:**
(a) HWB shall act as Bosanna’s authorized operational agent for recruiting, screening, skill-testing, and presenting qualified custodial candidates to Bosanna for hiring.  
(b) HWB shall conduct all required training in chemical safety, bloodborne pathogens, APPA Level 2 standards, equipment operation, and District customer service protocols.  
(c) HWB’s Shift Supervisor shall exercise full day-to-day operational direction, work assignment, quality inspection, and disciplinary recommendation authority over the W-2 workforce on site.

3.4 **Criminal Background Checks & FAST Fingerprinting:**
(a) Prior to assigning any employee to the Facility, Bosanna and HWB shall ensure that each individual has undergone and cleared a National Criminal Background Check and FAST Fingerprinting in strict compliance with Texas Education Code § 22.0834 and District T&C Section 80–88.  
(b) No person with pending charges or felony convictions involving crimes against children, sex offenses, weapons, controlled substances, or violence shall be placed on campus without the advance written approval of the District Chief Financial Officer or District Chief of Police.

3.5 **Zero-Overtime Structural Architecture & Fatigue Mitigation:**
(a) HWB shall engineer and manage the master shift schedule to strictly cap all individual worker shifts at forty (40) straight-time hours per week, **permanently eliminating unbudgeted 1.5× overtime expenses**.  
(b) By maintaining an expanded badged workforce roster of twenty-nine (29) to thirty-two (32) W-2 employees across the 140 weekly shifts (1,120 total weekly hours), HWB ensures that absenteeism, sick leave, and vacation relief are absorbed by straight-time relief technicians rather than costly overtime extensions.  
(c) This zero-overtime protocol prevents worker burnout, fatigue-induced quality decay, and safety incidents, safeguarding Bosanna's net profit margin and guaranteeing peak APPA Level 2 performance on every shift.

### ARTICLE V: PENALTIES, CLAWDOWNS & RISK ALLOCATION

5.1 **The District 2-Hour Penalty Clause (T&C § 159):**
(a) Under District T&C Section 159, failure to correct an unsatisfactory condition within two (2) hours of notification results in a deduction equal to **two times (2×) the average square foot cost multiplied by the square footage of the flagged area**.  
(b) **Allocation of Penalty:**
- If a deduction is assessed due to HWB’s supervisory negligence, delayed dispatch, or operational failure, HWB shall absorb the deduction, and Bosanna may offset the empirical penalty amount against HWB’s next monthly Management Fee.
- If a deduction is assessed due to Bosanna’s failure to fund payroll, maintain the required headcount, or process W-2 worker onboarding, Bosanna shall absorb the penalty in full with zero reduction in HWB’s Management Fee.

5.2 **Master Key Control & Re-Keying Liability (T&C §§ 149–151):**
(a) Master key rings issued by the District shall be checked out each evening and returned each morning in strict accordance with HWB’s Poka-Yoke Steel Tether Protocol.  
(b) If a key is lost, the $100.00 District fee shall be paid by the Party employing the individual who lost the key. If campus re-keying is mandated due to negligence, liability shall follow the employing or supervising Party whose gross negligence caused the loss, subject to the limitation of liability provisions herein.

5.3 **Default & Re-Procurement Protection (T&C § 41):**
Both Parties acknowledge that under District T&C Section 41, contract default authorizes the District to re-bid and charge the cost difference to the vendor. HWB warrants that its Lean Six Sigma operational systems and built-in relief roster eliminate dropped shifts, safeguarding both Parties against default proceedings.

### ARTICLE VI: TERM, RENEWAL & TERMINATION

6.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date (October 1, 2026) and shall continue for an initial term of **one (1) full year**, expiring on September 30, 2027.

6.2 **Option Years:** If the District exercises its annual renewal options under the Prime Contract (up to four (4) additional one-year terms), this Agreement shall automatically renew co-terminus with the Prime Contract, maintaining all terms, pricing structures, and exclusivity protections intact.

6.3 **Funding-Out Clause (T&C § 71):** The Parties acknowledge that under Texas statutes, District expenditures are subject to annual Board of Trustees budget appropriations ending August 31. In the event the Board fails to appropriate funds for custodial services, Bosanna may terminate this Agreement without penalty, upon immediate written transmission of the District’s official non-appropriation notice to HWB.

6.4 **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other Party materially breaches any provision hereof and fails to cure such breach within the thirty (30) day notice period. Non-payment of HWB’s Management Fee within ten (10) business days of due date constitutes immediate material breach.

6.5 **Equipment Repossession Upon Termination:** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement for any reason, HWB shall have immediate, unrestricted access to the Facility to de-install, remove, and repossess all HWB-owned machinery, chemical dispensers, auto-scrubbers, vacuums, and tools. Bosanna shall not assert any lien, claim, or encumbrance over HWB equipment.

### ARTICLE VII: EXCLUSIVITY, NON-CIRCUMVENTION & COVENANTS

7.1 **Absolute Exclusivity:** During the term of this Agreement and any extensions or renewals thereof, Bosanna grants HWB the **exclusive right** to manage custodial operations at the Frisco Campus. Bosanna shall not solicit bids from, negotiate with, or contract with any other facility management company for the Frisco Campus.

7.2 **Non-Circumvention:** Bosanna covenants that it will not enter into any direct janitorial or custodial agreement with Collin County Community College District for the Frisco Campus that bypasses, excludes, or reduces the participation of HWB.

7.3 **Non-Solicitation of Management Personnel:** Neither Party shall, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of two (2) years following termination, directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any executive, operational manager, or supervisory employee of the other Party without express written consent.
