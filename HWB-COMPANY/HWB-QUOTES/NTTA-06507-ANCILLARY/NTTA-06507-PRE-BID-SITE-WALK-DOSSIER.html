4.0 On-Site Forensic Inspection Checklist

Use this physical inspection checklist during each walkthrough stop to capture field intelligence:

  • Janitor Closets & Utility Sinks: Check presence of working mop sinks, hot water supply, hose thread connectors, chemical shelving, and proper ventilation in each closet.
  • Consumable Par Stock Staging: Verify current stock levels of NTTA-supplied toilet paper, roll towels, foam soap refills, and trash liners. Note where NTTA receives and stores deliveries.
  • Recycling Dumpster Audit ($200 Fine Shield): Inspect outdoor dumpster corrals. Are the recycling dumpsters distinctly color-coded? Do they have padlock hasps? Note if open to outside road crews.
  • Floor Substrate Condition: Examine rubber athletic flooring in locker/fitness areas for chemical film; inspect VCT wax buildup or stripping needs; check high-traffic commercial carpet pathways.
  • Restroom Fixtures & Partitions: Count stalls, urinals, sinks, and showers in Plano and Frisco. Note any hard water scaling, stainless partition rust, or broken paper dispensers.
  • Cellular Signal / Wi-Fi Coverage: Check smartphone cellular reception inside interior janitor closets and lower-level utility corridors. Reliable data signal is essential for daily electronic photo upload check-ins.
  • Incumbent Equipment Recon: Identify equipment left on-site by incumbent contractor (auto-scrubbers, vacuums, burnishers). Note brand names, physical condition, and chemical brands used.
