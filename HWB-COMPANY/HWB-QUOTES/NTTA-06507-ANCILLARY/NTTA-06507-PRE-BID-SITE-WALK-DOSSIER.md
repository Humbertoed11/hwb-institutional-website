## 5.0 SigmaFidelity™ Operational Safeguards (Poka-Yoke)

### 1. The Zero-Bag Recycling SOP ($200 Fine Shield)
- Under no circumstances will a plastic trash bag ever be thrown into an NTTA recycling dumpster.
- Blue clear liners are used inside building recycling cans for transport only. At the dumpster corral, technicians invert the bag, dump the loose recyclables into the recycling dumpster, and place the plastic liner into the landfill dumpster.

### 2. Automated Daily Photo Logs (Invoice Dispute Shield)
- To eliminate any risk of NTTA invoice deductions (`Monthly Amount ÷ Scheduled Service Days`) or "ghost staffing" allegations, cleaning technicians take before-and-after timestamped photos of restrooms and common areas during every shift.
- Photos are uploaded directly to the HWB operations portal / Telegram bot for real-time audit logging.
