### SUBJECT: Public Information Act Request – Municipal Janitorial Master Agreements, Pricing Exhibits & Subcontractor Utilization Records

Dear Public Information Coordinator:

Pursuant to the Texas Public Information Act, Texas Government Code Chapter 552, HWB Cleaning Services LLC respectfully requests copies of public records regarding recently awarded and executed municipal janitorial service contracts with the City of Dallas.

Under Texas Government Code § 552.022(a)(3), information in an account, voucher, or contract relating to the receipt or expenditure of public or other funds by a governmental body is explicitly designated as public information not excepted from required disclosure.

Specifically, we request digital copies (via PDF download or electronic transmission) of the following public records:

#### 1. Citywide Janitorial Service Price Agreement (Council File # 26-1944A)
*   **Solicitation Identifier:** `CSP-BYZ25-00028708`
*   **Awardee:** Ambassador Services LLC
*   **Council Approval Date:** June 24, 2026 (Item 70, Estimated Amount $45,780,678.43)
*   **Requested Items:**
    1.  The fully executed Service Price Agreement / Contract between the City of Dallas and Ambassador Services LLC.
    2.  **Exhibit B (Pricing Schedule):** The complete itemized line-item pricing sheet, including individual facility monthly billing amounts, cleanable square footages, frequency matrices, and hourly porter billing rates.
    3.  **Form BID-FRM-625 (Subcontractor Utilization Intent Form):** The complete approved M/WBE participation plan detailing designated minority- and women-owned subcontractors, scopes of work, and committed dollar/percentage values fulfilling the 23.8% Business Enterprise Hub mandate.
    4.  **Final Scoring Tabulation Matrix:** The final composite score sheet for all thirty-seven (37) proposal submissions, showing scores across Cost (25), Experience (25), Qualifications (25), Work Plan (20), and Local Preference (5).

#### 2. Facilities & Real Estate Management 14 Facilities Agreement (Council File # 25-0471)
*   **Council Resolution / Item:** Item 39, File # 25-0471
*   **Awardee:** Ambassador Services LLC
*   **Council Approval Date:** March 26, 2025 (Estimated Amount $1,928,263.80)
*   **Requested Items:**
    1.  The fully executed Service Price Agreement for janitorial services across the 14 facilities managed by the Department of Facilities and Real Estate Management (FREM).
    2.  The complete Exhibit B pricing schedule indicating the monthly rate and cleanable square footage allocated to each of the 14 individual facilities.

#### 3. Recent Billing & Invoicing Vouchers
*   Copies of the three (3) most recent monthly invoices and payment vouchers submitted by Ambassador Services LLC and approved by the City of Dallas for municipal janitorial services under the above agreements.
