## 1.0 Executive Summary

This empirical analysis extracts, analyzes, and indexes actual public contract awards from the City of Dallas and regional Texas public bodies. These empirical records provide verifiable pricing baselines, staffing models, and subcontracting requirements. They replace assumptions with verified public transaction records.

The primary prime contractor for City of Dallas municipal janitorial services is **Ambassador Services LLC**. Ambassador holds both the overarching Citywide Master Agreement and individual department agreements. Under City of Dallas Business Enterprise Hub (BEH) rules, Ambassador must subcontract at least **23.8%** of its contract volume to certified M/WBE vendors. This mandate creates an immediate, multi-million dollar revenue path for HWB Cleaning Services LLC.

## 3.0 Deep Dive: City of Dallas Master Agreement (File # 26-1944A)

### 3.1 Solicitation & Competition Mechanics
*   **Solicitation Number:** `CSP-BYZ25-00028708`
*   **Approval Date:** Authorized by Dallas City Council on June 24, 2026.
*   **Total Proposers:** 37 cleaning companies submitted bids.
*   **Winning Awardee:** Ambassador Services LLC ("most advantageous proposer of thirty-seven").
*   **Pre-Solicitation Meeting:** October 7, 2025 at 10:00 AM CST.
*   **Funding Source:** City of Dallas General Fund (subject to annual appropriations).

### 3.2 Evaluation Scoring Rubric
The City evaluated all 37 competing proposals across five strict categories:
1.  **Cost:** 25 Points (Pricing schedule across standard, periodic, and emergency task items).
2.  **Experience:** 25 Points (Past performance on high-volume commercial/municipal campuses over 5+ years).
3.  **Qualifications:** 25 Points (Key management resumes, supervisor-to-cleaner ratios, and safety record).
4.  **Work Plan:** 20 Points (Quality control procedures, equipment fleet, chemical green standards, and emergency response times).
5.  **Local Preference:** 5 Points (Dallas County / City of Dallas physical business presence).
*   **Total Evaluation Score:** 100 Points.

### 3.3 The 23.8% M/WBE Subcontracting Carve-Out
City of Dallas Business Enterprise Hub rules require prime contractors on contracts over $250,000 to subcontract a minimum of 23.8% to certified Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprises (Form `BID-FRM-625`).
*   **Ambassador Services 5-Year Total:** $45,780,678.43
*   **Mandatory M/WBE Allocation:** **$10,895,801.47**
*   **Monthly Subcontract Pool:** **$181,596.69 per month**

Prime contractors like Ambassador frequently face challenges meeting this quota with reliable, certified contractors who maintain commercial insurance, background clearance, and quality systems. By presenting an ISO 9001-certified operational model and Texas HUB / NCTRCA MBE certifications, HWB Cleaning Services LLC stands as a prime subcontract partner.
