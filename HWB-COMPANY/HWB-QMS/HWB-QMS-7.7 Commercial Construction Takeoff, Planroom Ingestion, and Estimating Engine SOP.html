Standard Operating Procedure: Commercial Construction Takeoff, Planroom Ingestion, and Estimating Engine

1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) standardizes the automated ingestion, architectural takeoff calculation, discrepancy auditing, and proposal preparation for commercial post-construction cleaning bids under CSI MasterFormat 01 74 23 (Final Cleaning). It ensures 100% empirical traceability, protects company margins through calibrated scope multipliers, and enforces strict labeling of data discrepancies.

2.0 Scope

This procedure applies to:

  • All general contractor Invitations to Bid (ITBs) received via Microsoft Graph email or BuildingConnected.
  • Proofpoint URL decoding and automated planroom downloading via Playwright.
  • Architectural blueprint parsing and finish schedule extraction using PyMuPDF (fitz).
  • The commercial bidding calculation engine (CSI 01 74 23 3-phase formulas and TMA variable scopes).
  • The PostgreSQL ConstructionBids database records and Excel proposal generation pipeline.

3.0 Operational Procedure

3.1 Planroom Ingestion & Proofpoint URL Resolution

Commercial invitations to bid frequently arrive wrapped in enterprise email security filters (e.g., Proofpoint URL Defense). The ingestion engine executes the following steps:

  1. Proofpoint Decoding: Intercept https://urldefense.com/v3/... links in ITB email bodies. Decode hexadecimal and base64 payloads to restore authentic BuildingConnected or Planroom project URLs.
  2. Authenticated Planroom Access: Dispatch headless Playwright tasks with persistent session credentials (stored in ~/.config/hwb_buildingconnected_profile/) to access project files, specs, and drawing sets.
  3. Standard Project Archiving: Store downloaded plans in standardized paths under HWB-COMPANY/HWB-ESTIMATING/PROJECTS/[Project_Name]/.

3.2 Vector Blueprint Takeoff & Finish Schedule Extraction

To eliminate manual blueprint measuring and reduce estimation labor to zero:

  • PyMuPDF Plan Parsing: Scan PDF vector drawings for architectural sheets (coded A-, AD-, or AS-) containing Room Finish Schedules, Floor Pattern Plans, and Life Safety Area tables.
  • Flooring Finish Classification: Extract square footage broken down by CSI division:
    • CSI 09 65 00: Resilient Flooring (VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl).
    • CSI 09 67 00: Resinous & Polished Concrete / Terrazzo.
    • CSI 09 30 00: Ceramic and Porcelain Tile (wet cores, restrooms).
    • CSI 09 68 00: Carpet Tile and Broadloom.
  • Glazing & Exterior Takeoff: Detect exterior window counts, mullion heights, and interior glass partition square footage.

3.3 CSI 01 74 23 Pricing Engine & Formula Standard

Commercial post-construction proposals must adhere to the standardized 3-phase clean model:

Standard Commercial Formula

Base Scope: Phase 1 (Rough Clean @ $0.10 - $0.18/sqft) + Phase 2 (Final Clean @ $0.20 - $0.32/sqft) + Phase 3 (Touch-Up / Fluff Clean @ $0.06 - $0.10/sqft).

Specialty Add-Ons: Machine Scrubbing ($0.12/sqft), Concrete Sealing ($0.25/sqft), Interior/Exterior Glazing ($2.50/pane), Pressure Washing ($0.15/sqft).

3.4 Empirical Integrity & Takeoff Discrepancy Mandate [2026-09-04]

Whenever an architectural blueprint lacks an explicit room finish schedule, contains contradictory civil lot acreage vs. interior scope, or relies on an estimator heuristic:

  • Database Tagging: The project record in the ConstructionBids table MUST be appended with the flag:
    [DATA DISCREPANCY: Heuristic Estimate - Field Verification Required].
  • Excel Proposal Alert: Insert a prominent Amber/Gold banner at Row 5 of the generated proposal workbook reading:
    "ESTIMATOR NOTICE: Data Discrepancy / Calibrated Footprint — Scope derived from calibrated architectural footprint heuristics. Subcontractor field verification recommended prior to contract execution."
  • Executive Reporting: Every technical brief and Telegram notification card must explicitly state the delta between documented and calibrated square footages.

3.5 Automated Proposal Staging & CEO Telegram Dispatch

  1. Workbook Generation: Compile proposal workbooks using openpyxl with institutional branding, line-item itemization, payment terms (Net 30/60 pay-when-paid), and insurance certificate references.
  2. Pending Outbox Staging: Stage the proposal email in the PendingOutbox table with general contractor contact metadata.
  3. Mobile Dispatch: Send a 1-tap interactive card to CEO Humberto Dominguez's Telegram bot with instant buttons: Approve & Submit, Adjust Scope, or View Specs.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Mathematical Verification: Sum of rough, final, touch-up, and specialty line items must exactly equal total proposal value without rounding drift.
  • Discrepancy Banner Check: Any heuristic estimate must possess both the database tag and the amber alert banner in the proposal file.
  • Outbox Holding: Under no circumstances may a proposal email be sent to a general contractor without explicit CEO Telegram or web approval.
