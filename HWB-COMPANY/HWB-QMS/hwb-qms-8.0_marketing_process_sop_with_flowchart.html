4.3 The 5-Pillar Enterprise Email Campaign Readiness Architecture (BUG-131 & Migration 043)

  1. Pillar 1 — Automatic Database Readiness Trigger (trg_leads_campaign_readiness): Every insert or update on the "Leads" table runs fn_leads_compute_campaign_readiness(), which strips email whitespace/lowercases addresses, formats 10-digit US phone numbers to (###)-###-####, classifies email_inbox_type (DIRECT_PERSONAL vs. ROLE_INBOX), and checks 6 readiness gates to set campaign_ready (TRUE/FALSE) and a plain-English email_hygiene_status tag (CAMPAIGN_READY, PHONE_ONLY, DO_NOT_EMAIL, DEAD_DOMAIN_NO_MX, INVALID_OR_SUPPRESSED, SUPPRESSED, DUPLICATE_RECORD, FACILITY_CLOSED, or NON_COMMERCIAL).
  2. Pillar 2 — DNS Mail-Server (MX) Verification ("EmailDomainHealth"): All unique email domains across the CRM are verified via TCP/UDP DNS MX queries (`QTYPE=15`). Live mail domains (4,991 verified domains) set email_mx_verified = TRUE, while expired or non-existent domains (70 NXDOMAIN domains) are automatically quarantined as DEAD_DOMAIN_NO_MX to prevent hard bounces.
  3. Pillar 3 — Permanent Email Suppression Ledger ("EmailSuppressionList"): Every opt-out on /unsubscribe writes the lowercase email address to "EmailSuppressionList" and flips every matching row in "Leads" to do_not_email = TRUE and campaign_ready = FALSE. The outbound Microsoft Graph mailer (core/services/email_service.py) checks "EmailSuppressionList" immediately before transmitting any commercial message.
  4. Pillar 4 — Multi-Site Owner Deduplication & Smart Greeting Fallbacks: Campaign queue generation in blueprints/crm_api.py enforces campaign_ready = TRUE, case-insensitive North Texas county matching (COLLIN, DALLAS, DENTON, TARRANT), and DISTINCT ON (LOWER(TRIM(email))) ordering by highest contract value so multi-location childcare owners receive exactly one email per campaign batch. When a lead has a generic contact placeholder (`Facility Director` or blank), templates automatically use "Hi there" for {first_name} and "Facility Director" for {director_name}.
  5. Pillar 5 — Backoffice Visibility (✅ Campaign Ready Filter Pill & Row Badge): The Commercial Leads toolbar features a 1-click ✅ Campaign Ready filter pill, and each lead row displays a green ✅ Campaign Ready badge or amber 📞 Phone Only badge.

4.4 The 4-Safeguard Daycare Registry Miner & Closure Reconciliation Engine

When the statewide Texas HHSC Daycare Registry miner (scripts/daycare_registry_sync.py) and website contact enricher (scripts/enrich_daycare_contacts.py) download new or updated childcare centers, all data passes through automatic sanitization (preserving website-scraped emails via COALESCE when the state registry row is blank) and 4 enterprise closure safeguards:

  1. Safeguard 1 — Partial-Download Circuit Breaker: Closure reconciliation only executes if the Texas Socrata API returns a complete statewide dataset (≥ 7,500 active centers), preventing network glitches from falsely closing active leads.
  2. Safeguard 2 — Explicit State Closure Verification: Before marking a missing permit number as closed, the miner queries the Texas HHSC API directly for that operation_number to confirm operation_status = 'N' or temporarily_closed = 'YES'.
  3. Safeguard 3 — Active-Deal & Customer Protection: Facilities with active sales progress (Active, Qualified, Trained, or Engaged) are protected from automatic overwrite and keep their sales notes intact.
  4. Safeguard 4 — Self-Healing Re-Activation & Outbox Cancellation: Confirmed closed centers are set to status = 'Closed - Facility Shut Down', campaign_ready = FALSE, and email_hygiene_status = 'FACILITY_CLOSED', and any unsent drafts in "Outbox" are automatically marked CANCELLED_FACILITY_CLOSED. If a temporarily closed center reopens on a future sync, it automatically self-heals back to New and CAMPAIGN_READY.

4.5 Closed-Loop Lead & Email Marketing Campaign Lifecycle State Machine & Multi-Jurisdictional Compliance (BUG-144 & BUG-145)

  1. Direct Lead-to-Campaign Enrollment (#modal-enroll-leads-campaign & POST /api/v1/marketing/campaign/enroll-leads): Selecting leads in the Commercial Leads desk (?view=leads) and clicking Send to Campaign enrolls them directly into an active or newly created campaign, enforces multi-jurisdictional compliance filters, sets Leads.status = 'In Campaign' (📧 In Email Outreach), and stages personalized HWB-COM-001 drafts in "PendingOutbox".
  2. Real-Time Telemetry Counter Synchronization (sync_campaign_telemetry_counts): Every enrollment, outbox approval/rejection, tracking pixel open (/api/v1/marketing/track/open/<token>.gif → Leads.status = 'Engaged' + priority_level = 'High'), and calendar booking link click (/api/v1/marketing/track/click/<token> → Leads.status = 'Qualified') automatically recalculates total_targets, staged_count, sent_count, opened_count, and walkthroughs_booked on "MarketingCampaigns".
  3. Channel-Specific Opt-Out Separation (✉️ Unsubscribed vs 🚫 DNC) & IETF RFC 8058 One-Click Unsubscribe: Every commercial email includes native List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click headers. Clicking unsubscribe (via GET or POST /unsubscribe/<token>) records the address in "EmailSuppressionList" and sets Leads.status = 'Unsubscribed' (✉️ Unsubscribed), campaign_ready = FALSE, and email_hygiene_status = 'DO_NOT_EMAIL' while keeping is_dnc = FALSE so email opt-out is never conflated with telephone Do Not Call (🚫 DNC).
  4. Universal Geofence & Public-Sector Exclusion Shield: Enforced across all 4 campaign endpoints (preview-count, campaign/create, enroll-leads, and generate-drafts), blocking strict-liability states (CA, WA), international TLDs (.ca, .uk, .eu, .au, etc.), and government/public school domains (.gov, .mil, .k12.tx.us).
  5. 1-Click Campaign Completion State Transition (POST /api/v1/marketing/campaign/<id>/complete): Clicking Complete on an active campaign cancels any remaining unsent drafts, returns unsent leads (STAGED / AWAITING_APPROVAL) back to 'New', advances sent unopened leads (SENT) from 'In Campaign' to 'Contacted' for telephone follow-up, and preserves all 'Engaged', 'Qualified', and 'Unsubscribed' leads.
