Standard Operating Procedure: AIA A401 Subcontract Agreement Review & Cashflow Architecture

1.0 Purpose

To protect HWB Cleaning Services LLC from predatory subcontract clauses, protect cash flow, prevent unearned trade damage backcharges, and ensure prompt payment upon completion of CSI Division 01 74 23 final cleaning subcontracts.

2.0 Scope

This SOP applies to every commercial construction subcontract, Master Subcontract Agreement (MSA), and Purchase Order presented by General Contractors (including Novel Builders, Weekes Construction, MYCON, Source Building Group, and other DFW/Texas commercial builders) prior to executive signature by Humberto Dominguez (CEO).

3.0 Step-by-Step Subcontract Review Procedure

3.1 Inspection Point 1: Retainage Release Protocol (Section 11)

Standard Risk: GCs frequently hold 5% to 10% retainage on all subcontracts until the entire building receives its final Certificate of Occupancy and Owner commissioning, which may occur 6 to 12 months after final cleaning is completed.

Institutional Mandate: HWB will strike or amend retainage language to require prompt release:

Mandatory Subcontract Rider Language:

"Retainage applicable to CSI Division 01 74 23 Cleaning Scope shall be released in full within thirty (30) days following written punch-list sign-off and acceptance of the cleaning work by the Contractor's Project Superintendent, and shall not be contingent upon final completion of other building trades or overall project closeout."

3.2 Inspection Point 2: Pay-When-Paid Terms & Payment Horizons

Standard Risk: GCs insert "Pay-if-Paid" clauses attempting to shift owner insolvency risk entirely to the cleaning subcontractor.

Institutional Mandate: Payment horizons must strictly adhere to Texas Property Code Chapter 28 (Texas Prompt Payment Act):

  • Progress billings submitted monthly by the 25th for work completed.
  • GC must disburse payment to HWB within seven (7) days of receiving payment from Owner, or no later than forty-five (45) days from invoice date.

3.3 Inspection Point 3: Poka-Yoke Trade Damage & Backcharge Armor

Standard Risk: Following final cleaning, other trades (electricians, painters, carpet installers) often track mud, drywall dust, and debris through previously cleaned spaces, after which the GC improperly charges HWB for re-cleaning.

Institutional Mandate: Section 3.3 of AIA A401 must be supplemented with our standard backcharge defense clause:

Mandatory Backcharge Protection Rider:

"General Contractor shall provide HWB Cleaning Services LLC with a minimum of forty-eight (48) hours written notice of any alleged cleaning defect or backcharge, detailing specific room numbers. HWB shall have the exclusive right to inspect and cure the condition. General Contractor is strictly prohibited from backcharging HWB for re-cleaning required due to trade traffic occurring after HWB's initial area sign-off."

3.4 Inspection Point 4: Site Prerequisites & Exclusions (Exhibit A)

Every executed subcontract must incorporate HWB's standard CSI 01 74 23 Inclusions & Exclusions:

  • Trash Dumpsters: GC must provide commercial dumpsters within 100 feet of building exit.
  • Utilities: GC must provide active 120V electricity and hot/cold water.
  • HVAC: Permanent HVAC must be operational during final dusting to prevent dust recirculation.
  • Exclusions: Pressure washing exterior masonry (unless itemized), concrete staining, hazardous abatement.

3.5 Inspection Point 5: Direct GC Payment Portal Onboarding

Within 48 hours of subcontract award, Silas Sync (VP of CRM) shall enroll HWB in the General Contractor's automated billing clearinghouse:
