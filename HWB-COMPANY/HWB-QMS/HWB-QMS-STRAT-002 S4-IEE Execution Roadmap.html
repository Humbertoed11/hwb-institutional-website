Standard Operating Procedure: S4/IEE Project Execution Roadmap

1.0 Purpose

This document details the step-by-step process for managing our projects. It helps us find and remove waste, verify that cleaning is done perfectly, and protect our staff and customers.

2.0 Scope

This roadmap applies to all service development, office management, and site cleaning operations managed by the company.

3.0 Execution Steps

We execute projects using a five-step quality loop:

3.1 Step 1: Define

We find areas where we are losing money or time. We map out the work flow and write down the project goals so the team is aligned.

3.2 Step 2: Measure

We gather actual numbers on our cleaning operations, count labor hours, and measure any losses from poor service quality.

3.3 Step 3: Analyze

We investigate why mistakes are happening and look for ways that staff time might be wasted due to poor communication.

3.4 Step 4: Improve

We set up our standard cleaning methods, assign trained crews to the site, and use software to track task completion automatically.

3.5 Step 5: Control

We monitor performance using live dashboards, conduct daily updates of client activities, and perform checks to make sure we are ready for quality audits.

Note

Every step in this roadmap must be verified using real numbers from our databases. We do not use placeholder or simulated data.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Conduct a project review meeting with management at least once a year.
  • Verify that all new company projects map to at least three points of our business alignment standard.
