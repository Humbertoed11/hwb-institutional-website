Standard Operating Procedure: City of Dallas Municipal Procurement & Business Enterprise Hub (BEH) Engagement

1.0 Purpose

This procedure defines how HWB Cleaning Services LLC registers, qualifies, bids, and manages contracts with the City of Dallas. It establishes the exact operational steps to maintain vendor standing on the City's Bonfire procurement portal, utilize diversity compliance databases (B2Gnow), secure discretionary purchasing opportunities ($3,000 to $50,000) under the Texas Historically Underutilized Business (HUB) rules, and partner with prime contractors on large-scale municipal agreements.

2.0 Scope

Applies to the Executive Directorate, Estimating, Sales & Marketing, and Backoffice Operations pursuing institutional custodial, post-construction cleaning, and facility maintenance contracts with City of Dallas departments (including Dallas Water Utilities, Dallas Public Library, Park and Recreation, Court Services, Aviation, and Police Facilities).

4.0 Step-by-Step Procedure

4.1 Bonfire Portal Registration & Commodity Codes

  1. Navigate to the official City of Dallas procurement platform: dallascityhall.bonfirehub.com.
  2. Maintain an active vendor profile registered under HWB Cleaning Services LLC with active contact details.
  3. Map and link the following Texas Comptroller NIGP commodity codes to receive automatic solicitation notices:
    • 910-39: Janitorial and Custodial Services
    • 910-04: Air Duct Cleaning Services
    • 910-70: Pressure Washing Services
    • 962-58: Post-Construction Cleaning Services
    • 910-25: Flooring Maintenance and Strip/Wax Services

4.2 City of Dallas Vendor Number Acquisition

  1. Upon award of any informal or formal municipal contract, submit the completed Vendor Registration Form (Revised 03.14.2023).
  2. Email the completed PDF along with the signed Form W-9 to CODVendorRegistrations@dallas.gov.
  3. Store the assigned 6-to-10 digit City Vendor Number in the SigmaFidelity™ database (`SystemSettings` and `InstitutionalBids`).

4.3 Discretionary Rotation Contracts ($3,000 to $50,000 Fast Track)

Under City of Dallas administrative purchasing policy and Texas Local Government Code, purchases between $3,000 and $50,000 do not require formal Council approval or advertised competitive sealed proposals. City buyers must contact at least two certified disadvantaged businesses on a rotating basis.

Mandatory Rule

Maintain active State of Texas Historically Underutilized Business (HUB) certification with the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts. Provide the active HUB certificate number to City buyers in Water Utilities, Libraries, and Park & Recreation to remain on the informal quote rotation list.

4.4 Recognized Diversity Certifications

The City of Dallas Business Inclusion and Development (BID) Plan recognizes three certifying bodies for M/WBE participation credit:

  • NCTRCA: North Central Texas Regional Certification Agency (MBE / WBE / SBE).
  • DFWMSDC: Dallas Fort Worth Minority Supplier Development Council (MBE).
  • WBEC-Southwest: Women’s Business Enterprise Council-Southwest (WBE).

4.5 B2Gnow Diversity Portal Profile Management

  1. Register on the City of Dallas diversity monitoring portal: dallas.diversitycompliance.com.
  2. Index Primary NAICS code 561720 (Janitorial Services) and secondary NAICS 238990 (All Other Specialty Trade Contractors - Final Construction Clean).
  3. Upload verified NCTRCA/DFWMSDC certification certificates. This ensures prime contractors (such as master agreement holders like Ambassador Services LLC) can locate HWB to satisfy their mandatory 23.8% BID subcontracting goals.

4.6 Mandatory Living Wage Compliance

All service bids submitted to the City of Dallas exceeding $100,000 must strictly comply with the City of Dallas Living Wage Policy (Resolution 15-2141 / Res. 26-2902A). Wage calculations must adhere to the MIT Living Wage Calculator for Dallas County (minimum $18.00/hr base rate, plus 20.00% statutory payroll burden).

5.0 Verification & Audit Checklist

  • Bonfire profile shows "Active" with verified NIGP commodity code 910-39 attached.
  • Vendor Registration Form and W-9 staged in `HWB-COMPANY/HWB-QUOTES/CITY-OF-DALLAS-PROCUREMENT/`.
  • B2Gnow directory profile verified and linked to Dallas vendor ID.
  • All City of Dallas bid pricing processed through `core/services/estimator.py` (`calculate_institutional_bid`) enforcing the $18.00 living wage floor.
