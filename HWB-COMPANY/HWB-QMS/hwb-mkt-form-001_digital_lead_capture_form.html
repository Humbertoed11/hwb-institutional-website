Standard Operating Procedure: Marketing Form & Contact-First Quote Intake Standard

1.0 Purpose

This procedure defines how public quote and walkthrough forms operate across www.hwbcleaning.com so busy commercial buyers can request a cleaning proposal in seconds without hitting unnecessary form barriers.

2.0 Scope

Applies to the main quote form (/get-quote), the post-submission pricing calibration card (quote_success.html / POST /update-quote-details), the homepage form (/), all 8 Facility Hubs (/facilities/<slug>), and all 18 North Texas City Hubs (/locations/<city>).

3.0 Universal Mandates

  1. Contact-First Step 1 (Zero Mandatory Zip/City/Schedule Wall): On both desktop and mobile, Step 1 requires only Facility Type, Contact Name *, and Work Email * (with Phone and Company / Building Name optional). Building City, Zip Code, Square Footage, and Cleaning Schedule sit inside an optional collapsible <details> drawer with zero HTML required attributes on city, zipcode, sqft, or company.
  2. 8 Core Facility Type Chips & Hub Pre-Selection: /get-quote presents all 8 commercial verticals (Office, School / Daycare, Medical Clinic, Warehouse, Church / Worship, Post-Construction, Auto Dealership, and Other Commercial). Clicking a quote link from any Facility Hub or City Hub passes ?facility=...&city=... so the form automatically selects the matching chip and pre-fills the city.
  3. Step 2 Post-Capture Calibration: Every non-bot submission writes or updates a verified record in PostgreSQL 16.15 "Leads" and passes lead_id and reference_code to quote_success.html so the buyer can optionally tap City, Cleaning Schedule, and Square Footage in 10 seconds via POST /update-quote-details.
  4. Accessibility & Consent Shield: Every <input> and <select> has an explicit <label for="...">, every phone consent checkbox (tcpa_consent) renders unchecked by default, and public pages display clear everyday business language with zero internal system terms.

4.0 Two-Step Conversion Architecture

4.1 Step 1 — Fast Quote Request (/get-quote & /facilities/<slug>)

  • Required Fields: Facility Type Chip, Contact Name, Work Email.
  • Optional Immediate Fields: Phone Number ((214)-000-0000 mask), Company or Building Name, plus optional expandable drawer for City, Zip Code, Approximate Square Footage, and Cleaning Schedule Frequency.

4.2 Step 2 — 10-Second Pricing Calibration (quote_success.html)

  • Route: POST /update-quote-details linked by lead_id.
  • Fields: Quick-tap City chips, Cleaning Schedule chips, and Approximate Square Footage, enriching the captured "Leads" record without risking Step-1 drop-off.

5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • GET /get-quote?facility=daycare&city=McKinney returns HTTP 200 with zero required attributes on city, zipcode, or company, explicit <label for="..."> bindings, and unchecked tcpa_consent.
  • POST /get-quote with Contact Name + Work Email + Facility Type saves the lead in "Leads", logs the consent receipt in "SecurityAuditLogs", and renders quote_success.html with a valid lead_id and reference_code.
