Project Charter: HWB-MOB-2026 Mobile Application

1.0 Purpose & Goals

The goal of this project is to develop and launch a standalone mobile application for our cleaning technicians. The app will ensure that 100% of our cleaning protocols are followed on-site. It will eliminate labor waste by using GPS checks and photo-verified task completion lists.

2.0 Project Milestones

Milestone Target Date Expected Deliverables
Define 03/25/2026 Identify customer requirements and high-level project boundaries.
Measure 04/10/2026 Gather baseline data on current labor hours and service costs.
Analyze 04/25/2026 Analyze task times and identify delays across different facilities.
Design & Implement 05/20/2026 Develop the React mobile application and begin testing with 3 crews.
Verify & Control 06/20/2026 Measure service quality improvements and hand over the app to daily operations.

3.0 Key Metrics & Quality Controls

  • Labor Verification: The app will compare GPS coordinates to verify that technicians clock in within 50 meters of the site.
  • Sequential Task Locking: Technicians must complete and verify tasks in order; high-stakes sanitation tasks will require photo proof before the next task opens.
  • Offline Support: The app will save progress locally when there is no internet connection and sync automatically when connection is restored.

4.0 Resource Allocation & Governance

This project is sponsored by the CEO (Humberto Dominguez). The design and development are managed by the Systems Architect (George), with CRM and database integration supported by Silas Sync.
