1.0 Purpose & Fiscal Authority

This procedure establishes the financial controls, accounts receivable/payable workflows, 1099 subcontractor payout verification, and cloud cost governance for HWB Cleaning Services LLC. Maria Bolanos (VP of Finance) holds sole operational authority over fiscal oversight, development cost budgets, and Warchest capital reserves, with final executive approval by CEO Humberto Dominguez.

2.0 Commercial Billing & Accounts Receivable

  • Recurring Commercial Janitorial Contracts: Invoiced monthly in advance or net-15 per executed service agreement, tracked in PostgreSQL 16.15 ("Invoices" and "Customers" tables) and reconciled with QuickBooks Online.
  • General Contractor Post-Construction Subcontracts: Billed per AIA G702/G703 schedule of values (rough clean, final detail clean, window wash, and touch-up puffy clean) with Conditional/Unconditional Texas Statutory Lien Waivers per HWB-QMS-8.6.
  • Margin Floor Protection: All commercial proposals and construction takeoffs must meet a minimum 35% gross margin floor prior to release.

3.0 Accounts Payable & 1099 Subcontractor Payout Gate

No disbursement may be issued to a 1099 subcontractor partner unless the PostgreSQL 16.15 "SubcontractorPartners" record confirms all four statutory prerequisites:

  1. Verified IRS Form W-9 with encrypted TIN/EIN on file (w9_status = 'Verified').
  2. Active General Liability Certificate of Insurance (coi_status = 'Verified' and non-expired coi_expiration_date).
  3. Executed Master Subcontractor Agreement (msa_signed = TRUE).
  4. Signed Texas DWC Form-83 Independent Contractor Affirmation (dwc83_signed = TRUE).

4.0 Cloud Infrastructure & Warchest Cost Control

  • Zero-Waste Cloud Operations: Production hosting on Microsoft Azure Web App for Containers and Azure Database for PostgreSQL Flexible Server 16.15 is continuously monitored against the monthly infrastructure budget.
  • Executive Tier Access Control: All Accounting SOPs and financial forecasts are restricted strictly to Executive and Admin roles via attribute-based access control on /manual.
