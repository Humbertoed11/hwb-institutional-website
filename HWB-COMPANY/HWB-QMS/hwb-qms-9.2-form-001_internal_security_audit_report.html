3.1 Key Internal & External Findings Remediated (BUG-152)

  • NC-01 (Auth Blueprints): Removed legacy hardcoded fallback password lists (ceo_authorized_credentials and bosanna_authorized_credentials), removed .lower() case-insensitive password matching, and locked /portal/bosanna/magic-login behind @partner_access_required.
  • NC-02 (Zero-Disk Vault): Removed plaintext PDF/HTML/TXT cache writes to /app/vault/hr_documents/ so all 1099 (W-9, COI, MSA, DWC-83) and W-2 (W-4, I-9, ID) documents exist exclusively inside AES-256 encrypted PostgreSQL 16.15 BYTEA columns and stream directly from memory.
  • NC-03 (Non-Root Container & Key Separation): Enforced USER hwbapp (uid=1000) in Dockerfile, excluded .env and vault/ in .dockerignore, upgraded core/security.py to MultiFernet domain-separated key derivation, and enforced immediate session revocation in load_user() when status != 'Active'.
