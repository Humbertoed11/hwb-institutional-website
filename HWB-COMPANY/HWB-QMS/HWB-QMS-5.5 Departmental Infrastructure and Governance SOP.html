Standard Operating Procedure: Departmental Governance

1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the functional scope, responsibilities, and governance structure of HWB departments. It ensures all activities are organized into clear, manageable units that support the digital and physical value chain.

2.0 Scope

This procedure applies to all operational departments, files, and server infrastructure managed by the company.

3.0 Departmental Registry

3.1 Executive & Strategy

  • Scope: Strategic leadership, financial oversight, and QMS accountability.
  • Infrastructure: Core application routes and the Executive Pulse dashboard.

3.2 Operations & Field

  • Scope: Service delivery, mobile equipment logs, and technical sanitation.
  • Infrastructure: Operations folder and the mobile field app.

3.3 Intelligence & IT

  • Scope: Digital infrastructure, system uptime, and AI agent orchestration.
  • Infrastructure: IT folder, compliance Nginx engine, and primary PostgreSQL databases.

3.4 Sales & CRM

  • Scope: Lead ingestion, client relationship intelligence, and proposal tracking.
  • Infrastructure: CRM folder, contact synchronizers, and lead ingestor scripts.

3.5 EHSQ (Environment, Health, Safety, Quality)

  • Scope: Safety compliance, ISO auditing, and risk management.
  • Infrastructure: Quality management folder, safety digital forms, and audit records.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Ensure that all departmental folders follow the standardized naming convention.
  • Confirm that all digital interactions are logged in the database.
