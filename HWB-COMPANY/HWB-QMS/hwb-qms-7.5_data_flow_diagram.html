Standard Operating Procedure: Data Flow Diagram

1.0 Purpose

To define the end-to-end flow of information through the HWB Cleaning Services LLC platform across public quote intake, 1099/W-2 self-service onboarding, commercial bidding, closed-loop email marketing, and the controlled QMS manual.

2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Physical Truth: Data flow nodes map directly to active Flask blueprints and PostgreSQL 16.15 tables.
  2. Audit Continuity: Every state transition is logged to "GlobalActivities" and tamper-proof WORM "SecurityAuditLogs".

3.0 The Data Flow Architecture

flowchart LR A["Public Quote & Workforce Forms"] -->|"Smart Merge Upsert"| B["Flask Engine (main_app.py)"] C["1099 & W-2 Self-Service Portals"] -->|"AI PDF Extractor + AES-256"| B B -->|"SQL Write"| D["PostgreSQL 16.15: Leads, Partners, Employees & BYTEA Vaults"] D -->|"Territory Isolation"| E["Backoffice Operations & Calling Desk"] E -->|"Closed-Loop Enrollment"| F["Marketing Campaign & Dual-Table Suppression Gate"] B -->|"WORM Audit Trigger"| G["PostgreSQL 16.15: GlobalActivities & SecurityAuditLogs"] B -->|"RBAC / ABAC Gatekeeper"| H["Controlled QMS Reader (/manual)"]

4.0 Verification (Error-Free Check)

  • Lead and onboarding submissions persist in PostgreSQL 16.15 and trigger instant Telegram and email receipts.
  • All security and audit events are recorded in "GlobalActivities" and "SecurityAuditLogs".
