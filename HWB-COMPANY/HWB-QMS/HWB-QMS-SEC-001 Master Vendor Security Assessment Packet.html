3.0 Security Architecture in Everyday Words

To ensure total clarity for non-technical managers and business reviewers, our security architecture is built on five simple, unbendable rules:

  1. Separate Rooms for Every Company (Multi-Tenancy): Every subscribing business has its own separate space. Through PostgreSQL Kernel Row-Level Security, the database physically stops Company A from ever seeing Company B's bids, customer lists, or cleaner records.
  2. Unbreakable Digital Locks (AES-256 Encryption): All data moving across the web is protected with modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Highly sensitive personal details (such as Social Security Numbers and direct deposit bank accounts) are scrambled with AES-256 encryption keys before being stored.
  3. The Quarantine Room (Clean Inbound Data): When a client uploads a spreadsheet of customer contacts, it never touches our main system right away. It lands in a quarantine holding room where computer rules clean up phone numbers, fix addresses, and check for duplicates first.
  4. Peter's 15-Minute Safety Net (Disaster Recovery): The system saves full database backup snapshots every hour. If an entire cloud server goes dark, operations can be fully restored in less than 60 minutes with less than 15 minutes of data difference.
  5. Tessa Test's Hourly Quality Sentinel: An automated digital inspector named Tessa Test runs 8 different test batteries every single hour to ensure all web links, security doors, phone masks, and database connections work with zero defects.
