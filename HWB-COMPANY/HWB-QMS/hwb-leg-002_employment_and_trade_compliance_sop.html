Standard Operating Procedure: Employment & Trade Legal Compliance

1.0 Purpose

To establish an industrial-grade, zero-defect legal compliance standard across all hiring advertisements, candidate onboarding, employee payroll, subcontractor intake, job site safety, and applicant data privacy. This procedure ensures HWB Cleaning Services LLC operates in 100% accordance with federal labor laws and the Texas Labor Code.

2.0 Universal Governance & Mandates

  1. At-Will & No-Guarantee Mandate: Every public job advertisement and candidate application must explicitly state that applying does not guarantee an interview, job offer, or employment. All employment is strictly at-will.
  2. Subcontractor Trade Separation: 1099 cleaning crews operate as independent businesses. Subcontractor registration places a crew on an on-call bid roster and does NOT guarantee contract awards, minimum hours, or exclusive territory.
  3. Equal Opportunity & Reasonable Accommodations: All recruitment copy must include standard non-discrimination protections and offer reasonable accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (PWFA).
  4. No-Deduction Rule without Written Consent: Per the Texas Payday Law, no paycheck deduction of any kind may occur without a signed, written agreement from the employee.
  5. Automated Pre-Publication Audit: Prior to publishing any new job opening or intake form, the automated audit script (python3 scripts/audit_hwb_legal_compliance.py) must execute with a 100% passing score.

3.0 The 5 Operational Compliance Modules

Module 1: Hiring & Job Advertisements

  • Title VII & Texas Labor Code Chapter 21: Prohibits bias based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, gender identity, or national origin. Uses strictly role-based job duties.
  • Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA): Protects workers aged 40 and older. Scans prohibit phrases like "young energetic crew", "recent college graduate", or maximum experience caps.
  • ADA & ADAAA: Physical demands (e.g., lifting 45 lbs, 8-hour standing) must reflect actual daily duties. Applicants must receive clear notice on how to request reasonable accommodations.
  • Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (PWFA) & PUMP Act: Accommodations for pregnancy, recovery, and nursing breaks must be supported across cleaning crews.
  • IRCA & Form I-9: Postings must require candidates to be "legally authorized to work in the United States" without demanding specific identity documents upfront.
  • USERRA & GINA: Guarantees protections for military service members, veterans, and prohibits genetic or family health inquiries.

Module 2: Wages, Hours & Paycheck Deductions

  • Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA): Classifies cleaning technicians and floor specialists as hourly non-exempt employees eligible for 1.5x overtime after 40 hours. Enforces starting wages well above the $7.25 minimum wage floor ($16.00–$24.00+/hr).
  • Texas Payday Law (Texas Labor Code Chapter 61): Enforces designated paydays twice a month (semi-monthly direct deposit on the 15th and last business day). Prohibits unauthorized deductions for equipment or uniforms.
  • Equal Pay Act: Pay rates are tied strictly to standardized position codes (HWB-POS-001 through 007) with zero demographic disparity.

Module 3: Subcontractor & Trade Classification

  • U.S. DOL Independent Contractor Final Rule (29 CFR Part 795): Ensures HWB does not exert employee-like behavioral control over 1099 cleaning crews. Crews control their own methods and tools.
  • Texas Workers' Compensation Act (Texas DWC Form-83): Requires every trade partner to affirm independent contractor status and verify $1,000,000 active general liability insurance.
  • In-Arrears Payment Protocol: Subcontractor payments are made strictly in arrears upon supervisor inspection sign-off and receipt of an itemized business invoice.

Module 4: Job Site Safety & Chemical Handling

  • OSHA HazCom (29 CFR 1910.1200): Safety Data Sheets (SDS) must be accessible digitally and on physical cleaning carts. Secondary spray bottles must be labeled according to GHS standards.
  • OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens (29 CFR 1910.1030): Standard clinical cleaning protocols, PPE, and annual training required for healthcare and educational facilities.
  • EPA Clean Water Act: Waste slurry from industrial floor scrubbers (Tennant M20/S20) must be drained strictly into indoor sanitary sewer cleanouts, never outdoor storm drains.

Module 5: Applicant Privacy & Background Checks

  • Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA - 15 U.S.C. § 1681): Written authorization is required prior to criminal background screening. Pre-adverse action notices must be provided if background findings affect hiring.
  • Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA): Applicant personal contact info is encrypted in transit and at rest, with restricted administrative access.

4.0 Verification & Audit Protocol

Mandatory Automated Verification Command:

python3 scripts/audit_hwb_legal_compliance.py

The automated audit suite inspects all 14 laws across active database tables, template files, and API endpoints. Any score below 100% halts deployment immediately.
