Standard Operating Procedure: Backoffice Operations & CRM Management

1.0 Purpose

This procedure defines the operational standards for navigating, managing, and maintaining the central SigmaFidelity™ Operations Hub (/admin/operations), our unified PostgreSQL relational database, and client account lifecycle. It ensures real-time operational visibility, flawless field dispatching, strict data confidentiality, zero duplicate records across all public and internal intake forms, verified email campaign readiness, and complete alignment between executive leadership and field cleaning crews.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all administrative operators, dispatch supervisors, executive managers, sales representatives, and partner account liaisons managing commercial accounts, leads, construction bids, work orders, and personnel rosters for HWB Cleaning Services LLC.

3.0 System Architecture & Database Foundation

The system operates on an immutable, enterprise-grade architecture:

  • Database Engine: PostgreSQL 16.15 (pgvector/pgvector:pg16 locally on hwb_postgres_dev with volume postgres_data_pg16 and Azure Database for PostgreSQL Flexible Server 16.15 on sigmajan-server in production; 86 public tables, 404,470+ rows, vector 0.8.2, pg_trgm, and fuzzystrmatch). All legacy SQLite files are officially retired.
  • Core Relational Tables:
    • "Customers" / "CustomerAccounts": Commercial client facilities, square footage, assigned sales representative (assigned_rep_id), assigned technicians, and contract values.
    • "Leads": Statewide commercial Texas prospects, GIS coordinates, building square footage, contact details, assigned territory owner (owner_id), and automated campaign readiness columns (campaign_ready, email_mx_verified, email_hygiene_status, email_inbox_type).
    • "EmailDomainHealth": DNS mail-server (MX) verification cache tracking all 5,061 unique email domains across the CRM (4,991 live domains with valid MX servers; 70 dead/expired domains quarantined as DEAD_DOMAIN_NO_MX).
    • "EmailSuppressionList": Permanent CAN-SPAM opt-out and hard-bounce ledger enforced by both /unsubscribe and the outbound Microsoft Graph gateway (core/services/email_service.py).
    • "Employees": Active W-2 technicians, payroll rates, facility assignments, and AES-256 encrypted PII vault columns.
    • "JobApplicants": Candidate pool (ATS) records, interview statuses, and application tracks.
    • "SubcontractorPartners": Verified 1099 subcontractor trade partners, crew sizes, insurance status, and trade specialties (10 unique partners synchronized between Azure and Local PostgreSQL 16.15).
    • "WorkOrders": Scheduled service visits, crew notes, shift times, and security ingress access codes.
    • "SystemSettings": Stores 17 permanent Google Ecosystem, Maps CID (4883166476987591300), Knowledge Graph (/g/11g9qb6tkk), Google Ads (862-242-8739 / AW-8622428739), GA4 (G-8BX5Q7THYR), GSC verification, 1-Click Google Review shortlink (https://g.page/r/CYTq8OcJfsRDEBM/review), Microsoft Bookings walkthrough URL, and McKinney HQ NAP/SEO parameters.
    • "SigmaVault": Tracks active institutional credentials and platform identifiers (including IDs #2–#7: GOOGLE_SEARCH_CONSOLE_TOKEN, GOOGLE_ADS_ACCOUNT_ID, GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_GA4_ID, GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_CID, GOOGLE_REVIEW_SHORTLINK, and MICROSOFT_BOOKINGS_URL) monitored by Rack 4 Credential Expiry Sentinel.
    • "ClientBreadcrumbs": Micro-interaction DOM logs tracking user clicks, rage clicks, and JavaScript errors for instant client troubleshooting.
    • "UserUiPreferences" (Migration 045): Stores per-user JSONB column layouts, active presets, and view preferences across devices (synced via GET/POST /api/v1/ui-preferences/<view_key>).
    • "SecurityAuditLogs": Immutable WORM ledger recording all administrative actions, logins, and sensitive PII unmasking events.

4.0 The Operations Hub Enterprise Architecture

The backoffice operations system is structured into core enterprise modules (hardened across all 87 HTML templates to 0 WCAG 2.2 AA violations / 100% Grade A+):

  1. Accounts Desk (?view=accounts): Tracks active client facilities (e.g., Collin College Frisco Campus, Bosanna LLC, NTTA). Manages building specifications, square footage, primary billing contacts, service frequencies, keyholder maps, and territory isolation (assigned_rep_id) for Sales representatives.
  2. Commercial Leads & Two-Phase Intake (?view=leads): Manages our commercial Texas lead pipeline. Supports Contact-First 10-Second intake tagging (Desktop vs. Mobile Express), displays post-submission pricing calibration updates, enforces Sales territory ownership (owner_id), and provides a 1-click ✅ Campaign Ready filter pill and status badges.
  3. Bids & Procurement Hub (?view=bids & ?view=construction_bids): Manages Institutional Bids, Commercial GC Pipeline (with inline Assigned Rep dropdown and hardened Enrich button), General Contractors Registry, and Texas Government Programs. Certifies estimates against Yamamoto Moto's bidding engine.
  4. Dispatch & Mobile Technician Hub (?view=monitor): Real-time operations board tracking nightly shifts, technician assignments, gate access codes, and mobile PWA workflow execution.
  5. Workforce & ATS Pool (?view=workforce): Active W-2 technicians, ATS candidate pool, 1099 subcontractor partners, and automated payroll exports.
  6. Safety & EHSQ (?view=safety): Daily Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) records, OSHA 300 logs, incident reports, and EPA-registered chemical Safety Data Sheets (SDS).
  7. IT Operations & Server Monitoring Bay (?view=it): 11-Rack Command Hub monitoring server health, Rack 4 Credential Sentinel, Rack 11 Cloudflare WAF Radar, database connection latency, automated WORM audit trails, and Fix-All remediation pipelines.
  8. Executive Center & Lexicon Governance (?view=executive): User management (with password visibility eye toggles and force_pwd_reset enforcement), 5-tier Telegram permissions, magic link issuance, and compliance reporting using plain commercial cleaning language.
  9. Enterprise Boolean Search & Syntax Engine: Universal search bar deployed across Leads, Accounts, General Contractors, Institutional Bids, and Programs desks. Recognizes uppercase and lowercase Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT), parentheses grouping, exact quoted phrases ("child care center"), and explicit column prefix targeting (city:Plano, status:new) with interactive intelligent help modal cards.
  10. 5-Pillar Enterprise Deduplication & Smart Merge Engine (BUG-122 & Migration 041): All 6 public intake and backoffice creation pipelines—Subcontractor Portal (/api/v1/workforce/subcontractor), Job Applicant Portal (/api/v1/workforce/apply), Website Quote & Backoffice Leads (/get-quote & /admin/add-lead), HR Employee Onboarding & Account Conversion (/api/v1/hr/onboard, /api/v1/leads/<id>/promote, /admin/add-account), SigmaAcademy™ Course Assignments (/api/v1/academy/assign), and Commercial GC & Institutional Bids (/api/v1/construction-bids/create & /api/v1/institutional-bids/create)—automatically pre-check existing records by normalized 10-digit phone, lowercase email, or composite entity key, smart-merge updated fields into the master record, send Reference ID confirmation receipts (SUB-#00XX, APP-#00XX, LEAD-#00XXX) to the user, and display an "Already Registered — Profile Updated" status notice.

5.0 Employee Master Profile & PII Vault Security (SEC-001)

  1. Zero Plaintext Storage: Social Security Numbers, ITINs, and direct deposit checking/savings accounts are encrypted at the database layer using AES-256 Fernet ciphers.
  2. Default Masked Display: Opening the employee modal displays Social Security Numbers as ***-**-#### and bank accounts as ••••••••####.
  3. Audited 30-Second Timed Reveal: Authorized administrators (Executive, Admin, Operations) may click "Reveal" to inspect decrypted identification. The gateway logs the accessor username and IP to SecurityAuditLogs, and the screen automatically locks and re-masks after 30 seconds.
  4. Automated Security Lockout Protocol: Terminated personnel automatically have badge credentials revoked, linked ATS records marked terminated, and facility access permissions terminated.

6.0 Client Problem Troubleshooting & DOM Breadcrumbs

  1. Forensic Activity Inspection: When a client or field technician reports unexpected website behavior or submission issues, support personnel can query GET /api/v1/telemetry/breadcrumbs?session_id=<id> to inspect the exact click stream, rapid rage clicks, and JavaScript exceptions leading up to the issue.
  2. Privacy-Safe Troubleshooting: Breadcrumbs automatically redact form inputs, allowing operators to diagnose client friction without exposing passwords or confidential banking numbers.
