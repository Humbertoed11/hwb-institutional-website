Institutional Controlled Manual: Dictionary of Systems, Operations & Cognitive Architecture

1.0 Purpose

This controlled manual serves as the definitive institutional dictionary for HWB Cleaning Services LLC. It codifies operational terminology, Lean Six Sigma methodologies, and artificial intelligence architectural concepts to guarantee complete linguistic and conceptual precision across executive leadership, human operators, and autonomous AI agents.

2.0 Scope

This dictionary governs all communications, software documentation, standard operating procedures, and automated reasoning pipelines across the SigmaFidelity™ ecosystem.

3.0 Master Lexicon & Architectural Concepts

AI Systems Architecture & RAG Cognitive Bias

Associative Narrowing

Formal Definition: A systemic failure mode in autonomous language and reasoning systems wherein broad, high-level, or open-ended strategic inquiries become prematurely anchored to a dense, localized historical record due to unconstrained lexical or semantic matching. The agent's cognitive scope collapses into the gravity well of unstructured sub-documents (such as jobsite field notes or walkthrough logs) that coincidentally contain ubiquitous operational vocabulary (e.g., cost, system, used, cleaning), blinding the model to broader multi-service baselines.

Root Cause: Performing unconstrained full-text or fuzzy SQL pattern scans (e.g., ILIKE %token%) against large unstructured text columns. Because comprehensive jobsite notes contain thousands of words covering every facet of facility operations, common business questions match these notes as false-positive attractors, injecting localized historical data into the AI prompt as "authoritative context."

Empirical Case Study (2026-10-04): In production Telegram operations (@Georgebytesbot), executive questions asking "What is our cost and what is the system to be used?" were answered strictly within the scope of Bid #39 (Collin County College Frisco Campus Walkthrough). Bid #39 contained multi-thousand-word field notes detailing "cost", "systems", "Pritchard", and "APPA standards", trapping George's reasoning in a single post-secondary account rather than presenting the company's enterprise pricing and software stack across all four service lines.

Poka-Yoke Countermeasures (ARCH-014):

  1. Decoupled Database Lookups: Stripping fuzzy notes ILIKE matching from active project context resolution; restricting context binding to deterministic identifiers (e.g., bid #17, lead #82474) or high-confidence title trigram similarity (> 0.65).
  2. Enterprise Stop-Word Lexicon: An immutable catalog of 120+ standard commercial and operational terms prohibited from triggering project-level database lookups.
  3. Deterministic Semantic Intent Detection: Automated regex and intent detection for broad corporate questions (e.g., "our cost", "system to be used", "website capabilities"), automatically resetting context to GENERAL.
  4. Rule 7 Prompt Governance: Explicit instruction compelling the reasoning engine to evaluate open questions against HWB's entire enterprise scope across all 4 service lines prior to narrowing to any single account.

Lean Six Sigma Quality Engineering

Poka-Yoke (ポカヨケ / Mistake-Proofing)

Definition: Any mechanism in a process or software architecture that helps an operator avoid (yokeru) inadvertent mistakes (poka) or makes it physically or logically impossible to make a defect. In the SigmaFidelity™ software platform, Poka-Yoke design manifests as: (a) eliminating required form fields dynamically on mobile viewports to prevent client validation deadlocks, (b) database triggers that prevent deleting security records, and (c) automatic stop-word filters that prevent AI associative narrowing.

Distributed Systems & Database Architecture

Idempotency

Definition: The property of an operation or script whereby executing it multiple times produces the exact same outcome as executing it once, with zero unintended side effects, duplicated rows, or corrupted states. Required for all SigmaFidelity™ background daemons, migration scripts, and recovery handlers.

Cloud Security & Perimeter Defense

Pincer Shield (Zero-Trust Ingress)

Definition: A dual-boundary security architecture where public traffic is filtered by an Anycast edge proxy (Cloudflare Full Strict SSL / TLS 1.3) while the cloud origin server (Azure App Service) strictly blocks all inbound connections not originating from the proxy's verified CIDR IP blocks. Concurrently, local development and database ports bind exclusively to 127.0.0.1 to prevent unauthorized internal network exposure.

Compliance & Data Governance

WORM Immutability (Write-Once-Read-Many)

Definition: A data storage paradigm where information, once committed to the database, can never be altered, overwritten, or deleted by any application user or automated script. Enforced in HWB's SecurityAuditLogs via native PostgreSQL triggers to meet ISO 27001 Control A.12.4 and SOC 2 Type II compliance standards.

Commercial Lead Generation & UX Conversion

Contact-First Intake

Definition: An asynchronous lead capture strategy that splits customer intake into two sequential phases: Phase 1 captures prospect contact credentials (name, email, phone) in under 10 seconds without cognitive burden; Phase 2 offers optional pricing calibration (square footage, frequency, facility type) on the confirmation screen after the primary lead has already been safely persisted to the database.

Cognitive Architecture & Decision Velocity

Information Arbitrage & Cognitive Synthesis

Definition: The transformation of unstructured raw operational data (building blueprints, municipal bids, inspection records) into high-conviction decision-ready insights. The primary value proposition of an autonomous agent is not raw data aggregation, but real-time synthesis that eliminates human administrative labor and exposes profitable operational opportunities.
