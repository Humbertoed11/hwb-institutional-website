Product Requirements Questionnaire: SigmaJan™ SaaS Platform

Use this questionnaire to interview new cleaning business clients (subscribers) to define their specific system configuration, rules, and integration needs.

1.0 Business Goals & Metrics

  1. Primary Bottleneck: What is the biggest operational bottleneck you want to solve?
    [ ] Crew time-tracking / labor waste
    [ ] Invoicing delays / collection speed
    [ ] Quality control disputes with customers
    [ ] Schedule management & dispatch coordination
  2. Success Metrics: What is your target improvement for Q1?
    [ ] Reduce administrative audit hours by _________%
    [ ] Lower customer complaint rates below _________%
    [ ] Increase crew time utilization by _________%

2.0 Branding & Tenant Isolation

  1. White-Labeling: Do you require a custom branded domain for your clients and supervisors?
    [ ] Yes (e.g., portal.yourcompany.com)
    [ ] No (use standard yourcompany.sigmajan.com subdomain)
  2. Client Separation: What level of database isolation does your IT policy require?
    [ ] Shared database with secure table filters (standard)
    [ ] Dedicated database instance per branch (enterprise)

3.0 Crew Dispatch & Geofencing

  1. Check-in Validation: What distance limit should be enforced for crew clock-ins?
    [ ] Strict: Within 50 meters of the facility centroid (recommended)
    [ ] Medium: Within 100 meters
    [ ] Loose: GPS coordinate logged, but check-in is not blocked
  2. Task Verification (Poka-Yoke): Which cleaning tasks require mandatory photo proof?
    [ ] High-stakes disinfection tasks only (e.g., medical, restrooms)
    [ ] All checklist tasks
    [ ] Supervisor audits only
  3. Offline Window: How long should the technician app be allowed to operate without an internet connection?
    [ ] Full shift (data saved locally, syncs upon clock-out)
    [ ] Hourly sync checks

4.0 Billing, Payments, & Integrations

  1. Invoice Automation: When should the customer invoice be generated?
    [ ] Instantly upon supervisor audit approval of the work order
    [ ] Weekly or monthly batch generation
  2. Accounting Sync: Which accounting system do you use?
    [ ] QuickBooks Online
    [ ] Xero
    [ ] None (internal invoicing only)
  3. Payment Gateway: Do you require automated customer card/ACH processing?
    [ ] Yes (via Stripe/Authorize.net)
    [ ] No (receive manual checks/wire transfers)

5.0 Customer Portal & Communication

  1. Client Portal Features: What actions should your clients be allowed to perform?
    [ ] Request extra supplies or special cleaning services
    [ ] View task photos and supervisor audit reports
    [ ] Pay outstanding invoices online
    [ ] Log a service complaint / defect ticket
  2. SMS Alerts: Do you want automated text alerts sent to your customers?
    [ ] Yes, when the crew arrives on-site
    [ ] Yes, when the job is completed
    [ ] No (email updates only)

6.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Ensure all checkboxes are filled and signed off by the client's representative.
  • Verify database connection parameters and billing details before provisioning the new tenant.
