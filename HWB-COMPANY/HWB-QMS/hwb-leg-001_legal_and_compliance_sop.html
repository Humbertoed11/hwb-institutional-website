Standard Operating Procedure: Legal and Compliance SOP

1.0 Purpose

To ensure HWB Cleaning Services LLC operates within all local, state, and federal legal frameworks, including contract management and regulatory compliance.

2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Guidance First: If a contract clause is ambiguous, ASK the CEO before signing or approving.
  2. Tier 6 Telemetry: Every contract audit or renewal must be logged.
  3. Physical Truth: Reference absolute server paths for the "Digital Contract Vault."

3.0 Procedure

3.1 Contract Review

All client service agreements must be reviewed for indemnity and liability clauses. Use the CRM Strategic Edit view to log specific legal requirements.

3.2 Regulatory Monitoring

Quarterly review of changes in OSHA, FLSA, and environmental regulations. Update the EHSQ dashboard accordingly.

3.3 Multi-Jurisdictional Electronic Communications & Anti-Spam Playbook (BUG-144 & BUG-145)

All commercial email outreach transmitted by HWB Cleaning Services LLC is governed by a 5-tier statutory and ISO compliance matrix enforced directly in application code (core/services/email_service.py, blueprints/crm_api.py, and blueprints/public.py):

  1. Federal Jurisdiction — CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.) & IETF RFC 8058 / RFC 2369: Every commercial message includes accurate sender headers, a physical postal address (3342 FM 1827 Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071), a clear 1-click unsubscribe link in the body, and native List-Unsubscribe + List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click headers for Gmail, Yahoo, and Microsoft Outlook. Opt-outs execute in 0 seconds via both GET and POST /unsubscribe/<tracking_token> with zero login required.
  2. State Jurisdiction — Texas Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 321, TDPSA (Ch. 541) & CA/WA Strict-Liability Geofence: Complies with the Texas Electronic Mail Solicitation Act and Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. Outbound cold campaigns automatically block California (CA — Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17529.5) and Washington (WA — RCW 19.190) leads across all 4 campaign endpoints.
  3. County & Municipal Jurisdiction — Collin County, Texas Exclusive Venue & Public Sector Exclusion: Sets exclusive legal venue in Collin County, Texas, and automatically excludes government, military, and public school domains (.gov, .mil, .k12.tx.us) from automated cold email blasts so public officials are engaged solely through formal RFP/Institutional Bidding channels.
  4. International Jurisdiction — CASL, GDPR, UK PECR & Australia Spam Act Geofence: Blocks all international country-code top-level domains (.ca, .ca.gov, .uk, .co.uk, .eu, .de, .fr, .es, .it, .nl, .au, .nz, .jp, .cn, .in, .mx, .br).
  5. ISO & SOC 2 Governance — Dual-Table Suppression Gate & Channel Separation: Satisfies ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27701:2019, and SOC 2 Type II Privacy Criteria P2.1 by enforcing a Dual-Table Outbound Suppression Gate in transmit_email() (checking both "EmailSuppressionList" and "Leads"), tamper-proof boot seeder protection in main_app.py, and separate channel statuses for email opt-out (✉️ Unsubscribed) versus telephone Do Not Call (🚫 DNC).

3.4 Full-Website TCPA, CIPA § 631, WCAG 2.2 AA & SOC 2 Compliance Architecture (BUG-146)

Every public, portal, and authentication route across https://www.hwbcleaning.com (37 total routes) enforces the 6-Pillar Enterprise Website Compliance Standard:

  1. TCPA Prior Express Written Consent (47 U.S.C. § 227 & Tex. Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 301–305): Both the Home Quote Form (templates/index.html) and Full Quote Form (templates/quote_form.html) make phone numbers optional and display an explicit TCPA call/SMS consent disclosure with clickable links to /privacy-policy (v2.3.0) and /terms (v2.2.0).
  2. Universal CIPA § 631 & CPRA Affirmative Cookie Gate: main_app.py @app.after_request automatically injects the #hwb-cookie-banner privacy dialog onto all standalone public HTML pages (/capability-statement, /prequal, /academy, /onboard/bosanna, /tma/estimator) so no page lacks privacy controls.
  3. ADA Title III & WCAG 2.2 Level AA Zero-Defect Standard: Every route enforces a single <h1> heading, a <main id="main-content"> landmark, a keyboard skip-link, explicit <label for> and aria-label bindings on all form controls, titled iframes, pinch-to-zoom enabled on all viewports, and rel="noopener noreferrer" + aria-label on all external links.
  4. SOC 2 CC6.1 Route & Margin Isolation: Internal reference routes (/design-system and /csi) require authentication (@login_required), internal gross margin sliders are hidden from unauthenticated users on /tma/estimator, and every HTTP response includes Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=(), payment=(), usb=(), interest-cohort=().
  5. OSHA 1910.1200 (GHS) & ISO 45001 Empirical Transparency: /compliance and /ehsq render live verified GHS chemical records from PostgreSQL "Chemicals" and verified OSHA safety records from "SafetyIncidents" (0.00 TRIR), while /methodology presents plainspoken inspection KPIs.
  6. Zero Internal Process Jargon on Public & Client-Facing Surfaces (BUG-147): Public legal pages (/terms, /privacy-policy, /unsubscribe), client/partner portals, training certificates, authentication screens, and outbound client emails execute 100% of underlying compliance and cryptographic controls silently in the backend while presenting plain-spoken commercial business language without internal SOP numbers, database table names, cryptographic cipher labels, or internal AI agent personas.
