Standard Operating Procedure: Purchasing Process (ISO 8.4)

1.0 Purpose

To define the standardized procedure for the selection and evaluation of external providers, ensuring zero-defect inputs into the SigmaFidelity™ value chain.

2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Tier 6 Telemetry: Every supplier performance audit must be logged.
  2. Physical Truth: Use absolute paths for the digital PO repository.

3.0 Procurement Lifecycle

graph TD Start([Need Identification]) --> Select[Supplier Evaluation] Select --> PO[PO Creation] PO --> Verify[Receipt & Verification] Verify --> SDS[SDS Registry Update] SDS --> Audit[Annual Supplier Review] Audit --> End([Complete]) Verify -- Defect --> CAR[Initiate Corrective Action] CAR --> Select

4.0 Procedure

  1. Selection: Evaluate suppliers based on Accuracy, Compliance (SDS), and Cycle Time.
  2. Execution: Document all orders via formal PO specifying SigmaFidelity™ Grade.
  3. Verification: Upon delivery, the Purchasing Lead verifies the shipment for built-in error-prevention compliance (intact labels and valid expiration).

4.5 1099 Subcontractor Onboarding, Dual-Mode Tax ID (EIN / SSN), Digital Substitute Form W-9, & Email Deliverability Standard (BUG-166 & BUG-167)

  • Dual-Mode Tax ID Switcher & Single-Member LLC Support (BUG-167): On the 1099 Subcontractor Onboarding Portal (/onboard/subcontractor/<token>) and backoffice subcontractor modals, operators and trade partners can switch between EIN (XX-XXXXXXX) and SSN / ITIN (XXX-XX-XXXX) with a single click. Single-Member LLCs (Disregarded Entities) may register using either a company EIN or the owner's SSN per IRS Form W-9 instructions.
  • Real-Time IRS / SSA Validation & Digital Substitute Form W-9 PDF Generator: All 9-digit Tax IDs are validated against official IRS EIN campus prefixes and SSA SSN rules (validate_federal_tax_id) before AES-256 Fernet encryption. Trade partners without a pre-signed W-9 PDF file on their phone may select Generate Digital W-9 Automatically (w9_mode='digital'), which captures IRS Part II certification under penalties of perjury (26 CFR § 31.3406(h)-3) and generates a locked PDF 1.4 Substitute Form W-9 in PostgreSQL "SubcontractorDocuments".
  • 1-Click Receipt COI Upload & Audited Backoffice TIN Reveal: Returning contractors in Pending COI status can upload their ACORD 25 Certificate of Insurance directly from the confirmation receipt card to advance immediately to Docs Under Review. Executive and Admin users can unmask encrypted TINs for 30 seconds via POST /api/v1/workforce/subcontractors/<id>/reveal-tin (logged as CRITICAL SUBCONTRACTOR_TIN_REVEALED in "SecurityAuditLogs").
  • Authenticated Email & 1-Tap SMS Onboarding Delivery (BUG-166): Outbound subcontractor portal invitations and receipts are signed with Cloudflare Microsoft 365 DKIM (selector1 / selector2), BIMI (default._bimi.hwbcleaning.com), and DMARC quarantine enforcement (p=quarantine), use canonical https://www.hwbcleaning.com CTA buttons, and include a 1-tap SMS invite option in the backoffice.

5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Supplier On-Time Delivery (OTD) >98%.
  • Zero unverified chemicals found at client sites.
  • 100% of active 1099 subcontractor partners have an encrypted W-9 (uploaded or digitally generated Substitute W-9), executed Master Subcontractor Agreement, and Texas DWC-83 election on file.
