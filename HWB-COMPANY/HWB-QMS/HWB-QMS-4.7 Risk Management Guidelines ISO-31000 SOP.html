Standard Operating Procedure: Risk Management Guidelines (ISO 31000)

1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the framework for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks within HWB Cleaning Services LLC. This ensures corporate resilience and operational continuity by aligning with ISO 31000 risk management principles.

2.0 Scope

This procedure applies to all operational, financial, and digital software development risks identified in our business.

3.0 Risk Assessment Matrix

  • Probability: 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost Certain).
  • Impact: 1 (Insignificant) to 5 (Catastrophic).
  • Criticality Score: Probability x Impact.

4.0 Procedure

  1. Identification: Scan our system logs, databases, and cleaning logs to identify potential operational or software failures.
  2. Assessment: Record the identified risk in the Risk Register.
  3. Mitigation: Implement controls (such as Peter's recovery directives or database checkpoints) to lower the risk.
  4. Guidance: If a high-level risk is identified, the agent must ask the CEO for instructions before starting any mitigation actions.

5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Perform an annual management audit of the active Risk Register.
  • Verify that all high-level risks have an associated mitigation plan.
