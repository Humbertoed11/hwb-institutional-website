Standard Operating Procedure: Municipal Procurement & Government Certification Playbook

1.0 Purpose

This playbook establishes the operating rules for HWB Cleaning Services LLC to qualify, bid, and win commercial cleaning, janitorial, and post-construction contracts with city, county, state, and federal agencies. It covers government diversity certifications, portal registrations, non-competitive discretionary purchases ($3,000 to $50,000), prime contractor teaming arrangements, safety credentials, and bidding risk controls.

2.0 Scope

This procedure applies across all HWB entities: Executive Leadership, Estimating, Sales & Marketing, Backoffice Operations, and Field Supervision. Target clients include the City of Dallas, North Texas Tollway Authority (NTTA), Collin College, Dallas ISD, Frisco ISD, Plano ISD, Texas State Agencies (TxDOT, Texas HHS), and Federal Installations.

4.0 City of Dallas Operational Playbook

4.1 The $3,000 to $50,000 Discretionary Fast Track

  1. Under Texas Local Government Code § 252.0215, City departments (Dallas Water Utilities, Public Libraries, Park and Recreation, Court Services) must contact at least two certified HUB vendors on a rotating basis for work between $3,000 and $50,000.
  2. These purchases do not go through formal 6-month RFP cycles or require City Council votes. Department managers issue direct Purchase Orders (POs) for quick-turn services (carpet extraction, hard floor refinishing, post-flood water removal, window washing).
  3. HWB's Sales team must provide HWB's State of Texas HUB certificate directly to departmental facility managers to ensure inclusion on the rotating quote roster.

4.2 B2Gnow Prime Teaming ($32M Ambassador Services ID/IQ)

  1. The City of Dallas awarded a 5-year, $32 Million citywide janitorial master agreement to Ambassador Services LLC (Contract `CSP-BYZ25-00028708`).
  2. Under the City’s Business Inclusion and Development (BID) Plan, Ambassador Services must allocate 23.80% to 25.00% of contract spending (approx. $7.6M to $8.0M) to certified M/WBE subcontractors.
  3. All subcontractor utilization and monthly payments are tracked inside the City's diversity compliance portal: dallas.diversitycompliance.com (B2Gnow).
  4. HWB maintains its primary profile on B2Gnow under NAICS 561720 (Janitorial) and 238990 (Specialty Construction Clean) so prime contractors can select HWB to fulfill their mandatory inclusion goals.

4.3 Bonfire Portal & Commodity Codes

  1. Register on the City of Dallas electronic procurement system: dallascityhall.bonfirehub.com.
  2. Attach the following Texas Comptroller NIGP commodity codes to receive instant bid alerts:
    • 910-39: Janitorial and Custodial Services
    • 962-58: Professional Cleaning Services (Post-Construction)
    • 910-70: Pressure Washing and Exterior Maintenance
    • 910-25: Flooring Maintenance (Stripping, Waxing, Polishing)
    • 910-04: Air Duct Cleaning and HVAC Remediation

4.4 City Vendor Number Staging

  1. Upon contract award or rotation selection, complete the official Vendor Registration Form (Revised 03.14.2023).
  2. Email the completed PDF form and signed W-9 to: CODVendorRegistrations@dallas.gov.
  3. Log the assigned City Vendor Number in PostgreSQL (`SystemSettings` and `InstitutionalBids`).

6.0 Quality Standards & RFP Scoring Multipliers

When public agencies score 100-point proposals, these industry credentials earn maximum points in the Technical Capability category:

  • ISSA CIMS & CIMS-Green Building: Proves operational quality, standardized supervisor ratios, and green cleaning compliance.
  • Green Seal GS-42 Standard: Mandates non-toxic, eco-friendly chemicals (GS-37/GS-40) and CRI Green Label HEPA filtration vacuums.
  • Kaivac Touchless Restroom Specialist: Standardizes 500-PSI pressure washing and chemical extraction, cutting bathroom cleaning time by 60% with zero mop cross-contamination.

7.0 The Estimating & Margin Defense Formula

All municipal bids must be calculated using `core/services/estimator.py` (`calculate_institutional_bid`):

  1. Living Wage Baseline: Enforce City of Dallas Living Wage floor ($18.00/hr) plus 20.00% statutory payroll burden (FICA 7.65%, SUTA 2.70%, FUTA 0.60%, Workers' Comp Code 9014 4.85%, General Liability 4.20%) = $21.60/hr loaded labor rate.
  2. Negotiation Triad:
    • Published Submittal Price: Calculated at 21.0% margin (Target Margin 18.0% + 3.0% Negotiation Buffer).
    • Authorized Field Close Price: Calculated at 18.0% margin (CEO target margin).
    • Walk-Away Floor: Calculated at 14.0% margin (absolute lowest acceptable rate).
  3. Wage Revision Clause: Every proposal must state that if the City Council raises the living wage floor during the 60-month term, billing rates adjust concurrently by the exact labor dollar delta multiplied by statutory burden (1.20) plus standard G&A.
  4. Liquidated Damages Defense: Prevent $100–$250 performance deductions using GPS geo-fenced clock-ins on the technician mobile app, alerting dispatch 45 minutes before shift start if a cleaner has not arrived.
