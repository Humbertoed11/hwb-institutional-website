## 1.0 Purpose
To define the governance, management, and organizational standards for the HWB Cleaning Services LLC **Official Corporate Intranet** (SharePoint Ecosystem). This SOP ensures data integrity, ISO 9001:2015 compliance, and the elimination of "Process Fog" across all digital portals.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all SharePoint sites, document libraries, and communication portals operated under the `hwbcleaning.com` Microsoft 365 tenant.

## 3.0 Intranet Taxonomy & Hierarchy

### 3.1 The "Single Source of Truth" Mandate
All corporate sites must be consolidated or linked to the **HWB SigmaFidelity™ Portal**. 
*   **Official URL:** `https://netorgft3163094.sharepoint.com` (Root Communication Site)
*   **Executive Hub:** `https://netorgft3163094.sharepoint.com/sites/sigmaexecutivehub` (Restricted Access)

### 3.2 Site Categorization
1.  **Communication Sites:** Used for broad company announcements, news, and the "Expert SEO" public-facing staging.
2.  **Team Sites:** Used for department-specific operational data (Sales, Finance, Operations).
3.  **Hub Sites:** The structural glue that connects all departments to the root Intranet.

## 4.0 Management Protocols

### 4.1 Document Control (ISO 9001 Alignment)
*   **Versioning:** All documents uploaded to the Intranet MUST have "Major Versioning" enabled.
*   **Naming Convention:** Files must follow the `HWB-[DEPT]-[YYYY]-[MM]-[DD]-[NAME]` format. 
*   **Zero Synthetic Data:** Placeholder or "make-belief" data is strictly prohibited in production libraries.

### 4.2 Permission Governance
*   **Owner:** CEO (Humberto Dominguez).
*   **Steward:** Systems Architect (George).
*   **Access Requests:** All new user access must be authorized by the CEO and logged in the `HWB-HR` employee registry.

### 4.3 Redundancy & Consolidation
*   Retired or legacy sites (e.g., `HWBDASH`, `HWB_Intranet`) must be audited by the Systems Architect and consolidated into the primary Hub to prevent data fragmentation.

## 5.0 Verification
*   **Monthly Audit:** Maria Bolanos (VP of Finance) and George (Systems Architect) will conduct a monthly "Process Fog" audit to ensure site activity aligns with corporate strategic objectives.
*   **Broken Link Check:** Automated weekly verification of all internal links and logo assets.

## 6.0 Notes and Cautions
*   **Data Sovereignty:** All data stored on the Intranet is the intellectual property of HWB Cleaning Services LLC.
*   **Compliance:** Sensitive client data from the **BabySOP.com** vertical must be stored in "HIPAA-Standard" encrypted libraries only.
