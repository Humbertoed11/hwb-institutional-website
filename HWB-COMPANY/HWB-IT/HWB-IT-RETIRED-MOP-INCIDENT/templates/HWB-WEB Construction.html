PHASE 01

The Rough Clean

Conducted immediately after framing, plumbing, and electrical are complete. The focus remains on the removal of large debris and preparation for the finish work.

  • Removal of all trash and construction debris.
  • Detailed sweeping and vacuuming of all surfaces.
  • Sticker and label removal from windows and fixtures.
  • Sanitization of high-traffic transit areas.
PHASE 02

The Final Clean

The most intensive stage. A detailed sanitization of every square inch ensures the facility is ready for occupation and official inspection.

  • Interior window cleaning including frames and tracks.
  • Deep cleaning of all cabinetry, drawers, and closets.
  • Detailed sanitization of restrooms and kitchenettes.
  • Floor restoration and first-pass polishing.
PHASE 03

The Fluff & Touch-Up

Conducted 24-48 hours before the grand opening. This phase eliminates settled dust and ensures a zero-defect visual presentation for the client partner.

  • Removal of new dust settled after the Final Clean.
  • Polishing of chrome, stainless steel, and glass.
  • Fingerprint and smudge removal from all touchpoints.
  • Final audit against the CSI: Clean Scene Investigation standard.
