3-Phase Post-Construction Cleanup FAQs

What are the 3 phases of commercial post-construction cleanup (CSI Division 01 74 23)?

Phase 1 is the Rough Clean (clearing framing debris, packaging, and sweeping subfloors), Phase 2 is the Final Detail Clean (removing stickers, paint overspray, and silicone from glass, wiping all cabinetry inside and out, sanitizing restrooms, and machine-scrubbing floors), and Phase 3 is the White-Glove Touch-Up (removing settled dust 24–48 hours before owner handover and Certificate of Occupancy).

How does HWB price commercial post-construction final cleaning bids?

We review your architectural floor plans, finish schedules, or walk the job site with your superintendent to price each phase based on square footage, interior and exterior glass, cabinetry, floor types, and turnover deadlines.

Are HWB construction cleanup crews OSHA-certified and insured for commercial job sites?

Yes. HWB Cleaning Services LLC maintains a verified 0.00 TRIR safety record, OSHA-10/30 trained field supervisors, site-specific Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs), full PPE compliance, and full Commercial General Liability and Workers' Compensation coverage.
