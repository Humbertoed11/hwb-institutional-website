The 3-Phase Cleanup Plan

Phased cleaning services for North Texas developments.

Phase 01

Rough Site Sweeping

Post-grading and utilities setup. We focus on clearing large construction debris, cleaning out concrete curbs and gutters, and sweeping main roadways to protect storm drains and keep the site clear.

Phase 02

Road and Curb Washing

The intensive wash. Complete power washing of completed asphalt streets, concrete sidewalks, driveway entries, and public walkways to remove heavy mud, vehicle tracks, and construction residue.

Phase 03

Handover Sweep & Detail

Conducted right before developer handover. We perform a final street sweep, clean public signs and mailboxes, and clear remaining windblown litter to ensure a completely clean neighborhood presentation.
