Active Neighborhood, Street & Builder Debris Cleanup

During the construction of residential subdivisions and commercial developments, building crews generate daily trash, lumber scraps, mud, and dust. Backed by HWB’s in-house Safety Department, PPE and PIT-trained teams work alongside project superintendents to haul away crew debris, keep transit lines and streets clear of mud for City Inspectors, power wash sidewalks, and detail interiors before handover.

  • Builder Trash & Lot Debris Removal During Active Construction
  • Street, Curb, Gutter & Transit Line Sweeping for City Inspections
  • 3-Phase Interior Rough, Final Detail & Touch-Up Cleaning
  • Final Superintendent Walkthrough and Quality Check

Division 1: 3-Step Neighborhood & Street Cleanup

Keeping active subdivisions, transit lines, and streets clear of debris for City Inspectors and municipal handover.

Step 01

Rough Site & Builder Debris Sweeping

During active construction and utility setup, crews clear framing trash, packaging, and building debris from lots, clean out concrete curbs and gutters, and sweep main transit lines to protect storm drains.

Step 02

Road, Curb & Sidewalk Washing

The intensive wash. Complete power washing of completed asphalt streets, concrete sidewalks, driveway entries, and public walkways to remove heavy mud, vehicle tracks, and construction residue.

Step 03

City Inspection & Handover Sweep

Conducted right before municipal or City Inspector review. Crews perform a final street and curb sweep, clean public signs, and clear remaining windblown litter so every transit line passes inspection.

Division 2: 3-Phase Interior Building Final Clean

Turnkey interior post-construction cleaning for newly built amenity centers, schools, medical clinics, offices, and commercial buildings.

Phase 01

Interior Rough Clean

Removing interior framing scraps, drywall dust, plastic sheeting, cardboard boxes, and trade trash, followed by full subfloor sweeping so flooring and finish trades can work cleanly.

Phase 02

Final Detail Clean

Safe removal of stickers, paint overspray, and adhesive from windows and glass, wiping all cabinets and drawers inside and out, detailing kitchens and restrooms, and scrubbing hard floors.

Phase 03

Final Touch-Up (Fluff Clean)

Completed 24 to 48 hours before final superintendent walkthrough or Certificate of Occupancy to remove settled dust, polish glass and fixtures, and leave every room move-in ready.

3-Step Neighborhood & 3-Phase Post-Construction Cleanup FAQs

What are the 3 phases of commercial post-construction cleanup?

Phase 1 is the Rough Clean (clearing framing debris, packaging, and sweeping subfloors), Phase 2 is the Final Detail Clean (removing stickers, paint overspray, and silicone from glass, wiping all cabinetry inside and out, sanitizing restrooms, and machine-scrubbing floors), and Phase 3 is the Final Touch-Up (removing settled dust 24–48 hours before owner handover and Certificate of Occupancy).

How does HWB price commercial post-construction final cleaning bids?

HWB reviews architectural floor plans, finish schedules, or walks the job site with the project superintendent to price each phase based on square footage, interior and exterior glass, cabinetry, floor types, and turnover deadlines.

Are HWB construction cleanup crews safety-trained and insured for commercial job sites?

Yes. HWB Cleaning Services LLC maintains an in-house Safety Department, a verified 0.00 TRIR safety record, OSHA-10/30 trained field supervisors, PPE and PIT safety training around heavy equipment, and full Commercial General Liability and Workers' Compensation coverage.

What is included in 3-Step Neighborhood, Street, and Builder Debris Cleanup?

During subdivision construction, HWB crews remove trash, lumber scraps, and packaging left by building trades, sweep streets, concrete curbs, and gutters so transit lines pass City Inspection, power wash streets, sidewalks, and driveways, and complete a final neighborhood detailing sweep before developer handover.
