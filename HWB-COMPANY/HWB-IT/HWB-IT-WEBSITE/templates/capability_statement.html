About Us

It’s not just about cleaning anymore; It is about the human experience that we all depend upon. We know what to do, how to do it and why we are here. HWB is a commercial cleaning company providing complete janitorial and custodial services to clients in the private and public sector. We operate throughout the DFW Metroplex and the entire State of Texas. Integrity, communication, and attention to detail are our company values.

Core Competencies
  • Client Satisfaction Driven: Clinical inspection routines & QA checks
  • Fast Problem Resolution: Instant digital escalation & local supervisor action
  • Year-Round Staff Training: Structured SOP & OSHA standard alignment
  • Cost Control: Transparent itemized production rates & zero billing creep
  • Green Cleaning Practices: Eco-friendly plant-based chemical stewardship
  • Safety Programs: OSHA-compliant commercial and industrial safety protocols
  • Technology Driven Supervision: GPS timestamps & digital task audit

  • Ironclad Reliability: We do what we say we are going to do. Period.
