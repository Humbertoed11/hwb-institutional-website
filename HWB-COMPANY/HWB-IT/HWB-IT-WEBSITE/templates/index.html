What is included in HWB's 3-Phase Post-Construction Final Cleaning for General Contractors?

Our CSI Division 01 74 23 post-construction turnover includes Phase 1 Rough Clean (debris, trash, and framing dust removal), Phase 2 Final Detail Clean (sticker, adhesive, and paint overspray removal from glass, full interior/exterior cabinet wiping, restroom fixture sanitization, and machine floor scrubbing), and Phase 3 White-Glove Touch-Up prior to Certificate of Occupancy.

How does HWB prevent cross-contamination in medical clinics, schools, and daycares?

We enforce a strict 4-Color Microfiber Zoning System paired with EPA List N hospital-grade disinfectants and HEPA filtration backpack vacuums: Red cloths are restricted to toilets and urinals, Yellow to restroom sinks and counters, Green to breakrooms and kitchens, and Blue to general office desks, classrooms, and glass.
