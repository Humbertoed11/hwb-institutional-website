How is commercial office cleaning priced in Dallas-Fort Worth?

Every building is different, so HWB Cleaning does not use one-size-fits-all square-foot rates. A supervisor meets on-site at the facility for a walkthrough to review cleanable square footage, weekly service frequency, restrooms and breakrooms, and floor types. From that visit, a clear written quote will be provided with no hidden fees.

How is a post-construction cleanup planned with a General Contractor?

Every job site has different finishes and inspection timelines. HWB reviews the plans and walks the site with the project manager or superintendent to confirm the exact cleanup tasks, schedule, and safety requirements for Rough Clean, Final Detail Clean, and final touch-up before handover.

How does HWB prevent cross-contamination in medical clinics, schools, and daycares?

HWB uses 4-color microfiber cloths paired with properly diluted disinfectants and HEPA backpack vacuums: Red cloths are used only for toilets and urinals, Yellow for restroom sinks and counters, Green for breakrooms and kitchens, and Blue for office desks, classrooms, and glass.
