How do you plan a post-construction cleanup with a General Contractor?

Every job site has different finishes and inspection timelines. We review the plans and walk the site with the project manager or superintendent to confirm the exact cleanup tasks, schedule, and safety requirements for Rough Clean, Final Detail Clean, and final touch-up before handover.

How does HWB prevent cross-contamination in medical clinics, schools, and daycares?

We use color-coded microfiber cloths paired with EPA-approved hospital-grade disinfectants and HEPA backpack vacuums: Red cloths are used only for toilets and urinals, Yellow for restroom sinks and counters, Green for breakrooms and kitchens, and Blue for office desks, classrooms, and glass.
