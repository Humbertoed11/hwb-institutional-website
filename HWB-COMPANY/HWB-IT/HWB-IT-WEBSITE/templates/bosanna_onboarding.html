Sigma Academy™ Safety & Quality Certification

Stage 3 of 4

Review the mandatory safety standards below and achieve 80%+ on the comprehension check to unlock your digital campus access credential.

Standard 1: Chemical Safety & HazCom (OSHA 1910.1200)
Always dilute chemicals according to dispenser specifications. Never mix bleach with ammonia or acid-based bowl cleaners (generates toxic chlorine gas). All secondary spray bottles must carry full GHS product labels.
Standard 2: Bloodborne Pathogens & PPE (OSHA 1910.1030)
Treat all bodily fluids as potentially infectious. Wear disposable nitrile gloves and eye protection. Dispose of contaminated materials in labeled red biohazard bags. Wash hands immediately after removing gloves.
Standard 3: Slip-and-Fall Prevention & Equipment Safety
Deploy bright yellow "Caution: Wet Floor" signs at all corridor entrances before wet mopping or operating floor scrubbers. Signs must remain upright until the surface is 100% dry. Inspect electrical cords for frays daily.
Standard 4: Emergency Response, First Aid & CPR/AED Protocol (T&C § 140 / 144)
In medical emergencies, notify the shift supervisor and call 911 immediately. Know the location of wall-mounted Automated External Defibrillator (AED) units in every campus building. All on-site non-cleaning supervisors maintain active American Red Cross / AHA First Aid & Adult CPR credentials.
Standard 5: Restroom Barrier Tension Poles & Safety Signage (T&C § 134 & SOW § 197)
Always place yellow 'Closed for Cleaning' tension barrier poles across restroom doorways before beginning service. Propping doors open with trash cans is strictly forbidden by Collin College safety rules. Post wet-floor caution cones until completely dry.
