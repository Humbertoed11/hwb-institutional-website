Industrial & Warehouse Cleaning FAQs

What equipment does HWB use for industrial warehouse concrete floor scrubbing?

We deploy industrial ride-on and walk-behind scrubber-sweepers (including Tennant M20-class equipment) paired with low-foam industrial degreasers to remove forklift tire marks, hydraulic oils, pallet debris, and warehouse dust from sealed and unsealed concrete.

Can HWB service 24/7 distribution centers and manufacturing plants without disrupting shifts?

Yes. Our OSHA-certified industrial crews coordinate aisle-by-aisle scrubbing, loading dock debris clearing, and high-volume locker room and restroom sanitization around active forklift traffic and shift handovers.

How do you quote industrial warehouse floor scrubbing in DFW?

We walk the warehouse with your facility manager to check the open bay and aisle square footage, the amount of tire marks or oil buildup on the concrete, and whether you need a one-time deep scrub or regular scheduled visits.
