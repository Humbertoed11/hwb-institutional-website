Industrial & Warehouse Cleaning FAQs

What equipment does HWB use for industrial warehouse concrete floor scrubbing?

HWB deploys industrial ride-on and walk-behind scrubber-sweepers (including Tennant M20-class equipment) paired with low-foam industrial degreasers to remove forklift tire marks, hydraulic oils, pallet debris, and warehouse dust from sealed and unsealed concrete.

Can HWB service 24/7 distribution centers and manufacturing plants without disrupting shifts?

Yes. Backed by HWB’s in-house Safety Department, PPE and PIT-trained industrial crews coordinate aisle-by-aisle scrubbing, loading dock debris clearing, and high-volume locker room and restroom sanitization around active forklift traffic and shift handovers.

How is industrial warehouse floor scrubbing quoted in DFW?

A supervisor walks the warehouse with facility management to check the open bay and aisle square footage, the amount of tire marks or oil buildup on the concrete, and whether a one-time deep scrub or regular scheduled visits are needed. A clear written quote will be provided.
