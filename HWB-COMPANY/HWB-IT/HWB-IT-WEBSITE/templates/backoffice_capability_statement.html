Corporate Overview & Operational Philosophy HWB-CAP-001

It is not just about cleaning anymore; it is about the human experience that our clients and communities depend upon every single day. HWB Cleaning Services LLC is a standardized commercial cleaning contractor delivering turnkey janitorial, deep sanitization, and specialized custodial solutions across the private and public sectors. Operating throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex and across the State of Texas, our operational delivery is grounded in three immutable pillars: Integrity, Communication, and Surgical Attention to Detail.

Core Competencies
  • Hospital-Grade Quality Assurance: Routine multi-point inspection audits and standardized digital checklists.
  • Rapid Incident Escalation: Direct supervisor communication with immediate same-day physical remediation.
  • Continuous Technician Training: Structured standard operating procedures (SOPs) and strict OSHA alignment.
  • Transparent Cost Control: Verifiable square-footage production rates with zero hidden fees or scope creep.
  • Green Chemical Stewardship: Certified plant-based, non-corrosive, and child-safe disinfectant formulations.
  • Safety & Compliance Programs: Zero-tolerance safety enforcement with full PPE compliance.
  • Digital Supervision: Real-time task timestamping and operational quality tracking.
  • 24/7/365 Mobilization: Daytime porters, evening deep sanitization, weekend crews, and emergency dispatch.
Key Differentiators
  • Hyper-Local Crew Mobilization: Technicians and team leads live within 5 to 10 minutes of assigned facilities, preventing transit delays and absenteeism during severe weather.
  • Dedicated Single-Point Supervision: An assigned field supervisor provides one direct telephone and email line for instant executive accountability.
  • Low-Decibel HEPA Fleets: Commercial vacuum and air purification equipment engineered to run during business hours without disturbing occupants or students.
  • Zero Citation Track Record: 100% inspection pass rate for licensed childcare centers governed by Texas Child Care Regulation health standards.
  • Ironclad Commercial Reliability: We execute exactly what is committed in the scope of work. Every single night.
