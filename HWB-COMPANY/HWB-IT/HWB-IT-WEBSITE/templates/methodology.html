DMAIC Framework

Every janitorial operations project is executed through the 5-phase DMAIC cycle to ensure continuous performance optimization.

  • Measure: Establish baseline performance and waste metrics.
  • Analyze: Identify root causes of cleaning inefficiency.
  • Improve: Set up standardized workflows and GHS-optimized pathing.
  • Control: Use real-time analytics to ensure performance stability.
