Commercial Floor Care & Deep Cleaning FAQs

How often should commercial VCT tile floors be stripped, sealed, and waxed?

High-traffic VCT tile floors in schools, medical clinics, and retail hallways typically need a full strip, rinse, and multi-coat wax application every 6 to 12 months, paired with periodic machine scrubbing and buffing to keep the shine.

How is VCT floor stripping, waxing, and carpet cleaning quoted in Dallas-Fort Worth?

A supervisor walks the area with facility management to measure the floor space, check the condition of the existing wax or carpet, and confirm whether furniture needs to be moved. A clear written quote will be provided before scheduling the crew.

Does HWB offer deep carpet cleaning for offices, schools, and daycares?

Yes. HWB uses commercial hot-water carpet extractors and fast-dry cleaning methods with child-safe, residue-free solutions to lift deep dirt, stains, and heavy foot-traffic wear from office carpets and classroom rugs.
