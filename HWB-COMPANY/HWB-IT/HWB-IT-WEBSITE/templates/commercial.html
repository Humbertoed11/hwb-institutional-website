Commercial Floor Care & Deep Cleaning FAQs

How often should commercial VCT tile floors be stripped, sealed, and waxed?

High-traffic commercial VCT floors in schools, medical clinics, and retail corridors should undergo a full chemical strip, neutralizer rinse, and 4-coat polymer wax application every 6 to 12 months, paired with quarterly top-scrubbing and high-speed burnishing.

How much does commercial VCT floor stripping and waxing cost in Dallas-Fort Worth?

Commercial VCT floor stripping, sealing, and 4-coat waxing in McKinney, Frisco, Plano, and Dallas typically ranges from $0.35 to $0.55 per square foot depending on total square footage and existing wax buildup.

Do you offer commercial hot-water carpet extraction for offices and daycares?

Yes. We use commercial hot-water extraction and low-moisture encapsulation systems with zero-residue, child-safe and pet-safe solutions to remove embedded soil, allergens, and traffic lane wear from commercial carpets and classroom rugs.
