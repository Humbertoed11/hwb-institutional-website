Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

1.0 Introduction

HWB Cleaning Services LLC ("we," "our," or "the Company"), based in Plano, Texas, is committed to maintaining the highest standards of data integrity and security for our B2B clients, prospects, and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information when you interact with our website (crawled and served at hwbcleaning.com), B2B quote calculators, and integrated CRM systems.

2.0 Categories of Data Collected

We only collect data necessary to provide accurate, clinical-tier commercial cleaning proposals. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, corporate email address, and telephone number.
  • B2B Facility Telemetry: Facility type (e.g., medical, office, industrial), square footage, traffic patterns, and requested service frequencies.
  • Communication Logs: Interactive records, email exchanges, and telephony consent metadata.
  • Google API User Data: If you explicitly authorize our integrations, we access designated email headers and GBP parameters to automate backoffice reporting in compliance with security guidelines.

3.0 Business Purposes for Processing

We process collected data exclusively for the following operational B2B purposes:

  • Generating customized commercial cleaning proposals and technical scopes.
  • Verifying facility size and usage level details to calculate accurate operational schedules.
  • Sending business alerts, invoices, and service-specific updates.
  • Initiating follow-up communication regarding your quote request (subject to phone/SMS opt-in consent).

We never sell, rent, or lease your personal data to third parties. We do not use collected data for targeted behavioral advertising, nor do we feed personal data into generic generative AI training engines.

4.0 Telephony & SMS Consent Safeguards (TCPA)

In accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), we do not initiate B2B phone calls or automated text messages (SMS) without obtaining your prior express consent. When you submit your phone number on our quote form:

  • Consent is obtained explicitly through checked opt-in checkboxes on our forms.
  • Mobile opt-in data and text messaging consent will not be sold, shared, or leased to third parties for marketing or other purposes.
  • You may opt-out of telephony or SMS communication at any time by replying "STOP" to any message or emailing our support desk.

5.0 State Privacy Rights (TDPSA & CCPA Compliance)

If you are a resident of Texas, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal data. We extend similar protections to residents of California and other states with comprehensive consumer privacy frameworks:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to obtain a portable copy of that data.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccuracies in your processed personal data.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal data collected from or about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt-out of processing for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or profiling. (We do not sell personal data or engage in profiling).

5.1 How to Submit a Rights Request

To exercise any of the rights listed above, you or your authorized representative may submit a verifiable consumer request to our compliance desk at info@hwbcleaning.com. We will respond to verified requests within 45 days of receipt.

5.2 Right to Appeal

If we deny your consumer privacy request, you have the right to appeal our decision. To initiate an appeal, email your decision appeal directly to info@hwbcleaning.com with the subject line "Privacy Request Appeal". We will respond to your appeal in writing within 60 days, detailing our rationale. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Office of the Texas Attorney General to file a formal complaint.

6.0 Security & Retention Protocols

All personal data is persisted in containerized, encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted in secure cloud architectures. We maintain strict internal role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized access. Data is retained only as long as necessary to satisfy operational business needs or legal requirements (such as corporate accounting and tax mandates).

7.0 Children's Privacy

Our services and website are structured exclusively for commercial, adult business-to-business audiences. We do not knowingly collect or maintain information from individuals under the age of 13 under any circumstances.
