Privacy Policy

Version 2.0.0 • Effective Date: June 1, 2026 • Last Updated: June 1, 2026

1.0 Introduction

HWB Cleaning Services LLC ("the Company"), based in McKinney, Texas, is committed to maintaining the highest standards of data integrity and security for B2B clients, prospects, and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how the Company collects, processes, and protects information when users interact with the website (crawled and served at hwbcleaning.com), B2B quote calculators, and integrated CRM systems.

2.0 Categories of Data Collected

The Company only collects data necessary to provide accurate, hospital-grade commercial cleaning proposals. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, corporate email address, and telephone number.
  • B2B Facility Details: Facility type (e.g., medical, office, industrial), square footage, traffic patterns, and requested service frequencies.
  • Communication Logs: Interactive records, email exchanges, and telephony consent metadata.
  • Google API User Data: If you explicitly authorize our integrations, we access designated email headers and GBP parameters to automate backoffice reporting in compliance with security guidelines.

2.5 Cookies, Tracking Technologies & Google Analytics (GA4)

In compliance with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) and AICPA SOC 2 Privacy Notice Criteria (P1.1), the Company provides transparent disclosure regarding web measurement technologies:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): We utilize Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-8BX5Q7THYR) to collect non-personally identifiable aggregated visitor usage data, including page dwell times, referral channels, browser types, and general geographic location. GA4 utilizes first-party cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish unique visitors without exposing personal identities.
  • Session Cookies: Temporary technical cookies are utilized solely to maintain session state across quote forms and administrative portals. These expire upon browser termination.
  • Internal Traffic Exclusion: To maintain strict data integrity, all internal staff sessions automatically suspend GA4 measurement tags upon authentication.
  • Browser Opt-Out: Users may block or delete cookies at any time via browser settings, or install the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on. Disabling cookies will not impair access to informational service pages.

3.0 Business Purposes for Processing

The Company processes collected data exclusively for the following operational B2B purposes:

  • Generating customized commercial cleaning proposals and technical scopes.
  • Verifying facility size and usage level details to calculate accurate operational schedules.
  • Sending business alerts, invoices, and service-specific updates.
  • Initiating follow-up communication regarding the quote request (subject to phone/SMS opt-in consent).

The Company does not sell, rent, or lease personal data to third parties. The Company does not use collected data for targeted behavioral advertising, nor is personal data fed into generic generative AI training engines.

4.0 Telephony, SMS & Internal Do Not Call (DNC) Policy

In accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and the Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR), the Company maintains strict telephony safeguards and an active **Internal Company Do Not Call (DNC) List** for all B2B outreach:

  • Consent Safeguards: Phone consent is obtained through explicit opt-in checkboxes on website quote forms. Mobile data and SMS consent will never be sold, leased, or shared with third parties.
  • Internal Do Not Call (DNC) Policy: If a business contact or facility manager requests to be placed on our Do Not Call list or requests no further phone contact, our integrated CRM immediately flags the lead record as Do Not Call (DNC).
  • Automated Outreach Suppression: Any phone number or lead record flagged as DNC is automatically suppressed and permanently blocked from future phone calls, SMS messages, email outbox broadcasts, and automated outreach queues.
  • Opt-Out Mechanism: Users and business contacts may request DNC placement at any time by replying "STOP" to any SMS message, verbally informing a representative, or emailing info@hwbcleaning.com with the subject "Do Not Call Request". DNC requests are processed within 24 hours.

4.1 Email Marketing, CAN-SPAM Act & 1-Click Unsubscribe (SOC 2 Privacy P2.1)

Under the federal CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.) and Texas Business & Commerce Code § 321, all commercial email communications originating from HWB Cleaning Services LLC strictly enforce:

  • Clear Identification & Physical Address: Every outbound email includes our verifiable physical corporate address (3342 FM 1827 Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071) and accurate non-deceptive subject headers.
  • 1-Click Automated Unsubscribe Mechanism: Every promotional or B2B email contains a prominent 1-click unsubscribe link. When clicked, the recipient address is immediately flagged as UNSUBSCRIBED, suppressed across all active outreach sequences, and blocked from future campaigns.
  • Instant Suppression Processing: Opt-out requests are executed immediately upon token reception and permanently recorded within our SQL database, exceeding the legal 10-business-day standard. Recipients may also manage email preferences directly at hwbcleaning.com/unsubscribe.

5.0 State Privacy Rights (TDPSA & CCPA Compliance)

If the user is a resident of Texas, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) grants specific rights regarding personal data. The Company extends similar protections to residents of California and other states with comprehensive consumer privacy frameworks:

  • Right to Access: Users have the right to confirm whether the Company processes personal data and to obtain a portable copy of that data.
  • Right to Correct: Users have the right to request correction of inaccuracies in processed personal data.
  • Right to Delete: Users have the right to request deletion of personal data collected.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Users have the right to opt-out of processing for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or profiling. (The Company generally does not sell personal data or engage in profiling).

5.1 How to Submit a Rights Request

To exercise any of the rights listed above, the user or an authorized representative may submit a verifiable consumer request to the compliance desk at info@hwbcleaning.com. The Company will respond to verified requests within 45 days of receipt.

5.2 Right to Appeal

If the Company denies a consumer privacy request, the user has the right to appeal the decision. To initiate an appeal, the user may email the appeal directly to info@hwbcleaning.com with the subject line "Privacy Request Appeal". The Company will respond to the appeal in writing within 60 days, detailing the rationale. If the appeal is denied, the user may contact the Office of the Texas Attorney General to file a formal complaint.

6.0 Security & Retention Protocols

Personal data is typically persisted in containerized, encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted in secure cloud architectures. The Company maintains internal role-based access controls to help prevent unauthorized access. Data is retained for periods deemed necessary to satisfy operational business needs or legal requirements (such as corporate accounting and tax mandates).

7.0 Children's Privacy

Company services and the website are structured exclusively for commercial, adult business-to-business audiences. The Company does not knowingly collect or maintain information from individuals under the age of 13 under any circumstances.
