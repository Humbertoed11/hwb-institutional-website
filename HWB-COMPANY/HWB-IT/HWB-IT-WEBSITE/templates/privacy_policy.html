HWB Cleaning Services LLC Privacy Policy • v2.5.0

Privacy Policy

Version 2.5.0 • Effective Date: June 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1.0 Introduction

HWB Cleaning Services LLC ("the Company"), based in McKinney, Texas, is committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security for B2B clients, prospects, and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how the Company collects, processes, and protects information when users interact with our website (hwbcleaning.com), commercial quote calculators, and customer support systems.

2.0 Categories of Data Collected

The Company only collects data necessary to provide accurate, hospital-grade commercial cleaning proposals. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, corporate email address, and telephone number.
  • B2B Facility Details: Facility type (e.g., medical, office, industrial), square footage, traffic patterns, and requested service frequencies.
  • Communication Logs: Interactive records, email exchanges, and telephony consent preferences.
  • Google API User Data: If you explicitly authorize our integrations, we access designated business profile parameters to support customer reporting in compliance with security guidelines.
  • Applicant & Contractor Onboarding Records (Sensitive Personal Data — TDPSA § 541.102): Only when an employment applicant, W-2 team member, or 1099 subcontractor voluntarily uses our secure onboarding portals and provides affirmative consent, the Company collects government-issued identification numbers (such as a driver's license, state ID, SSN, or EIN), work authorization documents, and payroll tax elections solely to fulfill statutory employment, tax, and background clearance requirements.

2.5 Cookies, Affirmative Consent & Global Privacy Control (GPC)

In compliance with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.055(e)), the California Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA Cal. Penal Code § 631), and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), the Company enforces strict opt-in consent and automatic universal opt-out signal recognition across all optional web analytics tools:

  • Affirmative Opt-In Gate: Optional analytics tools (Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Microsoft Clarity) remain turned off when you first visit our website and only run if you click Accept All on the privacy banner.
  • Automatic Global Privacy Control (Sec-GPC: 1) Recognition: Our website automatically detects and honors browser-based universal opt-out signals, including Global Privacy Control (Sec-GPC: 1 / navigator.globalPrivacyControl) and Do Not Track (DNT: 1). When your browser sends a GPC signal, optional analytics and advertising scripts are automatically suppressed without requiring you to click any banner.
  • Essential Security Cookies Only: When a visitor selects Essential Only, sends a Global Privacy Control signal, or takes no action, only strictly necessary first-party security and session cookies are used.
  • Internal Traffic Exclusion: All internal staff sessions automatically disable analytics tracking upon signing in.
  • Do Not Sell or Share Personal Information: HWB Cleaning Services LLC never sells or shares personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Visitors may reset cookie preferences at any time by clearing site storage or selecting Essential Only on the consent banner.

3.0 Business Purposes for Processing

The Company processes collected data exclusively for the following operational B2B purposes:

  • Generating customized commercial cleaning proposals and technical scopes.
  • Verifying facility size and usage level details to calculate accurate operational schedules.
  • Sending business alerts, invoices, and service-specific updates.
  • Initiating follow-up communication regarding the quote request (subject to phone/SMS opt-in consent).

The Company does not sell, rent, or lease personal data to third parties. The Company does not use collected data for targeted behavioral advertising, nor is personal data fed into generic generative AI training engines.

4.0 Telephony, SMS & Internal Do Not Call (DNC) Policy

In accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and the Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR), the Company maintains strict telephony safeguards and an active **Internal Company Do Not Call (DNC) List** for all B2B outreach:

  • Consent Safeguards: Phone consent is obtained through explicit opt-in checkboxes on website quote forms. Mobile data and SMS consent will never be sold, leased, or shared with third parties.
  • Internal Do Not Call (DNC) Policy: If a business contact or facility manager requests to be placed on our Do Not Call list or requests no further phone contact, our customer records immediately flag the contact as Do Not Call (DNC).
  • Automated Outreach Suppression: Any phone number or contact flagged as DNC is automatically suppressed and permanently blocked from future phone calls, SMS messages, and outreach queues.
  • Opt-Out Mechanism: Users and business contacts may request DNC placement at any time by replying "STOP" to any SMS message, verbally informing a representative, or emailing info@hwbcleaning.com with the subject "Do Not Call Request". DNC requests are processed within 24 hours.

4.1 Email Marketing, CAN-SPAM Act & One-Click Unsubscribe

Under the federal CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. § 7701 et seq.) and Texas Business & Commerce Code § 321.051, all commercial email communications originating from HWB Cleaning Services LLC strictly enforce:

  • Clear Identification & Physical Address: Every outbound email includes our verifiable physical corporate address (3342 FM 1827 Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071) and accurate non-deceptive subject headers.
  • Native One-Click Unsubscribe: Every outreach email includes built-in one-click unsubscribe headers alongside a visible 1-click footer link.
  • Channel-Specific Opt-Out Separation & Permanent Suppression List: Email opt-outs set the contact preference to Unsubscribed and permanently record the address on our Do Not Email list (blocking all future email campaigns while keeping phone and email preferences separate). Explicit replies requesting no phone or all-channel contact immediately apply full Do Not Call (DNC) protection across both phone and email.
  • Texas Commercial Scope & Public-Sector Guard: Outbound B2B email marketing is limited to commercial businesses in Texas, automatically excluding California (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17529.5), Washington State (RCW 19.190), international domains (CASL/GDPR/PECR), and government or educational domains (.gov, .mil, .edu, .tx.us) unless prior written consent is on file. Recipients may manage preferences at hwbcleaning.com/unsubscribe.

5.0 State Privacy Rights (TDPSA & CCPA Compliance)

If the user is a resident of Texas, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) grants specific rights regarding personal data. The Company extends similar protections to residents of California and other states with comprehensive consumer privacy frameworks:

  • Right to Access: Users have the right to confirm whether the Company processes personal data and to obtain a portable copy of that data.
  • Right to Correct: Users have the right to request correction of inaccuracies in processed personal data.
  • Right to Delete: Users have the right to request deletion of personal data collected.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Users have the right to opt-out of processing for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or profiling. (The Company generally does not sell personal data or engage in profiling).

5.1 How to Submit a Rights Request

To exercise any of the rights listed above, the user or an authorized representative may submit a verifiable consumer request to the compliance desk at info@hwbcleaning.com. The Company will respond to verified requests within 45 days of receipt.

5.2 Right to Appeal & Texas Attorney General Complaint Mechanism

If the Company declines to take action on a consumer privacy request, the user has the right to appeal the decision under Texas Business & Commerce Code § 541.053. To initiate an appeal, email info@hwbcleaning.com with the subject line "Privacy Request Appeal". The Company will respond to the appeal in writing within 60 days, detailing the reasons for the decision. If the appeal is denied, you may submit a formal consumer privacy complaint to the Office of the Texas Attorney General through its online complaint mechanism at https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint.

6.0 Security & Retention Protocols

Personal information is stored in encrypted cloud databases protected by strict role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized access. Data is retained only as long as needed to fulfill business operations or legal requirements (such as accounting and tax records).

7.0 Children's Privacy

Company services and the website are structured exclusively for commercial, adult business-to-business audiences. The Company does not knowingly collect or maintain information from individuals under the age of 13 under any circumstances.
