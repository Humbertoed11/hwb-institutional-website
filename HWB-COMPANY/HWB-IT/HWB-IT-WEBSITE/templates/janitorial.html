Daily Office Cleaning & Janitorial FAQs

How is the cleaning checklist decided for each janitorial visit?

HWB does not use a one-size-fits-all checklist because every building has different priorities. A supervisor meets on-site at the facility for a walkthrough to review the offices, restrooms, breakrooms, floors, and traffic patterns. From that walkthrough, a custom room-by-room cleaning plan is built for the exact work the building requires.

What is the difference between a Day Porter and a Nightly Janitorial Crew?

A Day Porter works during regular business hours to keep busy lobbies, restrooms, coffee stations, and conference rooms clean and restocked throughout the day, while a Nightly Janitorial Crew handles full-building vacuuming, mopping, trash removal, and restroom disinfection after hours.

How is recurring janitorial service priced in McKinney, Frisco, Plano, and Dallas?

Monthly pricing is built from an on-site walkthrough based on cleanable square footage, weekly service frequency, the number of restrooms and breakrooms, and flooring types. Following the visit, a clear written quote will be provided.
