Daily Office Cleaning & Janitorial FAQs

What is included in HWB's nightly commercial office janitorial service?

Our standard nightly janitorial scope includes trash and recycling removal with liner replacement, 4-color microfiber disinfection of desks, switches, and door handles, clinical restroom sanitization and restocking, breakroom and sink cleaning, HEPA backpack vacuuming of carpets, and damp mopping of hard floors.

What is the difference between a Day Porter and a Nightly Janitorial Crew?

A Day Porter works during active business hours to keep high-traffic lobbies, restrooms, coffee bars, and conference rooms spotless and stocked throughout the day, whereas a Nightly Janitorial Crew performs full-building vacuuming, mopping, trash removal, and deep restroom disinfection after hours.

How much does recurring janitorial service cost in McKinney, Frisco, Plano, and Dallas?

Recurring commercial janitorial service in North Texas typically ranges from $0.08 to $0.16 per square foot per month depending on cleaning frequency (1x to 7x per week), employee headcount, and restroom count.
