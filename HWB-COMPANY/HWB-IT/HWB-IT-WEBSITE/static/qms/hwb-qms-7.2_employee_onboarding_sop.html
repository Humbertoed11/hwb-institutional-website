Standard Operating Procedure: Employee Onboarding & Sensitive PII Vault Protocol

1.0 Purpose

This procedure establishes the clinical standard for recruiting, screening, deduplicating, onboarding, and securing the personal data of all workforce personnel at HWB Cleaning Services LLC. It guarantees that technicians and trade partners are verified, trained, badged, and equipped from day one without duplicate database records, while protecting their sensitive identification and banking data under Texas Bus. & Com. Code § 521.053, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 Type II regulations.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all W-2 commercial cleaning technicians, 1099 subcontractor cleaning crews, field supervisors, and administrative personnel managed within the SigmaFidelity™ Operations Hub.

3.0 Public Workforce Intake Portal (/work-with-us) & Smart Deduplication

Public recruitment is routed through the central recruitment portal (https://hwbcleaning.com/work-with-us):

  1. Two-Track Intake: Candidates select either Track 1 (W-2 Commercial Cleaning Technician) or Track 2 (1099 Subcontractor Partner Crew). The portal supports direct URL deep-linking (?track=subcontractor).
  2. Phone Number Masking (PROC-002): All applicant telephone numbers must be validated and automatically masked as (###)-###-#### on every keystroke. Arbitrary characters, incomplete 10-digit strings, and unformatted entries are blocked before submission.
  3. Zero Plaintext Collection at Intake: Public application forms strictly prohibit requesting Social Security Numbers, ITINs, or bank account numbers during initial intake. Sensitive PII is collected only after a formal conditional offer is extended.
  4. Error Handling: Native browser alert() popups are eradicated. All input failures produce styled, accessible error banners (#tech-error-box and #sub-error-box) with autofocus on the first invalid field.
  5. 5-Pillar Enterprise Deduplication & Smart Merge (BUG-122):
    • When a W-2 applicant submits POST /api/v1/workforce/apply or a 1099 subcontractor submits POST /api/v1/workforce/subcontractor, the gateway checks existing records by 10-digit phone number, lowercase email address, and normalized company name.
    • If an existing record is found, the system preserves the original record ID and registration date, merges any updated trade specialties, crew size, role/shift preferences, or contact details, and returns status: "already_registered".
    • The frontend renders the privacy-safe blue status card (#tech-already-registered-message or #sub-already-registered-message) showing the registrant's Reference ID (APP-#00XX or SUB-#00XX) and original registration date, while sending an updated confirmation receipt to their email inbox and suppressing duplicate internal staff alerts.

4.0 Candidate Pool (ATS) & Onboarding Workflow

  1. Intake Ingestion: Completed applications populate the Candidate Pool (ATS) subtab in the Backoffice Operations Hub (/admin/operations?view=workforce&subtab=candidates), while 1099 trade partners populate the Subcontractors registry (10 verified partners synchronized across Azure and Local PostgreSQL 16.15).
  2. Vetting & Background Clearance: Before facility deployment, candidates undergo DPS criminal history checks, I-9 employment verification, and reference validation. DPS clearance dates and I-9 completion dates are logged in the employee profile.
  3. Promotion to Personnel Roster & Badge Preservation: Clicking the "+ Onboard" action button opens the hiring modal (POST /api/v1/hr/onboard). The backend checks "Employees" by applicant_id, 10-digit phone, and email before inserting. First-time hires receive a unique employee badge (HWB-EMP-####) and Hired status; repeat onboarding calls update the existing employee's pay rate, role, and facility assignment without creating a duplicate employee row.
  4. SigmaAcademy™ Training Assignment Deduplication: Assigning safety courses or packages via POST /api/v1/academy/assign checks "AcademyEnrollments" by course_id and worker identifier, reusing the existing enrollment and magic_token rather than generating duplicate training rows.
  5. 4-Stage Self-Service 1099 & W-2 Onboarding Portals, AI Document Auto-Extraction & Tamper-Evident Receipts (BUG-139–BUG-142 / Migrations 047–050):
    • 1099 Subcontractor Self-Service Portal (/onboard/subcontractor/<token>): Issued automatically (in Auto-Approve mode) or upon Backoffice review (in Manual Review Gate mode via SystemSettings.subcontractor_onboarding_mode). Subcontractors upload IRS Form W-9 and ACORD 25 Certificate of Insurance (auto-extracted via core/services/subcontractor_doc_extractor.py into PostgreSQL "SubcontractorDocuments" as AES-256 encrypted_payload BYTEA + SHA-256 hash) and electronically sign the Master Subcontractor Agreement (MSA) and Texas DWC Form-83 waiver, generating locked ReportLab PDF certificates.
    • W-2 Employee Self-Service HR Portal (/onboard/employee/<token>): Hired technicians upload IRS Form W-4, DHS Form I-9, Government Photo ID, and a Voided Check / Direct Deposit slip (auto-extracted via core/services/employee_doc_extractor.py into PostgreSQL "EmployeeDocuments" as AES-256 encrypted_payload BYTEA + SHA-256 hash) and electronically sign their ISO 9001 Clause 7.2 Job Description & Handbook Acknowledgment, generating a locked ReportLab PDF certificate.
    • 5-Layer Universal Input Masking & Tamper-Evident Transaction Receipt Cards: Global client-side masking engine (/static/js/sigma_input_masks.js) enforces live formatting across all EIN (XX-XXXXXXX, maxlength="10"), SSN/ITIN (XXX-XX-XXXX, maxlength="11"), Phone ((###)-###-####, maxlength="14"), ZIP (#####-####, maxlength="10"), 9-digit ABA Routing (maxlength="9"), and 2-letter State (maxlength="2") fields (with dynamic EIN/SSN switching on 1099 tax classification changes). Upon form submission or page revisit, the active form collapses and displays a Tamper-Evident Transaction Receipt Card (#onboarding-receipt-card / #w2-receipt-card) showing the Receipt ID (HWB-1099-000XX-YYYYMMDD / HWB-W2-000XX-YYYYMMDD), SHA-256 signature verification, downloadable PDF certificates, and a controlled "Re-Open Form" toggle.

5.0 Form W-4, Banking & The Sensitive PII Vault Protocol (SEC-001)

HWB enforces zero plaintext storage for all government identifiers and banking credentials:

Data Field Input & Display Standard Database Storage Standard Access & Reveal Protocol
Social Security Number (SSN) / ITIN Password-bulleted entry with live ###-##-#### keystroke formatting. Default display: ***-**-####. AES-256 Fernet column encryption (ssn_encrypted) with dedicated last-four search index (ssn_last_four). Restricted to Executive, Admin, and Operations roles. Clicking "Reveal" displays decrypted digits with an automated 30-second countdown timer. Auto-remasks immediately upon timeout or modal dismissal.
Direct Deposit Checking / Savings Account Default display masked as ••••••••####. AES-256 Fernet column encryption (direct_deposit_account_encrypted) and last-four index. Protected from general staff view. Raw account numbers are never transmitted across standard GET queries or payroll CSV exports.
Bank Routing Number (ABA) 9-digit validated routing format. Validated against Federal Reserve routing directory. Visible to authorized payroll managers for ACH NACHA direct deposit file generation.

6.0 Immutable Audit Logging & Access Controls

  1. Audit Logging: Every unmasking of an employee's Social Security Number is logged to the "GlobalActivities" table with accessor user ID, username, employee ID, timestamp, and client IP address.
  2. Unauthorized Access Quarantine: If an unauthorized user (such as Field Sales) attempts to access sensitive identification endpoints (POST /api/v1/hr/employees/<id>/reveal-ssn), the gateway rejects the request with HTTP 403 Forbidden and automatically generates a SECURITY_VIOLATION event in the security ledger.
  3. Automated Security Lockout Protocol: When an employee's status changes to Terminated or Fired, the system automatically sets their badge status to Revoked, records the termination date, and marks linked candidate records as terminated.

7.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Employee file contains signed Form W-4, verified I-9, and state ID expiration date.
  • Social Security Number displays exclusively as ***-**-#### upon loading the employee profile.
  • Direct deposit account displays exclusively as ••••••••####.
  • Clicking "Reveal" records a verifiable entry in "GlobalActivities".
  • Repeat submissions to /api/v1/workforce/apply, /api/v1/workforce/subcontractor, /api/v1/hr/onboard, and /api/v1/academy/assign return status: "already_registered" and merge updates with zero duplicate rows.
