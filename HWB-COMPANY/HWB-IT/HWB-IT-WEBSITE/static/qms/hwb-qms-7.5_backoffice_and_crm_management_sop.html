Standard Operating Procedure: Backoffice Operations & CRM Management

1.0 Purpose

This procedure defines the operational standards for navigating, managing, and maintaining the central SigmaFidelity™ Operations Hub (/admin/operations), our unified PostgreSQL relational database, and client account lifecycle. It ensures real-time operational visibility, flawless field dispatching, strict data confidentiality, and complete alignment between executive leadership and field cleaning crews.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all administrative operators, dispatch supervisors, executive managers, and partner account liaisons managing commercial accounts, leads, work orders, and personnel rosters for HWB Cleaning Services LLC.

3.0 System Architecture & Database Foundation

The system operates on an immutable, enterprise-grade architecture:

  • Database Engine: PostgreSQL 13+ (Local container: hwb_postgres_dev; Production: Azure Database for PostgreSQL Flexible Server). All legacy SQLite files are officially retired.
  • Core Relational Tables:
    • "Customers": Commercial client facilities, square footage, assigned technicians, and contract values.
    • "Leads": Statewide commercial Texas prospects, GIS coordinates, building square footage, and contact details.
    • "Employees": Active W-2 technicians, payroll rates, facility assignments, and AES-256 encrypted PII vault columns.
    • "JobApplicants": Candidate pool (ATS) records, interview statuses, and application tracks.
    • "WorkOrders": Scheduled service visits, crew notes, client notes, shift times, and security ingress access codes.
    • "GlobalActivities": Immutable SOC 2 / ISO 27001 audit ledger recording all system events, conversions, and sensitive data accesses.

4.0 The Operations Hub Modular Views (/admin/operations)

The centralized Operations Hub is structured into 5 dedicated operational modules:

  1. Workforce Hub (?view=workforce):
    • Active Technicians: Displays active W-2 commercial cleaning technicians, assigned customer facility, hourly rate, weekly hours, and compliance document counts.
    • Candidate Pool (ATS): Real-time intake from /work-with-us. Provides one-click + Onboard candidate promotion to W-2 or 1099 personnel.
    • Subcontractor Partners: 1099 commercial cleaning partner companies, primary contacts, and verified insurance certifications.
    • Payroll & Labor Tracking: Weekly allocated labor hours, bi-weekly payroll projections, and ADP/QuickBooks CSV export.
  2. Portfolio Accounts (?view=accounts): Tracks active client facilities (e.g., Collin College Frisco Campus, Bosanna LLC, NTTA). Manages building specifications, square footage, primary billing contacts, service frequencies, and keyholder maps.
  3. Commercial Leads (?view=leads): Manages our commercial Texas lead pipeline across Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, Houston, and San Antonio. Tracks lead qualification, outreach cadence, walkthrough scheduling, and one-click account conversion.
  4. Dispatch & Work Orders (?view=monitor): Real-time operations board tracking nightly shifts, technician assignments, gate access codes, ingress instructions, and supervisor inspection pass rates.
  5. Safety & EHSQ (?view=safety): Daily Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) records, OSHA 300 logs, incident reports, and EPA-registered chemical Safety Data Sheets (SDS).

5.0 Employee Master Dossier & PII Vault Security (SEC-001)

  1. Zero Plaintext Storage: Social Security Numbers, ITINs, and direct deposit checking/savings accounts are encrypted at the database layer using AES-256 Fernet ciphers.
  2. Default Masked Display: Opening the employee modal displays Social Security Numbers as ***-**-#### and bank accounts as ••••••••####.
  3. Audited 30-Second Timed Reveal: Authorized administrators (Executive, Admin, Operations) may click "Reveal" to inspect decrypted identification. The gateway logs the accessor username and IP to GlobalActivities, and the screen automatically locks and re-masks after 30 seconds.
  4. Automated Security Lockout Protocol: Terminated or Fired personnel automatically have badge credentials revoked, linked ATS records marked terminated, and facility access permissions terminated.

6.0 Prime Contractor Cockpit Integration (/portal/bosanna/cockpit)

  1. Decoupled Partner Cockpit: Prime contractor partners (e.g., Bosanna LLC) manage on-site facility personnel, deployment approvals, and cleaning checklists through their dedicated portal.
  2. Status Synchronization: Changes made in the partner cockpit synchronize immediately with the central HWB database, maintaining absolute data consistency without double entry.

7.0 Verification & Quality Control

  • All lead conversions and employee onboardings must be verifiable in the GlobalActivities feed.
  • Subtab transitions in the Workforce view must synchronize with URL parameters without page reloads.
  • Sortable drag-and-drop table headers must persist user column orders across sessions.
  • Zero synthetic data: all records must correspond to physical commercial properties or verified human personnel.
