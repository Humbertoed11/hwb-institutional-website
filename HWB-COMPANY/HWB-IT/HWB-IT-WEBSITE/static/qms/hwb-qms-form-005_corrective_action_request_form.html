Corrective Action Request Form

1.0 Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document and manage the resolution of nonconformities identified within the QMS, ensuring that root causes are addressed and preventive actions are implemented.

2.0 Scope

This form is used for all corrective actions resulting from audits, complaints, or process failures.

3.0 Prerequisites

  • Identification of a nonconformity.
  • Authorization to initiate a Corrective Action Request (CAR).

4.0 Procedure (Form Layout)

    • [ ] Other: _____

  • Root Cause(s) Identified: __________ ______________

4.4 Corrective Action Plan (Preventive Action)

  • Action(s) to Prevent Recurrence: _________ ______________
  • Responsible Person: __ Target Completion Date: __

4.5 Implementation and Verification

  • Verification of Effectiveness: _____________
  • Verified By: __ Verification Date: __

4.6 Closure

  • Final Approval (Sign/Date): _______

5.0 Verification

The effectiveness of the corrective action is verified by the QMS manager or designated auditor before closure.

6.0 Notes and Cautions

  • Ensure the root cause analysis goes beyond the surface symptom (use "5 Whys" if necessary).
  • Records must be retained according to section 7.5 of the Quality Manual.
