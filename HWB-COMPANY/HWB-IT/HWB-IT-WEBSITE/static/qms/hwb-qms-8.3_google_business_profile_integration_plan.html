4.0 Standard Governance & Poka-Yoke Safeguards

  1. Category Integrity (No School/Daycare Category Lock): The primary category must remain Commercial cleaning service (with secondary categories Janitorial service, Carpet cleaning service, Window cleaning service, and Pressure washing service). Never assign Day care center or School as a GBP category—Google automatically disables customer reviews on educational/childcare categories.
  2. Separation from Legacy Retail Merchant Center: HWB Cleaning Services LLC is a B2B commercial facility service provider and must remain strictly decoupled from legacy retail Google Merchant Center accounts (such as legacy account 8126538).
  3. Bidirectional Website Schema Handshake: Every public page via templates/base.html and standalone B2B RFP pages (prequal.html, capability_statement.html, academy_catalog.html) must declare "hasMap": "https://www.google.com/maps?cid=4883166476987591300" and include the CID URL, Review Shortlink, and Knowledge Graph ID in "sameAs".
  4. 1-Click Review Velocity Loop: The global website footer, post-quote confirmation card (quote_success.html), and all 3 executive email signatures (executive_signature.html) must link directly to https://g.page/r/CYTq8OcJfsRDEBM/review.
