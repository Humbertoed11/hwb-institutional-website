Standard Operating Procedure: Sales Process SOP

1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the institutional sales intake, qualification, territory assignment, walkthrough scheduling, proposal calibration, and post-quote Google Review loop for commercial janitorial and facility cleaning prospects across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. It guarantees zero top-of-funnel conversion friction, strict sales territory protection, rapid executive response, and empirical pricing accuracy.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all inbound digital quote inquiries (/get-quote), Google Maps Local Pack leads (CID: 4883166476987591300), Microsoft Bookings walkthrough appointments (FacilityWalkthroughquote@NETORGFT3163094.onmicrosoft.com), phone prospects (+1-214-586-0257), Calling Workstation outreach (/sales-desk), Commercial Construction Bids (?view=construction_bids), and CRM account conversions across the commercial cleaning pipeline.

3.0 Universal Sales Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Contact-First Architecture: Prospect contact credentials (Name and Email or Phone) must be secured first within 10 seconds of user interaction before requesting complex architectural details.
  2. Mobile Express Optimization: On mobile screens (≤ 768px), form fields must collapse to the essential 4-field intake, with automated removal of HTML required attributes to prevent client-side validation lockups.
  3. Empirical Insurance Representation: All public representations of commercial coverage must strictly state our verified $2,000,000 commercial liability policy (ACORD 25 certificate). Marketing figures must never cite unverified estimates.
  4. 100% CRM & Conversion Attribution Traceability: Every inbound quote submission must immediately persist to PostgreSQL, trigger automated multi-channel alert dispatch (Telegram Operations Gateway and Email), and fire CIPA/CPRA consent-gated conversion attribution to GA4 (G-8BX5Q7THYR) and Google Ads (862-242-8739 / AW-8622428739).
  5. Strict Sales Territory Isolation: Accounts assigned the Sales role are strictly isolated to records explicitly assigned to their user ID across Commercial Leads (owner_id = current_user.id), Calling Workstation (/sales-desk), Active Clients (assigned_rep_id = current_user.id), and Commercial Construction Bids (assigned_rep_id = current_user.id).

4.0 The Two-Phase Sales Sequence & Local Prominence Loop

4.1 Phase 1: Contact-First 10-Second Intake & HQ Map Handshake

  • Desktop Intake: Captures Contact Name, Work Email, Phone (optional for email-preferred clients), Facility Type, and Service Scope alongside an interactive Google Maps embed of McKinney HQ (3342 FM 1827 Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071, CID: 4883166476987591300).
  • Mobile Express Mode: Automatically active on viewports ≤ 768px. Dynamically hides non-essential inputs (City/Zip, Cleaning Frequency, Company Name) and applies intelligent backend defaults (lead_source = 'Website Quote Form (Mobile Express)').
  • Bot Defense Shield: Inbound submissions pass through the honeypot trap (hp_organization_url) and submission timing filter (> 3 seconds) to drop spam instantly.

4.2 Phase 2: Post-Submission Pricing Calibration & 1-Click Google Review Loop

Upon initial submission, the prospect is immediately transitioned to the confirmation screen (quote_success.html) displaying the 10-Second Pricing Calibration card and conversion telemetry:

  • Dual Conversion Telemetry: Fires generate_lead ($150.00 USD) on initial load and pricing_calibrated ($250.00 USD) on calibration submission to both GA4 (G-8BX5Q7THYR) and Google Ads (AW-8622428739).
  • 1-Touch City & Frequency Chips: Immediate selection chips for Plano, Dallas, Frisco, Fort Worth, Irving, and neighboring North Texas hubs, plus 1x Weekly, 3x Weekly, 5x Weekly, or One-Time Deep Clean schedules updating PostgreSQL via AJAX (POST /update-quote-details).
  • 1-Click Google Review & Executive Signature Handshake: Both the confirmation card footer on quote_success.html and all 3 executive email signatures in executive_signature.html feature a "Verified on Google Maps • Leave a Review" badge linking directly to https://g.page/r/CYTq8OcJfsRDEBM/review.

4.4 Strict Sales Role Territory Isolation & Calling Workstation (BUG-126–BUG-130 & Migration 042)

  • Commercial Leads & Calling Workstation Isolation (owner_id = current_user.id): Representatives with the Sales role only view and dial leads explicitly assigned to their user ID on both the Operations Leads view (?view=leads) and the Calling Workstation (/sales-desk). Unassigned master leads remain invisible until assigned by an Executive, Admin, or Manager, or created directly by the sales representative.
  • Dynamic Calling Script Personalization ({Caller} Tag): All calling scripts in the database (user_scripts) use the dynamic {Caller} placeholder, which automatically resolves at runtime to the logged-in representative's first name while allowing each representative to save custom personal script variations.
  • Active Client Account Isolation (assigned_rep_id = current_user.id): On the Clients tab (?view=accounts), Sales role users only see active client facilities assigned to their territory or created by them.
  • Commercial Construction Bid Ownership (assigned_rep_id = current_user.id, Option A): On the Commercial Construction Bidding pipeline (?view=construction_bids), Sales role users only view and manage construction bids assigned to their user ID via the inline Assigned Rep dropdown (POST /api/v1/construction-bids/<bid_id>/assign-rep), and can trigger live GC contact enrichment via the hardened Enrich button.

5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Quality Check)

  • All quote submissions trigger instant Telegram notification to the operations team within 1.5 seconds.
  • Lead records are verified in the PostgreSQL database with proper source tagging and Google Ads AW-8622428739 conversion attribution.
  • Mobile form submissions complete without browser validation errors on both iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
  • Post-submission pricing calibration updates reflect accurately in the Lead detail pane in the backoffice.
  • All review badges and signature links point to the verified 1-click Google Review modal (https://g.page/r/CYTq8OcJfsRDEBM/review).
  • Sales role accounts strictly view only assigned rows across Leads, Calling Desk, Clients, and Commercial Construction Bids with zero unassigned leakage.
