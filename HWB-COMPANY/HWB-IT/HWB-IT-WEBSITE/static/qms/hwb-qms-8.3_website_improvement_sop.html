Standard Operating Procedure: Website Improvement, WCAG 2.2 AA & Telemetry Optimization SOP

1.0 Purpose

This standard operating procedure defines the engineering, accessibility, legal privacy, and conversion optimization standards for https://www.hwbcleaning.com. It ensures 100% WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance across all 87 HTML templates, CIPA § 631 & CPRA affirmative opt-in consent gating for analytics and advertising tags, and seamless bidirectional integration with Google Maps and Microsoft Bookings.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all public marketing templates, 11 DFW city corridor landing pages, standalone B2B RFP documents (/prequal, /capability-statement, /academy), the Two-Phase Contact-First quote funnel (/get-quote and quote_success.html), and all internal backoffice operational templates.

3.0 Standard Procedure & Architectural Pillars

  1. WCAG 2.2 AA Zero-Violation Template Standard: Every HTML template in templates/ must include a keyboard-accessible skip link (.sigma-skip-link targeting #main-content), explicit aria-label or visible text on all buttons and links, and 4.5:1 minimum color contrast. All changes must pass compliance_code_linter.py with 0 violations (100% Grade A+).
  2. CIPA § 631 & CPRA Affirmative Consent Telemetry Gate: Third-party analytics and ad conversion scripts—specifically Google Analytics 4 (G-8BX5Q7THYR), Google Ads (AW-8622428739 / Account 862-242-8739), and Microsoft Clarity (q8x3j1k9wm)—must remain inert inside <script type="text/plain" data-consent-category="analytics"> tags until the visitor clicks Accept All on the cookie consent banner, triggering triggerCompliantTelemetry(). Authenticated backoffice and login routes are 100% air-gapped from third-party heatmaps.
  3. Interactive Google Maps & CID Handshake: The /get-quote page embeds a responsive, lazy-loaded Google Maps iframe pinned to McKinney HQ (3342 FM 1827 Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071) alongside direct CID links (https://www.google.com/maps?cid=4883166476987591300).
  4. Automated Google Review & Conversion Attribution Loop:
    • Quote submissions on quote_success.html dispatch both GA4 generate_lead ($150.00) and Google Ads conversion (send_to: AW-8622428739) events, plus pricing_calibrated ($250.00) upon 10-second facility calibration.
    • The global footer (templates/base.html), post-quote confirmation badge (templates/quote_success.html), and all 3 executive email signatures (templates/executive_signature.html) provide 1-click access to HWB's Google Review modal (https://g.page/r/CYTq8OcJfsRDEBM/review).
Mandatory Compliance Note

Never place raw snapshots or backup HTML files in the project root directory; all historical snapshots must be archived in .backups/ to keep compliance linters clean.
