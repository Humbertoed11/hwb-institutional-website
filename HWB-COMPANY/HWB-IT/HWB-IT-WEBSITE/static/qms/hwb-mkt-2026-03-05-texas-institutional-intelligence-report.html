1.0 Executive Summary

This report details high-fidelity, free public data assets for the Texas institutional market. We have identified specific portals for Universities, K-12 Districts, and Municipal structures that align with the SigmaFidelity™ market capture strategy.

2.0 Texas Institutional Data Assets

2.1 Public Universities & Colleges (THECB)

  • Asset: Master List of Higher Education Institutions.
  • Fidelity: Includes all public 4-year universities, community colleges, and medical institutes.
  • Source: THECB Institutional Directory
  • Strategic Value: Massive facility footprints; multi-building complex opportunities.

2.2 K-12 School Districts & Campuses (TEA / AskTED)

  • Asset: Complete Texas Education Directory (CSV).
  • Fidelity: 100% verified campus addresses and administrative director names.
  • Source: TEA AskTED Reports
  • Strategic Value: High-occupancy facilities requiring recurring, compliance-heavy cleaning.

2.3 Municipal & Government Structures (TxGIO)

  • Asset: StratMap Statewide Address Points.
  • Fidelity: Granular geographic data for public buildings (Courthouses, Police Stations, City Halls).
  • Source: TxGIO DataHub
  • Strategic Value: Stable, long-term government contracts.

3.0 Integrated Data Parameters (Texas Standard)

All Texas institutional datasets will be extracted with the following unified fields: 1. Operation_Name (Entity Name) 2. Location_Address (Physical Site) 3. Phone_Number 4. County 5. Zipcode 6. Director_Name (Primary Decision Maker) 7. Total_Capacity (or Building SQF) 8. Email_Address 9. City / State

4.0 Strategic Findings

  • Zero-Cost Access: All primary sources identified are maintained by state agencies and provide free bulk downloads.
  • Market Concentration: DFW and Houston remain the primary "Hubs" for institutional facility growth.
  • Next Action: Initiate the HWB-QMS-7.5 Data Acquisition SOP to govern the download and injection of these Texas assets.
