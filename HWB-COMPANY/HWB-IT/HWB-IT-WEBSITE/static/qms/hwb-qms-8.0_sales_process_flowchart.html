Standard Operating Procedure: Sales Process Flowchart

1.0 Purpose

This document provides a visual representation of the Commercial Sales & Territory-Isolated Pipeline Process at HWB Cleaning Services LLC, covering lead ingestion, territory assignment (`assigned_rep_id`), calling workstation outreach, Microsoft Bookings walkthroughs, Yamamoto Moto takeoff calibration, and customer promotion.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all sales activities within the Commercial Leads, Calling Workstation (`/sales-desk`), Commercial Bidding, and Customer Accounts modules.

3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Strict Territory Isolation: Sales profiles only view leads, accounts, and construction bids explicitly assigned to their `assigned_rep_id`.
  2. 35% Margin Floor: Every commercial proposal and construction bid is calibrated by Yamamoto Moto to enforce a minimum 35% gross margin.
  3. Physical Truth: Quote intake executes via `templates/quote_form.html` and `templates/index.html` with Smart Merge deduplication.
