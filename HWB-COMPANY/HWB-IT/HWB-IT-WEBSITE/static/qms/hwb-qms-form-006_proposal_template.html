Standard Operating Procedure: Sales Proposal Template

1.0 Purpose

This template provides a standardized, high-fidelity framework for submitting bids and proposals. It is designed to emphasize HWB’s strategic differentiators: Information Fidelity, Resource Optimization, and ISO 9001 Alignment.

2.0 Scope

Applies to all formal business development bids, RFPs, and service proposals generated by the Sales & Marketing department.

3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)

  1. Guidance First: If an RFP requirement is ambiguous, ASK the CEO before drafting a response.
  2. Tier 6 Telemetry: Every sent proposal must be logged in the SigmaInteractionLog.
  3. Physical Truth: Use absolute server paths for the "Proof of Performance" data attached to proposals.

4.0 Procedure

  1. Open a new document using the HWB-QMS-FORM-007 Official Letterhead.
  2. Populate the bracketed information [ ] with client-specific data.
  3. Ensure the "Six Critical Areas" are tailored to the specific solicitation (RFP).
  4. Submit as a high-quality PDF via the internal Transmission Hub.

5.0 Template Content

[RECIPIENT NAME] [RECIPIENT TITLE] [RECIPIENT ADDRESS]

RE: Proposal for Professional Facility Management & Janitorial Services - [Solicitation/RFP #]

Dear [Contact Name],

HWB Cleaning Services LLC would like to thank you for the opportunity to submit our proposal in response to [Solicitation Name/Number]. We are eager to bring our specialized SigmaFidelity™ standards to [Location/Facility Name].

HWB Cleaning Services LLC, headquartered in Plano, Texas, is a premier provider of janitorial and integrated facility management services for the DFW metroplex. Built on over 20 years of expertise and a foundational commitment to ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System standards, we have established ourselves as the region's trusted provider for high-performance environments.

Our approach differs from traditional janitorial models. While others offer a "Commodity Clean," HWB provides Information Fidelity. Our expertise in managing specialized sectors—including education, healthcare, and high-volume logistics—allows us to protect your facility’s health while eliminating the strategic waste of "Payroll Mopping."

Sincerely,

[SENDER NAME] [SENDER TITLE] HWB Cleaning Services LLC

6.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Client name and address are 100% accurate.
  • Proposal is saved as a PDF (not .docx).
  • Document matches the clinical "Identity Card" look.
