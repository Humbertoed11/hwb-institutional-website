1. Purpose & Operational Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the technical and off-site citation standards required so that HWB Cleaning Services LLC (headquartered at 3342 FM 1827, Ste 8d, McKinney, TX 75071) is consistently indexed, cited, and recommended by AI search and answer engines—including OpenAI ChatGPT Search (OAI-SearchBot / ChatGPT-User), Perplexity (PerplexityBot), Anthropic Claude (ClaudeBot), Google AI Overviews (Google-Extended), and Microsoft Bing (bingbot / IndexNow)—across all 11 North Texas commercial corridors.

3. Mandatory On-Site GEO Technical Controls (8 Pillars)

  1. Machine-Readable AI Manifests (/llms.txt & /llms-full.txt): The application serves structured Markdown fact sheets at the root domain detailing HWB's McKinney headquarters, phone number, NAICS 561720 and CSI 01 74 23 codes, square-footage pricing benchmarks, 11 DFW cities and ZIP codes, and OSHA 0.00 TRIR safety record.
  2. AI Crawler robots.txt Permissions: /static/robots.txt explicitly permits OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, and bingbot across all public service, city, and safety pages (including /compliance and /ehsq).
  3. 1:1 Visible-to-JSON-LD FAQ Parity: Every FAQPage JSON-LD block on /, /services/*, and /locations/* must match visible HTML <details> FAQ accordions rendered on the same page. Global invisible duplicate FAQPage markup in base.html is prohibited.
  4. High-Information-Gain City & Service Pages: All 11 city pages (McKinney, Frisco, Plano, Dallas, Allen, Richardson, Addison, Irving, Fort Worth, Arlington, Waxahachie) must display unique commercial corridors, ZIP codes, and city-specific FAQs.
  5. Instant IndexNow Submission & PostgreSQL AI Bot Telemetry: All 32 canonical URLs in sitemap.xml are pushed via scripts/push_indexnow.py, and every incoming AI bot request is logged in PostgreSQL 16.15 "SecurityAuditLogs" under AI_CRAWLER_TELEMETRY.
