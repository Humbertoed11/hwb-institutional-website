Executive Summary: Client Needs, Goals & Proposed Plan

🏢 The Client's Current Situation & Needs

Sundance Square and downtown property management oversee 11 landmark commercial buildings in Fort Worth. Every business day, hundreds of office tenants, retail customers, and corporate guests walk through these ground-floor lobbies.

Heavy daily foot traffic, tracked-in street grit, and rain have dulled the natural stone, tile, and terrazzo surfaces. Dirt has settled into corners and grout lines, while spills and moving equipment have left scuffs and spots. The management team needs these entrance lobbies to look welcoming, clean, and well-maintained every single morning.

🎯 Client Goals & Objectives

  • Strip Away the Dirt: Deep-clean floors down to the bare surface so managers can clearly see the real floor condition and any existing damage.
  • Protect Against Future Stains: Seal stone and grout to prevent coffee, sodas, and grease from soaking in and ruining floors.
  • Set Up a Reliable Routine: Establish a predictable cleaning schedule (quarterly or semi-annually) that keeps floors shining and stays within budget.
  • Zero Tenant Disruption: Perform all work quietly and safely without interrupting daytime business.

🤝 What HWB Cleaning Services Proposes

HWB Cleaning Services LLC proposes a straightforward, 3-step floor care plan tailored specifically for the 11 downtown lobbies (11,153 total cleanable square feet):

1. Deep Clean Reset (Phase 1)
Heavy rotary scrubbing and extraction to pull out ground-in grime and old build-up, giving managers a clean baseline to review floor condition.
2. Stain Protection (Phase 2)
Application of commercial penetrating sealer (BulletProof®) into pores and grout lines to lock out moisture and liquid stains.
3. Ongoing Care (Phase 3)
Scheduled quarterly or semi-annual maintenance passes to wash and protect the floors year-round without diamond grinding.
⏰ 100% Flexible Shift Scheduling: While standard operations run on dedicated night shifts (post-7:00 PM) dispatched from Arlington, TX to ensure zero daytime disruption, all shift hours and dates are completely flexible. If building management prefers earlier evening starts, weekend execution, split-building passes, or adjustments around specific tenant events and security desk schedules, HWB will customize the dispatch timing to match your exact operational requirements at no additional charge.

1.0 Service Roadmap: 3-Phase Commercial Floor Care Plan

HWB Cleaning Services LLC proposes a structured commercial hard-floor cleaning, conditioning, and protection program tailored specifically for the 11 high-profile ground-floor commercial lobbies (11,153 Cleanable Square Feet) within the Sundance Square and Downtown Fort Worth corridor. All operations are executed by a dedicated crew of 2 Specialized Floor Technicians on night shifts to guarantee zero disruption to daytime tenants, retail patrons, and executive visitors.

Per client specification, the program is engineered in three distinct sequential phases:

  • Phase 1 deep clean (expose damage to consider options): Comprehensive machine rotary scrub, deep cleaning solution, edge hand scraping, and extraction across all 11 lobbies to strip surface dirt down to the bare floor and expose existing wear and damage for mutual review; does not grind stone or remove scratches.
  • Phase 2: Modular Protective Sealer Option: Application of LATICRETE® STONETECH® BulletProof® deep penetrating sealer into stone pores and grout lines immediately following Phase 1. Repels oil and water stains; does not stop acid etch marks on polished marble.
  • Phase 3: Ongoing Recurring Maintenance Choice: Property management selects between Track A: Quarterly Maintenance (Every 3 Months) or Track B: Semi-Annual Maintenance (Every 6 Months) for regular machine wash and extraction to keep floors clean without diamond grinding.
📐 Commercial Minimum Billing Baseline (600 SQFT): All commercial lobby locations are quoted based on measured cleanable square footage, subject to an industry-standard 600 Cleanable Square Feet minimum billing baseline per facility. This baseline accounts for downtown equipment mobilization, safety transit, fresh water and power hookup, wall perimeter masking, and certified supervisory inspection. Facilities under 600 SF (Virtuoso @ 560 SF, Knights of Pythias @ 414 SF, and Plaza Hotel @ 345 SF) are billed at the standard 600 SF baseline rate across all service phases.
🛡️ Property Protection & Furniture Handling Protocol: To prevent accidental scuffs, scratches, or property damage to tenant furnishings and historic decor, building management is requested to clear and replace all lobby furniture, accent tables, and artwork prior to and following service. Where moving assistance by HWB crew is requested, it is available and billed on an hourly basis.

PHOTO 2: CHASE BANK (420 THROCKMORTON) CONDITION: TRAFFIC WEAR & LOSS OF SHINE

Heavy Traffic Walkway Wear & Dull Finish

Primary floor system: Polished Terrazzo

Condition Observed: Clear loss of shine along the main walking path. While the upper right section still reflects light, the center walkway has become dull and cloudy from grit and sand tracked in from outside. This daily foot traffic has worn down the surface polish, allowing dirt to settle into the floor.

Recommended Service: Regular machine scrubbing and deep dirt extraction under Track A: Quarterly Floor Care ($0.54/SF) to remove gritty soil and keep the terrazzo shining; deep scratches require Option C: Diamond Restoration.
PHOTO 3: PETROLEUM BLDG (210 W 6TH ST) CONDITION: ACID ETCH RINGS & DULL SPOTS

Acid Stain Marks & Chemical Etch Rings

Primary floor system: Polished Marble Tile

Condition Observed: Numerous splash marks and droplet rings scattered across the floor. Acidic liquids (coffee spills, fruit drinks, hand sanitizer drips, or acidic custodial cleaners) ate into the polished marble surface. This leaves dull, chalky white spots that trap dirt and cannot be cleaned off with normal mopping.

Recommended Service: Diamond polishing to shave past the damaged surface and restore the mirror shine, followed by Option A: BulletProof® Penetrating Sealer to protect against liquid stains.
PHOTO 4: VIRTUOSO BUILDING (505 MAIN ST / HISTORIC SCHWARZ BLDG) CONDITION: DEEP GOUGES & EQUIPMENT SCRATCHES

Deep Scratches & Gouges from Moving Equipment

Primary floor system: Polished Marble Tile

Condition Observed: Heavy white scratch lines caused by dragging metal items (unpadded dollies, furniture legs, freight carts, or maintenance equipment) across the floor. These deep gouges cut into the black marble, ruining the high-gloss appearance.

Recommended Service: Requires Option C: Capital Diamond Restoration ($2.65 / SF) using coarse diamond grinding to level out deep scratch valleys, followed by fine polishing steps to bring back a mirror-like shine.
