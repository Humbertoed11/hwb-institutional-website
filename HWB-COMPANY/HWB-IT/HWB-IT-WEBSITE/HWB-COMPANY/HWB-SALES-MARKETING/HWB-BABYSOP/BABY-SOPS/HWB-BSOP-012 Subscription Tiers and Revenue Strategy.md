# HWB-BABYSOP: Subscription & Impact Model

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Sustainability Through Subscription | Impact Through Education.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the revenue and access model for the HWB-BABYSOP ecosystem. The goal is to create a sustainable "Infrastructure-as-a-Service" (IaaS) for businesses while providing free foundational training to schools.

## 2.0 Subscription Tiers

### 2.1 The "Founder" Tier (Business Subscription)
*   **Target:** Startups, Small Businesses, Growing Enterprises.
*   **Model:** $49/mo or $499/year.
*   **Deliverables:**
    *   Full Access to 50+ Baby SOP Templates.
    *   Unlimited Business Birth Certificate Registry.
    *   Direct Messaging Access to the Silver Fox Pool.
    *   Monthly "Fidelity Audit" Reports.

### 2.2 The "Future Project" Tier (School Subscription)
*   **Target:** High Schools, Vocational Centers, DECA/FBLA Clubs.
*   **Model:** **FREE ($0).**
*   **Deliverables:**
    *   Student Registry Access for Future Projects.
    *   Free Sigma-Junior™ Certification Modules.
    *   Classroom "Gemba" Resources for Teachers.
    *   Digital "Fidelity ID" Cards for all students.
