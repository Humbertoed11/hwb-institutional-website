Standard Operating Procedure: Organizational Structure

1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the official company structure and key roles for HWB Cleaning Services LLC. It makes sure everyone knows their job and sets a clear separation between operational teams and quality planning teams.

2.0 Scope

This structure applies to all personnel, administrative staff, and AI agents operating within the company ecosystem.

3.0 Key Roles & Roster

3.1 Leadership & Executive Control

  • Humberto Dominguez (CEO): Permanent approval authority for all project documentation, strategic reports, and QMS SOPs.
  • VP of Finance: Responsible for fiscal oversight, development costs, and financial audits.

3.2 Quality & System Architect (George)

  • George (Lead Developer / Systems Architect): Lead autonomous agent. The sole authority in charge of ISO 9001 and QMS compliance. Manages the system architecture and tech stack.

3.3 Operational Support Specialists

  • CRM Specialist: Manages relational customer lists, synchronization logic, and database integrations.
  • Marketing Lead: Oversees automated B2B customer generation and corporate outreach.
  • Recovery Specialist: Responsible for automated backups, system snapshots, and storage management.
  • Innovation Lead: Manages tech forecasting and new product design.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Conduct an organizational review during the annual management meeting.
  • Verify that all automated system processes match their assigned role scope.
