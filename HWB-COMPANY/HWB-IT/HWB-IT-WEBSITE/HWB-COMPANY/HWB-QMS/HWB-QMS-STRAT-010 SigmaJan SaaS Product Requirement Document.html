Product Requirement Document: SigmaJan™ SaaS Platform

1.0 Purpose & Scope

This document defines the functional and technical requirements for the SigmaJan™ software platform. SigmaJan is a multi-tenant Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application designed to help commercial cleaning business owners run, scale, and audit their operations using Six Sigma quality controls.

2.0 Target Audience & Personas

  • The Business Owner (Admin): Manages client billing, crew schedules, and operations dashboards.
  • The Crew Supervisor: Oversees site audits, coordinates check-ins, and manages chemical compliance on-site.
  • The Technician (Cleaner): Executes site-specific checklists, uploads photos of finished tasks, and logs work hours via the mobile app.
  • The End Customer (Facility Manager): Accesses reports, pays invoices, and submits service requests through the client portal.

3.0 Functional Requirements

3.1 Module 1: Multi-Tenant Architecture & White-Labeling

  • Data Isolation: All database tables (such as clients, schedules, and chemicals) must use a tenant_id to prevent companies from seeing each other's data.
  • Custom Branding: Subscribing cleaning businesses can upload their company logo, set custom colors, and map the portal to their own web domain.

3.2 Module 2: Scheduling, Dispatch, & GPS Tracking

  • Visual Calendar: A drag-and-drop calendar interface to schedule shifts and assign crews to facilities.
  • GPS Check-In Validation: Cleaners can only clock in when their phone's GPS is within 50 meters of the facility centroid.
  • Route Optimization: Integration with mapping services to calculate the fastest driving routes for mobile crews.

3.3 Module 3: Field Execution (Mobile App)

  • Offline Support: Technicians must be able to complete checklists and take photos without internet, with automatic syncing once a connection is restored.
  • Sequential Task Locking: Technicians cannot check off task N+1 until task N is completed and verified with photo proof.

3.4 Module 4: Billing, Invoicing, & Accounting

  • Automated Invoicing: Generate and email invoices to customers instantly upon work order completion.
  • QuickBooks Integration: Automatically sync client invoices, payouts, and expenses with QuickBooks Online.
  • ACH & Credit Card Processing: Integrated payment gateway (like Stripe) for automatic invoice collection.

3.5 Module 5: Sales & CRM

  • Proposal Generator: Automatically create PDF bid proposals based on building square footage and cleaning frequency.
  • Automated Lead Follow-ups: Email and text follow-up campaigns to nurture cold leads.
  • Customer Request Portal: Facility managers can log in to view audits, pay bills, and report service complaints.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Verify database query isolation by testing that Tenant A cannot access Tenant B's data under any condition.
  • Confirm that the offline database (IndexedDB) syncs successfully within 60 seconds of network restoration during testing.
