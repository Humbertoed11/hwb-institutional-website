Standard Operating Procedure: Backoffice & CRM Management Suite

1.0 Purpose

This document explains how to use and manage the HWB backoffice and client database. It ensures that client information remains accurate, access is kept safe, and automated timeouts protect the system when it is not being used.

2.0 Scope

This procedure applies to all office employees, support team members, and developers who manage our digital tools and customer database.

3.0 Operational Procedures

3.1 System Access and Login

To access the administrative tools:

  1. Open the web browser and navigate to the `/login` route.
  2. Enter your corporate username and password.
  3. If the login is successful, you will be redirected to the main operations hub. All failed attempts are sent to a safety page and recorded for review.

3.2 Login Timeout and Inactivity Policy

To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive company and client data, a strict inactivity policy is enforced:

  • Inactivity Window: The system continuously checks for user activity (mouse movements, clicks, scrolls, or keystrokes). If no activity is detected for **30 minutes**, an alert is triggered.
  • Countdown Alert: A conspicuous warning modal overlay appears on the screen with a **1-minute (60 seconds) timer**.
  • Action Choices:
    • Keep Working: Clicking this button hides the warning, resets the 30-minute idle timer, and sends a quick backend ping (`/heartbeat`) to refresh your session cookie.
    • Logout Now / Expiration: Clicking this button or allowing the 60-second timer to hit zero immediately logs you out and redirects the browser to the login screen.
  • Backend Enforcement: To prevent any bypass of the frontend timer, a hard **31-minute session lifetime** is enforced in the server configuration. Any request made after 31 minutes of silence is rejected.
Security Standard

Never leave your computer unlocked or unattended while logged in. If you step away from your desk, the system will automatically log you out after 30 minutes to protect our client files.

3.3 Managing the Double-Sidebar Layout

The office dashboard uses two sidebars to make work faster and cleaner:

  • Primary Sidebar (Far Left): Used to switch between main areas of the website (Pulse, CRM, Assets, Lab).
  • Control Rack (Inner Left): Used for specific actions inside each department (e.g., "Add Activity," "Filters").

3.4 Adding Leads and Client Conversions

  1. Gathering Data: The marketing team saves new leads as raw files inside the marketing folder.
  2. Ingesting Leads: Run the `Lead Ingestor` script to transfer the files directly into the PostgreSQL database.
  3. Client Conversion: Find the lead in the marketing dashboard and click the **"Convert ⚡"** button. The system will automatically move the information to the active Client list and create a new history record.

4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)

  • Timeout Verification: Open the dashboard, remain completely still for 30 minutes, and confirm that the session warning modal pops up.
  • Heartbeat Verification: Click "Keep Working" on the modal and verify that it hides the warning and that the browser sends a successful `/heartbeat` network request.
  • Hard Logout Verification: Allow the countdown timer to hit zero and confirm that the browser is redirected to the home index page.
