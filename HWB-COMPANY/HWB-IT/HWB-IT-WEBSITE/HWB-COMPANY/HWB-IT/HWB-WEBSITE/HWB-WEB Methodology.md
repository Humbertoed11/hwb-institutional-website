# The Math of the "Waste of Talent" Report

## 1.0 High-Impact Data Fact (The "Bombastic" Truth)
**"Role dilution and skill gaps cost employers 25 working days per employee annually—essentially deleting one full month of professional output every year."**
*   *Source:* [Multiverse - The Skills Gap Report 2023](https://www.multiverse.io/en-GB/resources/skills-gap-report-2023) (Referencing productivity loss in high-skill environments).

## 2.0 The Mathematical Model
The SigmaFidelity™ calculator uses a multi-layered approach to determine the **Annual Hidden Deficit (AHD)**.

**Formula:** `AHD = (DL + OC + TR)`

*   **DL (Direct Labor Misallocation):** `[Employee Hourly Wage] × [Cleaning Hours/Week] × 52`.
*   **OC (Opportunity Cost):** The value lost by the employee not performing their core specialized task (e.g., lesson planning, client retention).
*   **TR (Turnover Risk Premium):** SHRM identifies that role dilution and burnout are primary drivers of turnover.

## 3.0 Strategic Citations (The Proof)

### A. Lean Six Sigma: The 8th Waste
In the Toyota Production System and Lean Six Sigma, **"Non-Utilized Talent"** is the 8th form of waste (*Muda*). It occurs when organizations assign tasks to employees that do not leverage their skills or experience.
*   *Source:* [University of North Texas (UNT) - Lean Six Sigma & The 8 Wastes](https://professionaldevelopment.unt.edu/lean-six-sigma-green-belt)

### B. Texas State Auditor: The Financial Impact of Turnover
The **Texas State Auditor's Office (SAO)** reports that the cost of replacing an employee—due to burnout or role dissatisfaction—typically ranges from **50% to 200% of their annual salary**.
*   *Source:* [Texas SAO - Classified Employee Turnover Report](https://sao.texas.gov/Reports/Main/23-706.pdf)

### C. Texas Workforce Commission: Opportunity Cost
The **Texas Workforce Commission (TWC)** highlights the impact of labor misallocation on state productivity. When a Director chooses "Teacher mopping," they are losing "Quality of Care" gains and suppressing regional economic output.
*   *Source:* [Texas Workforce Commission - Annual Impact Reports](https://www.twc.texas.gov/news/annual-reports)

### D. Texas Administrative Code: Janitorial Prohibition
Texas Minimum Standards for Child-Care Centers **§746.1203(3)(C)** explicitly mandates that caregivers must be free from activities not directly involving teaching, care, and supervision, specifically naming **"Janitorial duties"** as a prohibited activity during child care hours.
*   *Source:* [Texas Secretary of State - TAC §746.1203](https://texreg.sos.state.tx.us/public/readtac$ext.TacPage?sl=R&app=9&p_dir=&p_rloc=&p_tloc=&p_ploc=&pg=1&p_tac=&ti=26&pt=1&ch=746&rl=1203)

## 4.0 Conclusion for Leadership
Having a $25/hr professional perform $15/hr janitorial work is not "scrappy management"—it is a **negative ROI activity**. SigmaFidelity™ provides the information fidelity to correct this systemic defect.
