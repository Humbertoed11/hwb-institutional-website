Texas Real Estate Research Center: Market Intelligence Ingestion & Mapping

This document records the strategic integration of data feeds, economic indexes, and market publications from the Texas Real Estate Research Center (TAMU TRERC) into HexGrowth's geospatial intelligence platform. It defines how regional land pricing, construction forecasts, and development metrics are indexed to increase system reasoning intelligence.

ℹ️ INTELLIGENCE OBJECTIVE

To ingestion-map TRERC's research findings to standard real estate lobes, establishing a deterministic baseline for statewide commercial and rural land evaluations.

1.0 TRERC Core Data Streams

The following TAMU TRERC research divisions provide data feeds for spatial modeling:

  • Texas Housing Insight (Monthly): Aggregates home sales, median home prices, building permits, construction starts, and monthly inventory counts by Metropolitan Statistical Area (MSA) and county.
  • Outlook for the Texas Economy (Monthly): Measures job growth, employment rates, local population flows, consumer spending indices, and inflation rates.
  • Texas Rural Land Markets (Quarterly): Analyzes acreage transfers, median price per acre, sales volume, and land market health across the seven rural land regions of Texas.
  • Commercial Real Estate Roundup (Quarterly): Audits office space utilization, retail mall adaptions, industrial manufacturing corridors, and warehouse demand indicators.

2.0 Ingestion Mapping to HexGrowth Decision Lobes

Data from TRERC is indexed into the PostGIS database and mapped directly to active feasibility lobes to optimize opportunity scoring:

3.0 Persistence & Knowledge Indexing

The ingestion process utilizes the system's persistence protocol (scripts/HEX-PERSISTENCE-PROTOCOL.py) to automatically scan the documentation repositories. Extracted data points are indexed inside the database table HEX_KB_Library (Neural Core) for use by the abductive and inductive inference loop during project site audits.
