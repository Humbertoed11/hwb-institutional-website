HEXGROWTH: HUD Customization Architecture (06/03/2026)

This report documents the architectural guidelines for delivering custom HUD layout profiles to developers based on their specific land use criteria.

1.0 Options for Customization

  • Option 1: Custom Layer Toggles (User-Controlled Layout): The client can turn map layers (like electric grids, flood zones, or school district boundaries) on or off from their sidebar to adjust visual clutter.
    Reference: hud.html
  • Option 2: Custom Lobe Weighting (Algorithm Tuning): The client can adjust how much individual data lobes influence the consolidated property score. For example, setting the Power Lobe to 80% and the School District Lobe to 0% for industrial warehouse users.
  • Option 3: Asset Class Presets (One-Click Profiles): Built-in profiles tailored to specific real estate sectors:
    • Retail Profile: Highlights daily traffic count, highway exits, and neighborhood demographics.
    • Industrial Profile: Highlights nearby transmission lines, power stations, and highway freight corridors.
    • Housing Profile: Highlights school ratings, wetlands, and zoning setbacks.
