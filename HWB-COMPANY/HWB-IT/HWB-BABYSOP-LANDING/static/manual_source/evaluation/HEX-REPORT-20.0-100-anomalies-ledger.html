Institutional Ledger: 100 Anomalies for Geospatial Intelligence

Scope: Defines the 100 secondary indicators and anomalous signals compiled by George Bytes to drive the proactive anomaly hunting engine of the HEXGROWTH terminal.

1.0 Zoning & Municipal Code Anomalies (Lobe 1: City Brain)

  1. Requests for height limit variations on windowless structures.
  2. Permit applications for thick, sound-insulating acoustic buffer walls.
  3. Sudden zoning reclassifications from agricultural to high-density utility nodes.
  4. FAA filings requesting crane clearances or high exhaust stack heights on empty land.
  5. Large-scale parcel consolidations (aggregations) by anonymous holding LLCs.
  6. Building permits for windowless industrial structures with huge footprints.
  7. Fast-tracked utility easement applications across public parklands or roads.
  8. Large setback variations (positioning structures unusually far from roadways).
  9. Spikes in minor site-clearing or soil boring permits with no major building filings.
  10. Fast-track municipal annexation requests for unincorporated fringe lands.

2.0 Infrastructure & Logistics Anomalies (Lobe 2: TxDOT Brain)

  1. Paving permits for heavy truck staging areas on low-occupancy sites.
  2. Proposals to modify TxDOT exit ramp capacities near vacant land.
  3. Installation of heavy-duty traffic signals at low-traffic rural road intersections.
  4. Weight rating increases or load capacity upgrades for local bridges.
  5. Right-of-way easement requests for fiber-optic duct banks.
  6. Unexplained utility pipeline relocation filings near transit nodes.
  7. Sudden spikes in vehicle counts on regional farm-to-market roads.
  8. Construction of triple-redundant secure site entrance lanes.
  9. Roadway widening requests with no matching commercial retail filings.
  10. Industrial rail spur reactivation requests adjacent to empty land.

3.0 Utility & Energy Grid Anomalies (Lobe 3: Power Brain)

  1. Substation capacity upgrades (transformer increases from 20MVA to 100MVA+).
  2. Applications for direct high-voltage line taps (138kV or 345kV spurs).
  3. Heavy underground power conduit banks crossing agricultural boundaries.
  4. Backup generator emissions licenses matching high megawatt capacities.
  5. Direct fiber junction vaults installed on agricultural boundaries.
  6. Battery energy storage system (BESS) permit requests near warehouses.
  7. Power purchase agreements (PPA) for large local solar arrays.
  8. Large utility substation land acquisitions.
  9. Redundant grid feeder lines routing from separate substations to one site.
  10. Utility supply lag alerts from grid operators (such as ERCOT).

4.0 Resources & Environmental Anomalies (Lobe 4: Legal/Env Brain)

  1. Industrial water tap sizes exceeding 12 inches on non-residential parcels.
  2. Wastewater discharge salinity surcharge agreements with high-volume thresholds.
  3. Sudden purchases of local wetland mitigation bank credits.
  4. High-frequency geotechnical soil boring permit clusters.
  5. Phase I environmental site assessments showing soil remediation on clean land.
  6. Local groundwater extraction limit increases.
  7. Purchases of adjacent buffer zones (non-buildable land rings).
  8. Easement quitclaims filed by utility providers to clear titles.
  9. Sudden drainage ditch modifications or retention pond volume expansions.
  10. High-capacity storm sewer tie-in applications.

5.0 Field Audit & Hardening Anomalies (Lobe 5: Ranger Brain)

  1. Construction of crash-rated security fences (K-rated bollards/gates).
  2. Windowless concrete walls built facing active public rights-of-way.
  3. High-density CCTV pole installations on property perimeters.
  4. High-lumen downward-pointing security lighting designs.
  5. Unscheduled private security patrols guarding empty dirt sites.
  6. No-fly zone requests or drone-detection system registrations.
  7. Multi-layer access card reader layouts at construction gates.
  8. Security guard shack construction permits with bulletproof glass.
  9. Large diesel fuel storage tank permits (for backup engines).
  10. Reinforced underground concrete basement or bunker vaults.

6.0 Sentiment & Social Signals (Lobe 6: Pulse Brain)

  1. Local resident complaints on social media about constant low-frequency humming noise.
  2. Community meeting debates regarding high-power usage projects.
  3. Neighborhood Facebook groups protesting "unnamed corporate projects."
  4. Social media posts showing pictures of heavy machinery on agricultural land.
  5. Spikes in local news reports about secret tech company acquisitions.
  6. Local subreddits discussing strange fiber-optic lines being laid.
  7. Inquiries on local forums about sudden drops in residential water pressure.
  8. Protests at city hall regarding agricultural tax exemptions being canceled.
  9. Nextdoor complaints about construction trucks damaging local roads.
  10. Online local job listings seeking critical infrastructure engineers.

7.0 Financial & Valuation Anomalies (Lobe 7: Finance Brain)

  1. Land transaction values exceeding local market averages by 200%+.
  2. Rapid flips of adjacent parcels by intermediate holding shell companies.
  3. Spikes in commercial property tax assessments on vacant land.
  4. Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) attached to minor municipal land deals.
  5. Unusually high earnest money deposits recorded on title filings.
  6. Option-to-purchase contracts with long due-diligence periods.
  7. Capital improvement budgets allocating millions to rural utility extensions.
  8. Significant shifts in localized CAP rates without matching commercial sales.
  9. Tax incentive agreements specifying minimum employee counts but huge capital costs.
  10. Massive loan registrations on parcels with small declared building scopes.

8.0 Ingestion & API Integrity Anomalies (Lobe 11: Scout Brain)

  1. Server connection time-outs on specific municipal GIS links.
  2. Sudden changes in polygon coordinates for county parcel shapes.
  3. 403 Forbidden redirects when fetching county appraisal data.
  4. Missing transaction records on historically high-volume routes.
  5. High latency spikes on public geocoding API connections.
  6. Discrepancy between spatial county boundaries and tax registry keys.
  7. Missing metadata fields (null owners) on recently sold parcels.
  8. Server redirects from HTTP to HTTPS on old county API targets.
  9. Inconsistencies in H3 grid calculations across resolution boundaries.
  10. Database constraint errors during bulk spatial data imports.

9.0 Relocation & Demographic Signals (General Development)

  1. Corporate job listings for high-capacity local recruitment before site filings.
  2. Spikes in short-term hotel bookings or rental housing demand in small towns.
  3. Corporate relocation filings registered with state secretary departments.
  4. Relocation consultant site visits recorded in city visitor logs.
  5. Unexplained spikes in school district enrollment forecasts.
  6. Surges in local utility connections for temporary construction trailers.
  7. Rapid absorption of local logistics or warehousing space by non-local buyers.
  8. High-volume purchases of regional office furniture or hardware equipment.
  9. Job postings for local executive assistants by unnamed multinational firms.
  10. Sudden increases in local commercial vehicle lease registrations.

10.0 Site Layout & Operational Signposts (General Development)

  1. Rooftop layouts showing huge chiller footprints with zero office space.
  2. High-volume diesel fuel delivery contract registrations.
  3. Large orders of cooling towers or HVAC systems from local suppliers.
  4. Installation of heavy-duty security gates at all parking entrances.
  5. Large asphalt parking lots with very few painted parking spaces.
  6. Building designs showing massive electrical intake rooms (switchgear).
  7. High-density fiber-optic connection panels inside warehouse lobbies.
  8. Permits for heavy-duty industrial backup batteries (UPS systems).
  9. Unusually high structural floor-load capacity ratings (heavy server racks).
  10. High-frequency HVAC maintenance contract filings.
