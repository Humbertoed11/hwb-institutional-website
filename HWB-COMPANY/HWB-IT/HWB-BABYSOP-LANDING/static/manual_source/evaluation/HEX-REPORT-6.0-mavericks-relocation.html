HEXGROWTH: Mavericks Relocation Opportunity (Valley View Site)

This report documents the initial data collection and score updates across all seven brain tracking lobes regarding the Mavericks' planned relocation to the 104-acre Valley View Mall site by 2031.

1.0 Lobe-Specific Intelligence Feed

  • Lobe 1 (City Brain - City Rules & Spending): The city has set up a Tax Reinvestment Zone (TIF) and spending plans to rebuild sewers and roads at the Valley View site. **Consensus: +3.5 Alpha Boost** due to strong city support.
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-1.0-city.html
  • Lobe 2 (TxDOT Brain - Roads & Traffic): The site sits near the intersection of I-635 (LBJ Freeway) and Preston Road, one of the busiest highway nodes in Dallas. **Consensus: +2.8 Infrastructure Score** due to high traffic count and exit lanes.
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-2.0-txdot.html
  • Lobe 3 (Power Brain - Electricity & Fiber): Building a massive arena requires heavy power lines. The site is close to high-voltage transmission lanes along LBJ. **Consensus: Neutral (New substation required).**
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-3.0-power.html
  • Lobe 4 (Legal/Env Brain - Environmental Risks): The former mall site requires inspections for soil contamination from old auto service shops and dry cleaners. **Consensus: Warning (Soil checks needed).**
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-4.0-legal.html
  • Lobe 5 (Ranger Brain - Physical Ground Truth): Demolition of the old mall structures is already active. Property boundaries are ready for redevelopment. **Consensus: Confirmed (Active demolition).**
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-5.0-ranger.html
  • Lobe 6 (Pulse Brain - Neighborhood Sentiment): The high-income neighborhoods nearby (Preston Hollow) are likely to protest increased traffic, noise, and game-day crowds. **Consensus: -1.5 Opposition Drag.**
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-6.0-pulse.html
  • Lobe 7 (Finance Brain - Market Trends): The purchase of land by Sands Corp ownership triggers a massive speculative land rush. Nearby retail and commercial land values will appreciate. **Consensus: +4.0 Value Boost.**
    Reference: HEX-LOBE-7.0-finance.html
