SIX SIGMA QUALITY ASSURANCE: Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) Ledger

Purpose: Identifies potential failure modes within the HexGrowth system, evaluates their severity and frequency, and defines automated controls to prevent quality defects. This ensures the technical core remains stable for acquisition validation.

1.0 System FMEA Rating Matrix

The table below lists the potential technical defects, scores their Severity (S), Likelihood of Occurrence (O), and Probability of Detection (D) on a scale of 1 to 10, calculates the Risk Priority Number (RPN = S × O × D), and outlines standard mitigations.
