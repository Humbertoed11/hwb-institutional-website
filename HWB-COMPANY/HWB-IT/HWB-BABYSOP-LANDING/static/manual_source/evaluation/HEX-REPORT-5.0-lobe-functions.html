HEXGROWTH: Lobe Expansion Plan (06/03/2026)

This plan maps the three alternative solutions directly into our core seven-lobe brain system, turning software ideas into active backend tracker features.

1.0 Lobe 1 Expansion (City Brain: Approval Forecasts)

Proposed Feature: Zoning Vote Predictor.

Lobe Integration: Extends HEX-LOBE-1.0-city.html.

How it works: The City Brain will read city hall vote records. Instead of just listing zoning zones, the brain will check similar past requests and calculate the percentage chance of getting a zoning change approved by the local council.

2.0 Lobe 5 Expansion (Ranger Brain: Field Dispatch)

Proposed Feature: Ground Truth Dispatcher.

Lobe Integration: Extends HEX-LOBE-5.0-ranger.html.

How it works: When the computer scoring system finds a confusing map detail (e.g. unmapped wetlands or road access blocks), the Ranger Brain issues a check request to local land surveyors. They use their phone app to upload photos, resolving data questions in real time.

3.0 Lobe 7 Expansion (Finance Brain: Reverse Matching)

Proposed Feature: Automated Site Finder.

Lobe Integration: Extends HEX-LOBE-7.0-finance.html.

How it works: Instead of having developers search manually, the Finance Brain scans the entire database daily. It flags any property matching target criteria (like close to utilities, cheap, and outside flood zones) and emails the lead straight to the developer.
