Separation Implementation Plan: Legal & Corporate Isolation (ISO 4.2)

Purpose: Outlines the legal actions, assignments, and corporate separation steps to isolate HexGrowth intellectual assets and vendor agreements from legacy parent entities.

1.0 Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment

To establish clean investment lines for acquisition valuation, all software assets must be legally assigned to the HexGrowth corporate entity:

  • Software & Codebase: Execute a patent and code assignment agreement transferring all rights to the 7-lobe neural decision engine, HUD code, database schemas, and ingestion algorithms from legacy parent entities to HexGrowth LLC.
  • Data Moat Transfer: Transfer ownership of all compiled PostGIS tables (containing the 122,193 Resolution-11 parcel records) and GIS shapefile datasets.

2.0 Contractual Separation

HexGrowth operations rely on external spatial data services and map engines. These agreements must be separated from legacy parent accounts:

  • Data Feed Contracts: Re-negotiate and sign independent vendor contracts for real estate database integrations (e.g., Regrid, Zoneomics, ATTOM Data) directly under the HexGrowth name.
  • API Engine Accounts: Migrate map service billing keys (e.g., Mapbox, CartoDB basemap subscriptions) out of legacy parent accounts into isolated billing profiles.

3.0 Corporate Governance & Board Resolutions

To implement these plans, the board of directors must approve the corporate transition:

  • Board Approval: The Chairman (Humberto Dominguez) and the CEO (Daniel Dominguez) must sign formal resolutions approving the transfer of assets and the isolation of databases.
  • Operational Liabilities: Establish independent commercial liability policies protecting the platform servers, data caches, and client portfolios.
Acquisition Condition: Clean legal separation of all software, databases, and vendor accounts is a critical condition of the final acquisition contract.
