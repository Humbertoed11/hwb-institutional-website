1.0 Advantage & Data Inputs

Front-runs global corporate investment by monitoring where the top 1% of capital is moving, and integrates regional economic indicators to calculate feasibility.

  • Global Capital Inflow: Corporate relocation intents, tech factory CAPEX, and local employment data.
  • TRERC Regional Indexes: MSA home price indexes, quarterly price per acre rural land values, and housing affordability indexes.
  • Capitalization & Yield Benchmarks: Early 2026 interest rate spreads, CPI vs PCE inflation signals, and historical cap rates.

2.0 Alpha Voting Logic

This is an Additive Lobe. It yields positive opportunity vectors based on real estate yields and capital inflows:

  • Market Cap Rate Valuation: Integrates dynamic cap rate benchmarks (extracted from comparative market logs) to calculate the Net Present Value (NPV) of cash flows.
  • Inflation & Interest Spread Adjustments: Adjusts target internal rate of return (IRR) models based on CPI and PCE spread trends, applying a cost-of-capital penalty when PCE inflation runs above 2.5%.
