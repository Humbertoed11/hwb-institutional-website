3.0 The Voting Mechanism

The Neural Core processes votes through a weighted sum calculation. Each operational lobe provides an Alpha Score (0.0 - 10.0), which is multiplied by its active weight. While initial baseline weights are defined below, Lobe 9 (Accountability Brain) dynamically tunes these parameters daily to minimize forecasting error.

L1 City Brain20%Zoning Velocity & TIF Zones

4.0 The Institutional Veto Rule

In accordance with Poka-Yoke principles, the Legal/Env Brain and Ranger Brain hold special jurisdictional authority:

  • Hard Veto: If Legal/Env detects a Level-5 Flood Zone or acute contamination, the HexScore is forced to 0.0 regardless of other scores.
  • Reality Override: The Ranger Brain (Field Audit) possesses the authority to manually adjust any other lobe's score based on physical 'Ground Truth' verification.

5.0 Ingestion and Output Loops

  • L11 Scout Gatekeeper: Prevents system contamination by verifying county endpoint latency and matching schema templates before routing to the operational lobes.
  • L9 Metacognitive Tuning: Audits consensus outputs daily against market comparables and updates active weights in HEX_SystemState.
  • L10 Report Output: Reads the completed consensus data and dynamic parameters to generate the final Customizable Market Reports.
