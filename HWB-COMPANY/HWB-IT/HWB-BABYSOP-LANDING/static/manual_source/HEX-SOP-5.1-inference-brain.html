Standard Operating Procedure: Inference Brain (Lobe 8)

1.0 Purpose

To define the architectural standard for the Inference Brain (Lobe 8) as the central synthesis engine. Lobe 8 coordinates data from the seven input lobes (L1 City, L2 Transit, L3 Power, L4 Legal/Env, L5 Ranger, L6 Pulse, L7 Finance) — pre-filtered and sanitized by L11 Scout Brain and dynamically weighted by L9 Accountability Brain — to generate predictive opportunities and risk metrics for L10 Alpha Brain compilation.

2.0 Logic Classification & Anomaly Hunting

Lobe 8 executes three forms of logical synthesis:

  • Deduction: Applies strict zoning laws and boundaries to filter out invalid sites.
  • Induction: Combines speed logs and land prices to recalculate baseline market value averages.
  • Abduction: Flags price jumps and public interest spikes to generate growth hypotheses.

2.1 Inquisitive Sub-routine (Proactive Anomaly Hunting)

Under abductive logic, Lobe 8 runs the Inquisitive Sub-routine. Rather than waiting for official project announcements, this engine stakes 3 to 6 indirect signals to detect anomalies. The routine triggers when a parcel shows stacked activity across the following sectors:

  • Power & Utilities (Lobe 3): Substation additions or high-voltage line conduits.
  • Zoning & Noise (Lobe 1): Special permits for thick acoustic wall boundaries.
  • Resources & Abatements (Lobe 4): Industrial water tap agreements, heavy wastewater flows, or unexplained tax abatements.
  • Sentiment & Labor (Lobe 6): Local noise complaints on social channels, or high-tech facility technician job postings.

If 3 or more signals are active on a single parcel, Lobe 8 flags it as a high-probability hidden development and triggers a targeted audit check via the Scout Brain.

3.0 Database Registry & Memory

Synthesized outputs are stored across the following core databases:

  • `"HEX_Projects"`: Holds the active opportunities mapped in the HUD viewport.
  • `"HEX_SystemState"`: Preserves the reasoning variables, inquiries, and hypotheses between sessions.
  • `"HEX_Telemetry"`: Logs the inference paths (Deduction, Induction, Abduction, and Inquisitive hunts) for execution audits.

4.0 Runtime Integration

The daily sync script HEX-DAILY-INFERENCE-SYNC.py triggers the Lobe 8 processing loop. Verification tests must assert that all calculations pass compiler checks and log compliant timestamps matching disk files.
