## Chapter 1: Introduction - What is "High-Fidelity" Cleaning?
When you listen to music on really good headphones, they call it "high-fidelity" because it sounds exactly like the original recording. I want my cleaning service to be the same way. I want every single room we clean to look exactly like our "perfect" standard, every single time. Most companies say they are good, but I want to *prove* it using data and science.

## Chapter 2: The Problem - Paying for Chores, Not Results
I noticed a big problem. In my business, we often pay people for the *time* they spend at a building, but that doesn't always mean the work was done right. I found out that about 15% of the money I spend on payroll was basically being wasted because things weren't organized. That’s like paying for a whole hour of work and having 10 minutes of it just disappear. I call this "Mopping Floors with Payroll," and I knew I had to stop it.

## Chapter 3: Step 1 (Define) - Mapping Out the Plan
To get my Black Belt, I had to start with the "Define" phase. This just means I had to be very clear about what I was trying to fix. I decided to build a mobile app for my cleaning teams. 

One of the first things I did was create something called a **SIPOC**. It sounds fancy, but it's just a simple chart that shows:
- **Suppliers:** Who gives us what we need (like me giving the budget).
- **Inputs:** What we need to start (like the cleaning supplies and the app).
- **Process:** The simple steps my team takes (Clock in, Clean, Take Photos, Clock out).
- **Outputs:** What we get at the end (a clean building and a report).
- **Customers:** Who is happy at the end (the people who own the buildings).

By mapping this out, I could see exactly where we might run into trouble before we even started.
