Following executive directive by CEO Humberto Dominguez on 10/12/2026, Super George has cataloged the competitive intelligence from the ProTeams.io assessment and placed five high-impact operational initiatives directly onto the official HWB Planning Table.

The Five Strategic Initiatives Placed on the Table:

  1. Automated Post-Shift "Proof of Work" Email Engine [HWB-PLAN-07]:
    Compiles completed tasks, GPS check-in/out stamps, and before/after photos into a client-ready digital summary delivered to property managers at 6:00 AM, eliminating client doubt and defending contracts against cancellations.
  2. Public Commercial Cleaning Cost Estimator [HWB-PLAN-08]:
    Deploys an interactive square-footage and frequency calculator on hwbcleaning.com that provides instant price ranges and weekly labor hour estimates while capturing high-intent commercial leads directly into PostgreSQL.
  3. Task-Level Photo Capture in Mobile Scope Engine [HWB-PLAN-09]:
    Requires cleaners to take live, watermark-stamped photos of high-liability tasks (restroom fixtures, commercial kitchen degreasing, final post-construction detailing) before closing a shift.
  4. Automated Cleaner Procedure Quizzes via Bot [HWB-PLAN-10]:
    Pushes automated 2-minute situational and technique questions weekly via Telegram/SMS using existing AcademyQuizQuestions, maintaining active EHSQ safety compliance in the field.
  5. Real-Time No-Show Sentinel & Rapid Reassignment [HWB-PLAN-11]:
    Detects if a cleaner has not clocked in 15 minutes before shift start, sending immediate alerts to supervisors with the three closest backup cleaners or vetted 1099 subcontractors for instant one-tap reassignment.
