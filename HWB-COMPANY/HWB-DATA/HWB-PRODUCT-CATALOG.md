# SigmaFidelity™ Product Catalog: The Agentic Enterprise
**Status:** ACTIVE - Version 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** 04/27/2026 by George

This catalog outlines the proprietary **Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS)** product lines developed by SigmaFidelity™. Each product is designed to replace high-cost human capital with scalable, deterministic, and LLM-orchestrated autonomous agents.

---

## 1.0 Revenue Operations (RevOps)

### 1.1 SigmaQuote™ (The Autonomous Estimator)
*   **Target Market:** Construction, Facility Management, Landscaping, Roofing.
*   **The Agent:** A multimodal vision agent (Phi-4 Reasoning) that reads blueprints or site photos, calculates spatial dimensions, applies local labor/material logic, and generates a client-ready proposal in < 3 minutes.
*   **Monetization:** $1,500/mo Base + $10/Blueprint OR 1.5% Revenue Share on closed contracts.

### 1.2 SigmaVoice™ (The 24/7 SDR)
*   **Target Market:** High-ticket B2B services, Real Estate, Legal Intake.
*   **The Agent:** An ultra-low latency (<650ms) voice agent connected to Azure Communication Services. It answers inbound calls, qualifies leads against an FAQ, books calendar appointments, and executes outbound follow-ups.
*   **Monetization:** $2,000/mo Base + $0.15/minute of talk time.

## 2.0 Backoffice & Finance (FinOps)

### 2.1 SigmaFinance™ (The Autonomous Controller)
*   **Target Market:** Agencies, SaaS, Subcontractors.
*   **The Agent:** A deterministic (Non-LLM) integration with Plaid/Stripe and the CRM. It autonomously matches bank feeds to invoices, identifies discrepancies, and triggers polite-but-firm follow-up sequences (via Email/SigmaVoice) for Accounts Receivable.
*   **Monetization:** $1,000/mo Base + 0.5% of successfully recovered aged receivables.

### 2.2 SigmaAudit™ (The Compliance Sentinel)
*   **Target Market:** ISO-9001 companies, Healthcare (HIPAA), Manufacturing.
*   **The Agent:** A continuous background process that audits digital paper trails (emails, completed Work Orders, SDS sheets). It flags missing signatures, outdated compliance documents, and prepares the "Pre-Audit" readiness report.
*   **Monetization:** $2,500/mo (Priced against a $90k/yr Compliance Officer).

---

## 3.0 Field & Infrastructure (DevSecOps)

### 3.1 SigmaDispatch™ (The Algorithmic Dispatcher)
*   **Target Market:** Logistics, HVAC fleets, Commercial Cleaning.
*   **The Agent:** Ingests live traffic data, tech skill levels, and SLA deadlines. It dynamically reroutes field workers via a mobile app, optimizing for the least windshield time and highest margin per hour.
*   **Monetization:** $50/mo per active field technician (SaaS model).

### 3.2 SigmaPeter™ (The Immortal Infrastructure)
*   **Target Market:** Mid-market companies with fragile on-premise or cloud setups.
*   **The Agent:** The commercialized version of our internal "Peter Sentinel." Provides 15-minute Shadow Snapshots, autonomous log rotation, and self-healing Docker/Azure restarts.
*   **Monetization:** $800/mo (The "Peace of Mind" subscription).

---

## 4.0 The "White-Label" Enterprise Package
*   **Target Market:** Franchisors (e.g., Servpro, JAN-PRO, Roto-Rooter).
*   **The Product:** A private-cloud deployment of the entire SigmaFidelity™ suite. The Franchisor rebrands the agents as their own proprietary technology to sell to their franchisees.
*   **Monetization:** $25,000 Setup Fee + $15,000/mo Platform Licensing + Azure Compute usage.
