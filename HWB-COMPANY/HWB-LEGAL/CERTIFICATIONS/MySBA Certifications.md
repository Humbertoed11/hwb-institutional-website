**The only legitimate SBA phone number related to Certifications is 1-866-SBA-HELP. "samregistration.com" is not affiliated with SBA.** SBA will **not** solicit information by email addresses other than a "@sba.gov" address. Capability statements are not required to apply for our programs. SBA does not charge any costs for applying to our programs or for Small Business Search (SBS) content or profile management.

## Benefits of Certification

- Access to sole-source and competitive set-aside contracts
- Assistance from federal procurement experts
- Business development assistance from dedicated Business Opportunity Specialists
- Opportunities to create joint ventures with established businesses
- Priority access to federal surplus property
