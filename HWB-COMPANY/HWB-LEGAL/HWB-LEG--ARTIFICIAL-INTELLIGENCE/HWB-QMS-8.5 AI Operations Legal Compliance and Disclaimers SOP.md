# 1.0 Purpose
This SOP establishes the legal compliance framework and mandatory disclaimers for all operations conducted by the Artificial Intelligence (AI) Department at HWB Cleaning Services LLC. It ensures that autonomous workflows, AI-generated communications, and data analytics mitigate liability and adhere to ethical standards.

# 2.0 Scope
This policy applies to all internal and external outputs generated by the SigmaFidelity™ AI suite, including marketing communications, CRM analytics, system code, and predictive modeling.

# 3.0 Procedure: Legal Review and Compliance

## 3.1 The Legal Review Mandate
The HWB Legal Department serves as the final authority on the compliance of AI operations.
1.  **Continuous Audit:** The Legal Department reserves the right to retroactively review, pause, or amend any AI-driven process or autonomous marketing cycle.
2.  **Input Requirement:** Major architectural shifts, the deployment of new AI personas, or sweeping changes to customer communication logic must receive formal input and clearance from the Legal Department before implementation.

## 3.2 Required Legal Disclaimers
To maintain transparency and manage expectations, the following disclaimers must be applied where appropriate:
*   **Communication Disclaimer:** Any automated email, direct message, or public update generated by an AI VP (e.g., Lauri Tells) must contain an identifiable footer: *"This communication was prepared by the HWB Autonomous AI Suite under the SigmaFidelity™ framework. For human assistance, please contact our office directly."*
*   **Data Accuracy Disclaimer:** Reports or dashboards generated by the AI must state: *"Data visualizations and insights are generated autonomously from empirical CRM data. While strict fidelity protocols are enforced, figures are subject to management verification."*

## 3.3 Ethical Impact and Data Handling
1.  **PII Protection:** AI agents must never log, output, or expose Personally Identifiable Information (PII) beyond the encrypted confines of the institutional database.
2.  **Empirical Absolute:** The AI Department shall not fabricate or hallucinate operational data. Any discovered deviation from the empirical dataset constitutes a critical failure requiring an immediate Corrective Action Request (CAR).
3.  **Non-Discrimination:** AI filtering for CRM and marketing operations (e.g., Lead Ingestor algorithms) must remain purely institutional and facility-focused (e.g., by NAICS codes or facility square footage) to avoid biased demographic targeting.

# 4.0 Verification
The effectiveness of these legal safeguards is verified via random compliance audits conducted by the CEO and the Legal Department on the `ActivityLog` and outbound communication channels.

# 5.0 Notes and Cautions
*   **Rapid Adaptation:** As AI regulations evolve, this document must be reviewed semi-annually by the Legal Department to ensure HWB Cleaning Services LLC remains fully compliant with federal and state AI governance laws.
