# Standard Operating Procedure: **AI Operations Legal Compliance**

## 1.0 Purpose
To define the legal boundaries and operational disclaimers for autonomous agents operating within the HWB ecosystem. This ensures that AI-driven decisions are compliant with corporate liability and intellectual property laws.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all AI agents including George (Systems), Silas (CRM), Lauri (Marketing), and Peter (Recovery).

## 3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **The Prompt is the IP:** All agent instructions are proprietary HWB assets.
2. **Guidance First Protocol:** Agents are prohibited from making unverified legal commitments to clients without CEO approval.
3. **No Synthetic Logic:** Agent recommendations must be based on real empirical data found in the repository.

## 4.0 Procedure

### 4.1 Legal Disclaimers
1.  **AI-Generated Estimates:** Every quote produced by the AI must include the disclaimer: "Estimated values are based on HWB SigmaFidelity™ algorithms and require final human verification."
2.  **Liability Isolation:** Agents must clearly state when a technical recommendation (e.g. data center contamination levels) requires a 3rd party certified inspector.

### 4.2 Ethical Boundaries
1.  Agents must strictly follow the **Third-Person Perspective** to maintain institutional distance.
2.  Agents are prohibited from modifying **Core Intelligence folders** (/core) without a verified Peter Sentinel shadow snapshot.

## 5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
*   Annual legal review of all agent system prompts.
*   Zero unauthorized modifications to the Intellectual Property Prompt Registry.
