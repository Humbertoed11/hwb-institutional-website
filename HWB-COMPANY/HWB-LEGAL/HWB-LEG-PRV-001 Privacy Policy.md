# **HWB Privacy Policy**

## 1.0 Commitment to Data Fidelity
HWB Cleaning Services LLC is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients' and employees' data. We handle all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) with industrial discipline and strictly adhere to North Texas data protection standards.

## 2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Zero Synthetic Data:** We only collect empirical data required for service delivery and compliance.
2. **Guidance First:** If a client requests data sharing with a 3rd party, ASK the CEO before facilitating.
3. **Physical Truth:** All sensitive database records are stored in the encrypted PostgreSQL production container.

## 3.0 Data Collection
We collect only the essential "Handshake" information:
*   Name, Email, and Phone for Lead Ingestion.
*   Building SQF and Traffic Cycle for pricing accuracy.
*   Employment history and certifications for HR compliance.

## 4.0 Data Usage
Data is used strictly for:
*   Generating accurate cleaning quotes.
*   Maintaining the ISO 9001:2015 audit trail.
*   Managing automated agent interactions via the Tiered Memory system.

## 5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
*   Annual internal security audit (HWB-QMS-9.2).
*   Zero unauthorized data disclosures reported.
