# HWB-LEG-002: Intellectual Property Master Prompt Registry

## 1.0 Purpose
This registry serves as the official corporate record for all "Master Prompts" used to generate Intellectual Property (IP) for HWB Cleaning Services LLC and the SigmaFidelity™ ecosystem. In an AI-driven organization, the prompt serves as the proprietary source code for creative assets. This document ensures reproducibility, brand consistency, and establishes a chain of custody for potential copyright or trademark filings.

## 2.0 Scope
This registry covers all generative AI prompts used for:
*   Company Logos and Sub-brand identities.
*   Product Design (Physical and Digital).
*   Marketing Graphics, Banners, and Hero Images.
*   SOP Architectural Diagrams and Flowcharts.

## 3.0 Standard Generative Custody Protocol
Before an asset is officially adopted by the company, its generation DNA must be logged in this registry using the following format:

*   **Asset Name:** The official name of the graphic or product.
*   **Department / Project:** The operational unit that owns the asset.
*   **Model & Version:** The specific AI used (e.g., Midjourney v6.0, Gemini Image Preview).
*   **Parameters / Seed:** Specific technical settings required to reproduce the asset.
*   **Master Prompt:** The exact, unedited text used to generate the asset.
*   **Negative Prompt:** Text used to restrict the generation.
*   **File Location:** Where the finalized asset is stored in the directory.
*   **IP Status:** (e.g., Internal Use, Trademark Pending, Concept).
