# Standard Operating Procedure: **Employee Competence SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose

This SOP defines the procedure for ensuring that all personnel whose work affects the quality of HWB Cleaning Services' products and services are competent. This includes processes for creating job descriptions, identifying and delivering training, and maintaining employee information files.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure applies to all employees of HWB Cleaning Services LLC and all management personnel responsible for hiring, training, and performance management.

## 3.0 Prerequisites

*   Defined roles and responsibilities (refer to `[[HWB-QMS-5.3 Organizational Structure SOP]]`).
*   Access to HR records and training resources.
*   Understanding of HWB Cleaning Services' quality objectives and service standards.

## 4.0 Procedure

### 4.1 Job Descriptions

1.  **Creation/Review:** For every position affecting service quality, a current job description shall be maintained using the `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-001 Job Description Template]]`.
2.  **Content:** Job descriptions shall clearly define:
    *   Role title and reporting lines.
    *   Key responsibilities and duties.
    *   Required education, skills, experience, and training for competence.
3.  **Approval:** All new or revised job descriptions must be reviewed and approved by the Operations & HR Manager and the Managing Director.

### 4.2 Training and Development

1.  **Training Needs Identification:** Training needs shall be identified during:
    *   New employee onboarding.
    *   Performance reviews.
    *   Introduction of new equipment, chemicals, or procedures.
    *   Analysis of nonconformities or customer feedback.
    *   A `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-002 Training Needs and Plan]]` shall be used to document identified needs.
2.  **Training Planning:** A training plan shall be developed outlining:
    *   Required training (e.g., equipment operation, chemical safety, cleaning techniques, QMS awareness).
    *   Method of training (e.g., on-the-job, external course, internal workshop).
    *   Timeline for completion.
    *   The `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-002 Training Needs and Plan]]` shall be updated accordingly.
3.  **Training Delivery:** Training shall be conducted by competent individuals and documented upon completion using the `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-003 Training Record]]`.
4.  **Effectiveness Evaluation:** The effectiveness of training shall be evaluated (e.g., through observation, testing, feedback) to ensure competence has been achieved.

### 4.3 Employee Information File

1.  **File Establishment:** A confidential information file shall be established for each employee affecting service quality.
2.  **Content:** Employee files shall contain pertinent records, including but not limited to:
    *   Application and hiring documents.
    *   Job description.
    *   Training records (refer to `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-003 Training Record]]`).
    *   Performance reviews.
    *   Certifications relevant to competence.
    *   A `[[HWB-FORM-7.2-004 Employee File Checklist]]` shall be used to ensure completeness.
3.  **Maintenance:** Employee files shall be maintained securely and updated regularly by the Human Resources department. Access shall be restricted to authorized personnel.

## 5.0 Verification

*   Competence records within employee files shall be reviewed periodically by the Operations & HR Manager.
*   Effectiveness of training shall be assessed through performance observations and feedback mechanisms.

## 6.0 Notes and Cautions

*   Maintaining accurate and up-to-date competence records is essential for demonstrating compliance and ensuring service quality.
*   All employee information must be handled with confidentiality and in accordance with relevant privacy regulations.
