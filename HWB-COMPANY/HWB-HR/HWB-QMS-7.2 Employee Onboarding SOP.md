# Standard Operating Procedure: **Employee Onboarding SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose
To ensure that all personnel are prepared, equipped, and informed of the company's QMS and safety protocols from their first day.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all new hires and management personnel.

## 3.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Guidance First:** If a new hire's background check is pending, ASK the CEO before digital onboarding.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Every onboarding step must be logged.
3. **Physical Truth:** Use absolute paths for the `Employee File Checklist`.

## 4.0 Procedure
1.  **Preparation:** Allocation of workspace/vehicle and procurement of PPE.
2.  **Access:** Creation of email and CRM credentials.
3.  **Safety:** Review of JHA and hazard communication standards.
4.  **Training:** provision of hands-on `quote_app` or CRM training.

## 5.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
*   Onboarding Form fully signed and filed.
*   System login verified for CRM `/crm/operations`.
