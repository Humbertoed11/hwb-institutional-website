## 3.0 Detailed Operational Discussion Points

### 3.1 Initiative 1: The "Proof-Badge" Digital Proposal (Kills Price Haggling)
* **The Field Problem:** When HWB bids $26–$28/hr for commercial janitorial or post-construction detailing, building managers often push back: *"Why are you charging $26/hr when Texas minimum wage is $7.25?"*
* **The HWB Execution:** In digital proposals, HWB embeds three clickable **Verification Badges**:
  1. **Federal Prevailing Wage Badge:** Links to SAM.gov SCA Wage Determination WD 2015-5231 ($21.48/hr total federal package for Dallas-Fort Worth janitorial staff).
  2. **Economic Inflation Badge:** Links to BLS CPI data showing a +53.2% rise in equipment, floor machinery, chemicals, and fuel since 2009.
  3. **Legal Protection Badge:** Links to HWB’s verified ACORD 25 Certificate of Insurance ($1M/$2M General Liability + Statutory Workers' Comp, EMR: .43).
* **Discussion Question for the Table:** Should HWB make these badges standard on all quotes above $2,500/month?

### 3.2 Initiative 2: The "Facility Scope Gap" Walkthrough Audit (The Trojan Horse)
* **The Field Problem:** Most cleaning companies walk a building, take notes, and email a plain dollar estimate. The property manager simply compares numbers and picks the cheapest quote.
* **The HWB Execution:** Instead of an estimate, HWB provides a **Facility Risk & Scope Gap Report**:
  * *Gap 1 (OSHA Hazard):* *"Your current vendor lacks an active OSHA HazCom SDS chemical binder on-site (Statutory OSHA fine risk: $16,131)."*
  * *Gap 2 (Security Risk):* *"Nighttime cleaning crew lacks verified background clearance or E-Verify documentation."*
  * *Gap 3 (Sanitation Deficit):* *"High-touch elevator buttons and conference tables show measurable organic biofilm."*
* **Discussion Question for the Table:** Can HWB's field estimators use a standard 10-point tablet checklist during walkthroughs to deliver this report within 30 minutes?

### 3.3 Initiative 3: The Three-Tier Commercial Service Spectrum
Medical Legal Spider proved that offering three clear options eliminates hesitation:
* **Tier 1: Basic Commercial Maintenance:** Daily trash, surface sanitization, and vacuuming. Competitive baseline for price-sensitive tenants.
* **Tier 2: Certified Quality Control (Hybrid Standard — The Core Offer):** Daily crew cleaning + bi-weekly photo inspection by an HWB Field Supervisor with digital sign-off sent directly to the property manager.
* **Tier 3: White-Glove High-Security (Bank, Medical & Cleanroom Grade):** 100% bonded, badge-tracked crews, ATP fluorescence cleanliness swabs, and ISO 9001 compliance logs.
* **Discussion Question for the Table:** Can HWB price Tier 2 at a 20% margin premium over Tier 1 as the default recommendation?

### 3.4 Initiative 4: RB2B Visitor Deanonymization on HWB Capability Pages
* **The Technology:** RB2B tracking script matches corporate IP addresses of website visitors to their LinkedIn profiles in real time.
* **The HWB Execution:** Deploy RB2B on:
  * `capability_statement.html`
  * `prequal.html`
  * `academy_catalog.html`
* When a property manager from CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, or a general contractor from Balfour Beatty views HWB’s prequalification page, Humberto Dominguez receives an immediate notification with the person's name, title, and company.
* **Discussion Question for the Table:** Who on the HWB sales team will follow up on LinkedIn within 60 minutes of an alert?

### 3.5 Initiative 5: "Get the Samples" Lead Magnet
* Replace high-friction "Request a Free Quote" forms with high-value downloads:
  * `[Download Sample Bank Cleaning Scope & Security Protocol]`
  * `[Download Sample Post-Construction Completion Punch-List]`
  * `[Download Subcontractor Compliance Package]`
* Property managers download these checklists to audit their current cleaning vendor, realize their existing vendor is cutting corners, and call HWB.

### 3.6 Initiative 6: The "One-Night Free Executive Lobby Detail" (The Test Case)
* Just as Medical Legal Spider offers a free test case to law firms, HWB offers skeptical property managers a free one-night detail of their main entrance lobby and executive restrooms.
* Once the building owner sees spotless fixtures and mirror-finish floors that their existing cleaner neglected for months, HWB closes the 12-month master contract.
