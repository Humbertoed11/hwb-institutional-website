### 4.2 The Three Scalable Commercial Engines

#### Engine 1: The Texas Daycare HHS Sanitization SaaS ($149.00 / month)
- **The Problem:** Texas Health and Human Services (HHS) enforces strict, non-negotiable sanitation regulations on child care centers and preschools. Daycares experience high annual turnover in nursery aides and janitorial staff. When new workers are hired, center directors struggle to train them in non-toxic dwell times and diaper-changing sanitization.
- **The Solution:** A recurring **$149.00/month B2B SaaS subscription** giving the child care facility unlimited staff certifications, an official laminated lobby certificate, and real-time state inspection documentation.
- **The Scale:** Enrolling just 100 Texas daycares generates **$14,900.00 in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) = $178,800.00 / year** of hands-off recurring revenue.

#### Engine 2: The 1099 Subcontractor Rapid "FastPass" ($49.00 per pass)
- **The Problem:** General contractors and prime commercial cleaning companies are terrified of uninsured, untrained independent subcontractors causing accidents or chemical damage on site.
- **The Solution:** Prime contractors mandate that every trade partner complete the **Subcontractor Rapid Clearance Pass (`PKG-1099-FASTPASS`)**. The independent cleaner pays a $49.00 pass-through fee directly through the portal to unlock their digital badge.
- **The Scale:** Certifying 150 trade partners a month generates **$7,350.00 / month = $88,200.00 / year** in pure software income.

#### Engine 3: Institutional Campus & Facility Licensing ($995.00 / month)
- **The Problem:** Large community college districts (such as Collin College or Dallas College) and corporate business parks contract out janitorial work to multiple cleaning companies. Facility directors have zero visibility into whether third-party contractor cleaners have passed background checks or OSHA safety training.
- **The Solution:** The district licenses a dedicated **Compliance Command Center** portal where college directors and campus police can monitor real-time headcount, background clearance dates, and QR credentials across all vendors.
- **The Scale:** 3 district licenses generate **$2,985.00 / month = $35,820.00 / year**.
