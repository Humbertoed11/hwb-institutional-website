# Standard Operating Procedure: **Environmental Management SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP establishes the framework for HWB Cleaning Services LLC’s Environmental Management System (EMS) in accordance with **ISO 14001:2015**.

## 2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Guidance First:** If a chemical's environmental impact is unknown, ASK the CEO before procurement.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Log all eco-audit results in the tactical DB.

## 3.0 Procedure
1.  **Green Certification:** Prioritize chemicals with Green Seal or EPA Safer Choice marks.
2.  **Dilution Control:** Mandate automated dilution to prevent toxic runoff.
3.  **Waste Sorting:** All recyclable materials must be processed through designated streams.

## 4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
* Quarterly SDS verification.
* Annual ISO 14001 impact audit.
