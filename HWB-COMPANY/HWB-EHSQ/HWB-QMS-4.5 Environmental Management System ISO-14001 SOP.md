# Standard Operating Procedure: **Environmental Management SOP**

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP establishes the framework for HWB Cleaning Services LLC’s Environmental Management System (EMS) in accordance with **ISO 14001:2015+Amd 1:2024** and **ISO 9001:2026 (ISO 9001:2015+Amd 1:2024)**.

## 2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Guidance First:** If a chemical's environmental impact is unknown, ASK the CEO before procurement.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Log all eco-audit results in the tactical DB.

## 2.1 2026 Edition & ISO Amendment 1:2024 Alignment (Clauses 4.1 & 4.2)
In accordance with the **2024 ISO Climate Action Amendment (+Amd 1:2024)** and the **2026 QMS Edition**, HWB Cleaning Services LLC explicitly evaluates and governs:
1. **Clauses 4.1 & 4.2 (Climate Action & Sustainability Context):** HWB evaluates whether climate change and environmental sustainability are relevant issues to its field operations and interested parties (municipalities, LEED/Green-certified corporate campuses, and institutional clients). Low-water auto-scrubbers, microfiber capture systems, and EPA Safer Choice chemical selection mitigate ecological impact.
2. **Quality & Environmental Culture:** Field supervisors reinforce a zero-waste culture through daily pre-shift checks and strict chemical dilution discipline.
3. **AI & Digital Governance (ISO/IEC 42001:2023):** Automated chemical inventory tracking, digital SDS verification, and AI-assisted scope estimating operate under deterministic human-in-the-loop governance (`HWB-QMS-7.7`).

## 3.0 Procedure
1.  **Green Certification:** Prioritize chemicals with Green Seal or EPA Safer Choice marks.
2.  **Dilution Control:** Mandate automated dilution to prevent toxic runoff.
3.  **Waste Sorting:** All recyclable materials must be processed through designated streams.

## 4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
* Quarterly SDS verification.
* Annual ISO 14001:2015+Amd 1:2024 impact audit.
