# Standard Operating Procedure: **Occupational Health & Safety (OH&S)**

## 1.0 Purpose
This SOP formalizes HWB’s commitment to a safe workplace, aligning with **ISO 45001:2018+Amd 1:2024** and **ISO 9001:2026 (ISO 9001:2015+Amd 1:2024)**.

## 2.0 Universal Mandates (2026 Baseline)
1. **Physical Truth:** Use absolute paths for all Site Survey artifacts.
2. **Tier 6 Telemetry:** Every safety incident must be logged instantly.

## 2.1 2026 Edition & ISO Amendment 1:2024 Alignment (Clauses 4.1 & 4.2)
In compliance with the **2024 ISO Amendment 1 (+Amd 1:2024)** and the **2026 QMS Edition**, HWB Cleaning Services LLC enforces:
1. **Clauses 4.1 & 4.2 (Climate Action & Thermal/Environmental Safety Context):** HWB evaluates climate-driven occupational hazards (extreme Texas heat stress during post-construction cleanups, indoor air quality, and ventilation requirements) and incorporates interested party safety requirements into every Job Hazard Analysis (JHA).
2. **Quality & Safety Culture:** Zero-injury performance (0.00 TRIR) is embedded as a shared ethical and operational value across all field crews and supervisors.
3. **AI & Digital Governance (ISO/IEC 42001:2023):** Automated safety incident logging, digital JHA verification, and Academy safety training tracking operate under strict human-in-the-loop oversight (`HWB-QMS-7.7`).

## 3.0 Procedure
1.  **Site Surveys:** Perform mandatory JHA (Job Hazard Analysis) before starting any new contract.
2.  **PPE Standard:** Zero-tolerance for working without approved safety gear.
3.  **Hazard Tracking:** Identify and mitigate risks like "Slip/Trip/Fall" and chemical exposure.

## 4.0 Verification (Zero-Defect Check)
* Monthly safety walk-throughs recorded in the Activity Log.
* 100% completion rate for mandatory safety orientation.
