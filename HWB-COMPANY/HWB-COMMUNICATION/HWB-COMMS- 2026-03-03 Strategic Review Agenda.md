# Strategic Review Agenda

![HWB Cleaning Services Logo](../HWB-IT/HWB-IT-WEBSITE/static/HWB-WEB%20HWB%20New%20Logo.png)
*Figure 1: Official HWB Cleaning Services LLC Corporate Logo.*

## 1.0 Purpose
To establish a disciplined communication and review cadence between the human Director and the AI Strategic Team (George & Gemini). These sessions ensure that all operational activities remain aligned with the ISO 9001 Quality Management System and the SigmaFidelity™ business objectives.

## 2.0 Scope
Applies to all daily stand-up reports and weekly strategic audits conducted within the Obsidian workspace and the Gemini CLI environment.

---

## 3.0 Daily Stand-Up Agenda (Execution Focus)
**Cadence:** Every morning upon system startup.
1.  **System Health Check:** Review Webserver Uptime and Production Diagnostics.
2.  **Lead Status:** Report on newly harvested assets and CRM opportunity movement.
3.  **Content Verification:** Review the day's scheduled LinkedIn posts or website updates.
4.  **Immediate Constraints:** Identify any "P0" bugs or blocks requiring Director intervention.
5.  **Today's Milestone:** Define the single most important output for the 24-hour cycle.

---

## 4.0 Weekly Strategic Audit Agenda (Alignment Focus)
**Cadence:** Every Friday or Monday.
1.  **KPI Analysis:** Review Cpk, RTY, and DPMO trends from the Sigma Orchestrator.
2.  **SEO Performance:** Audit keyword rankings and indexing status via GSC.
3.  **GBP Engagement:** Review customer feedback, reviews, and post-engagement metrics.
4.  **Document Control:** Verify that all new SOPs and records are properly versioned and filed.
5.  **"Zero-Cost Illusion" Filter:** Evaluate if the week's activities successfully addressed strategic waste.
6.  **Next Week's Roadmap:** Adjust the project priorities based on empirical performance data.

---

## 5.0 Verification & Records
*   **Naming Convention:** Any document saved in the `HWB-COMMUNICATION` folder must follow the format: `HWB-COMMS- [YYYY-MM-DD] [FILE NAME].md` (e.g., `HWB-COMMS- 2026-03-03 Daily Stand-Up.md`).
