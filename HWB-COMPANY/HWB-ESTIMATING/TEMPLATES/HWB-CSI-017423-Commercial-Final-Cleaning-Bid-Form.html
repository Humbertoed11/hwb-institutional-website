✓ Scope Inclusions (By HWB Cleaning Services)

  • Removal of all stickers, protective plastic films, tape residue, and silicone smears from glazing, frames, and millwork.
  • Interior and exterior storefront glass up to 25 ft height (ground / ladder / rolling scaffold reach).
  • Vacuuming and wipe-down of all window tracks, sills, and aluminum mullions.
  • Rotary brush scrub and slurry extraction on ceramic, porcelain, and quarry tile grout lines.
  • Acid wash and descaling of all new plumbing porcelain and chrome fixtures.
  • Detailing interior and exterior of all breakroom casework, vanity counters, and shelving.
  • All cleaning chemicals, industrial equipment, HEPA vacuums, rotary scrubbers, and PPE provided by HWB.
  • Trash hauled directly to on-site general contractor dumpster.

✕ Scope Exclusions (By General Contractor / Others)

  • On-site dumpster / disposal container (to be provided by GC within 100 ft of building perimeter).
  • Continuous operational electrical power and pressurized water on site during cleaning operations.
  • Exterior window cleaning exceeding 2 stories requiring swing-stage or boom lifts (available via add-on quote).
  • Hazardous material abatement (asbestos, lead, biohazard, or mold remediation).
  • Pressure washing of exterior site concrete / parking lots (available as separate mobilization).
  • Repair of pre-existing scratch damage, etching, or gouges caused by prior construction trades.
  • Re-cleaning caused by subsequent trade traffic after HWB punch-list sign-off (billed at $45.00/man-hour T&M).
